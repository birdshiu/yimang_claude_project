**Outer Warehouse** is the first area you reach in the Warehouse Zone. It is dormant when first visiting. Powering on the facility activates numerous hazards but allows Yi to proceed into the area.

## Subrooms

- Enemy gauntlet room. The entire ceiling is electrified and slowly descends as the fight goes on, eventually limiting vertical mobility, but stops before reaching the floor, even if enemies aren't defeated in time. Enemies that take vertical space or teleport into the arena in the air get killed by the ceiling, if their appearance is stalled by not defeating initial enemies.
