**Boundless Repository** is part of the Warehouse Zone. Upon entering, Yi encounters Yanlao and then is prompted to fight Xingtian.

### Repository Cells

After defeating Xingtian, the only way to progress is to enter the attached repository container in the bottom left. After reaching and entering Controllable Robot, Yanlao uses his sniper (howitzer) weapon to destroy it, but missing Yi. Afterward, a chase through a series of four repository cells starts. Once entered, backtracking is not possible, but the sequence can be repeated if necessary.

Each section has one colored frog pillar statue that can be hacked in-between the sniper shots. They act as keys to opening the Hexachrem Vault near the Root Node.

### Hexachrem Vault

Besides the four hexachrem stations found in each of the four repository cells that go in order of green, blue, yellow, and orange, – the fifth, red statue is inside a separate subroom across from the exit after the last repository cell, in the bottom right of the Boundless Repository. The sniper attacks happen more frequently in the room, making it impossible to activate it until the person controlling that weapon is defeated.
