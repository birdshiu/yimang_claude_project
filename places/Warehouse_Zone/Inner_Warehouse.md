**Inner Warehouse** is the second area in the Warehouse Zone. The elevator in the center of the area needs to be powered on to progress. Before being powered it will read: "*Warning The elevator system is currently low on power. Please turn on the power supply on both sides before boarding.*" The terminals that turn power on are connected to the lift by a series of large cables visible in the floor.

## Subrooms

- On the left side of the map is a puzzle subroom where crates can move in only one direction that can be changed by the Mystic Nymph. It is needed to jump through the lasers and travel on crates multiple times to access hack points required to both open the door to the poison jar and be able to get to it. The reward is Molted Tianma Hide.

## Connections

- Outer Warehouse - transport crate
- Boundless Repository - after activating two power supplies displayed by the activation of the large frog statues and using the elevator

In the spot with a button that releases a single crate to ride on, Yi can use his Mystic Nymph to hack and release a rope from the platform above. This must be done quickly as he will be vulnerable to the electrified presses while controlling the Nymph. This gives access to the upper part of Inner Warehouse with one of the Council Statues in the area and a Tao Fruit.
