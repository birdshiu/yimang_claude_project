The **Sky Tower** is an entrance to the Empyrean District. It is a platforming section involving numerous hazards and platforms that phase in and out. No enemies appear in the platforming area.

- Empyrean District (Passages)

  - Lake Yaochi Ruins
  - Tiandao Research Center
