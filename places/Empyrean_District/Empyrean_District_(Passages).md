The lower section of the Empyrean District. The quickest route to get here is through Sky Tower. It is possible to access it first from right side, given the player has explored the Grotto of Scriptures.

The miniboss, Tianshou, is found drenched in pink water that needs to be turned off to begin the fight. To make the fight easier, there is a hack point found by exploring the right half of the map (just on the other side of Tianshou's room). It will open the door to a Robot Minion that Yi can pilot. This also grants the Robo Fight! achievement.
