## Connections

- Empyrean District (Passages)
- Empyrean District (Sanctum)

## Subrooms

To access the room behind the large door, the bells must be hit with Charged Strike in the correct order.

There is a painting with 5 suns over the mountains in the room with a Spiked Mutant on the top-right from the large door. The power for the building must be turned on for the painting to appear. The suns in similar positions as the bells hint on its connection to the puzzle.
