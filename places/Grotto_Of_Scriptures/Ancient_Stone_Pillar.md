**Ancient Stone Pillar** is located inside the Grotto of Scriptures and where Ji's Vital Sanctum is set.

The main vast hall has countless black stone stelae with records of the solarian history for countless years. There is a beatiful glass mural in the ceiling of the cave with light from above illuminating the room. It has the image of four important figures, two of whom look like Lear and Jietong and the two others are unknown, same as in the upper level of Grotto of Scriptures (Entry), in front of which Ji first reveals his identity to Yi.

In the far back room of this grotto, Ji stores many different relics of the past likely with sentimental value, including an old prototype Shanhai 1000 model and the Golden Record beside it. Golden Record is a backer content that plays their brief audio messages.
