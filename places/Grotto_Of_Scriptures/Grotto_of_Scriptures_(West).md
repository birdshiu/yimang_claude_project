Western part of the Grotto of Scriptures. The elevator room is cut off from most of the area until the shortcuts are opened from the other side, which are accessed from the upper entrance. Upon entering there Yi will bow at a statue of Lear and a cutscene will play.

A prominent feature of this location is the three rotating platforms. By default (and after Yi dies) they are horizontal but they can rotate into climbable walls; both angles open and close off passages. It's advisable to open the door shortcut (below to the right) first then climb up to the hidden passage up top. That will eventually lead to the control terminal that rotates the platforms horizontally again.

## Subrooms

- **Secret Tomb of Luyan**
  - With the platforms set vertically, go through the hidden passage across from Lear's Tomb, then climb up the platform.
  - Inside, complete a test using Parry and Unbounded Counter. (Tip for UC timing: the statues fire when the circle converges on them.)
  - Beyond that is Secret Mural III.
- **Secret Tomb of Lear**
  - Located at the lower level, three lights will activate as the other secret tombs are opened.
  - Once all three of the other secret tombs have been opened the miniboss Cixing will spawn outside the tomb's entrance
  - Inside is a recording of Lear's famous Inaction Declaration, and an item necessary for the Shooting Star Ending.
