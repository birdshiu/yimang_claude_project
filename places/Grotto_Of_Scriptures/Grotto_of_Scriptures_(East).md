Eastern section of the Grotto of Scriptures. At the beginning a security system detects Yi as an intruder, activating traps such as spiked boulders. This area contains two of the three tombs that must be accessed to open Lear's Secret Tomb.

Near the center of this area is a huge boulder suspended above the floor. Once released it will roll into Grotto of Scriptures (West) then fall into Grotto of Scriptures (Entry) where it breaks the glass dome to reveal a chest. In addition, there is a hidden passage behind where the boulder was suspended that leads to the **Secret Tomb of Yin Jifu**.

## Connections

- Grotto of Scriptures (Entry)
- Grotto of Scriptures (West)

## Subrooms

- **Secret Tomb of Yin Jifu**
  - Take the hidden passage behind the huge boulder.
  - In the big chamber, let loose a spiked ball then jump down and strike the Rhizomatic Energy Sphere to maintain the platforms.
  - Complete a short combat sequence.
  - Inside, complete a test of parrying ability.
  - Once passed, collect Secret Mural I.
- **Secret Tomb of Guiguzi**
  - Manually drop a spiked ball and by staying ahead of it, use Mystic Nymph to open hatches in the floor to alter its path. The ball easily kills enemies so combat may not be necessary.
  - Inside, complete a test utilizing Parry, Unbounded Counter, and Tai-Chi Kick.
  - Once passed, collect Secret Mural II
