**Grotto of Scriptures** is a bronze-colored zone that is administrated by Sol Ji.

Grotto of Scriptures Chip is the map data chip unlocking details on this zone. It can be obtained from the Shanhai 1000 in the Ancient Stone Pillar after defeating Ji.
