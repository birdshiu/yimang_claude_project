**New Kunlun Control Hub** at the **Root Pinnacle** is the final destination of the game. This is where the Tiandao Council held conference and controlled the Council Code system to revise rules and regulations.

Logs of Council Proceedings can be accessed to show the ten Sols discussing their solution to the Tianhuo Virus, as well as a conversation between Yi and Eigong before their initial fight.
