**New Kunlun Hub** is a dusty-yellow zone, center of New Kunlun and area where Yi's base is located.

Upon repairing the broken Shanhai 9000 at the Four Seasons Pavilion, it will unlock details on this zone. After speaking with it, Yi will unlock options to buy or take map data chips by force from other Shanhai and give them to the one in Pavilion to unlock details on other zones.
