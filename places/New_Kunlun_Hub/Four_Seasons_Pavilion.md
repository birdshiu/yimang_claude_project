**Four Seasons Pavilion** is Yi's base of operations in the New Kunlun Hub. It acts as a safehouse for him and his allies.

When the area resets (from resting or teleporting in) some NPCs will move, at times, to a spot indicated by a yellow exclamation mark on the map, providing new dialogue between characters.

Giving Shuanshuan the Unknown Seed and both GM Fertilizers provides a means to reach all the chests on the upper levels of the pavilion.

## Connections

- New Kunlun Central Hall
