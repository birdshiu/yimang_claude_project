The **Abandoned Mines (Location)** acts as a back door to the Livestock Area. It is very dark here, but there are Rhizomatic Energy Spheres available to light the way and/or to activate rail systems temporarily. At some points a switch can be hacked to permanently light up an area.

During the fight with Yinyue, Yi has access to a Pipe Refill Station and an Azure Sand container, offering much more sustain than other boss fights.

## Connections

- Factory (Underground)
- Underground Cave - requires Access Token

## Contents

There are 3 items:

- Swift Blade Jade
- Tao Fruit x1
- Herb Catalyst x1

## Trivia

- By scouting with the Mystic Nymph at the pond, the player can see they are on the other side of the cave where the game starts.
