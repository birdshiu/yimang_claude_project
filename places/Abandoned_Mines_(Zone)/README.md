**Abandoned Mines** is a green-colored zone, presumably, where jin or other precious metal mining operations took place. Its abandoned passages said to be connected to the Livestock Area.

Abandoned Mines Chip is the map data chip unlocking details on this zone. It can be found on the broken Shanhai 9000 in the Factory (Underground).
