After entering into the strange room, Yi finds himself in Lady Ethereal's dream. Anomalous activity reveals something unnerving.

Lady Ethereal's dream sequence has heavy platforming and uses all previously learned movement skills.

Location doesn't have a map and there are no dropped items or database logs which can be missed (considering the location can't be revisited).
