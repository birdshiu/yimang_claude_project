## Appearance

Despite its name, Radiant Pagoda appears to be more pyramid in shape than of a pagoda. There are Solarian characters for 玲瓏寶塔 / Radiant Pagoda on the base of the pagoda: "M M J G". Before power is restored, Radiant Pagoda appears to be off and glows red. After power has been restored, the Radiant Pagoda glows teal.

Within the entry room of the pagoda, three screens are plastered on the wall showcasing the Control Panel, as well as a Root Node in front of it. In the main room, the triangular nature of the pagoda is clear, with a central platform in the background that Yingzhao is plugged in on, and what appears to be a ball of rhizomatic energy above it. Some parts of the floor have the ability to turn into hazards, and do so when Yingzhao is defending the pagoda.

Kuafu's room is filled to the brim of his cherished memories alongside his Vital Sanctum. These items include a motorcycle, an assistive mechanical arm, a Baigong Village Windmill from his hometown, and photos of his friends (including one with Kuafu and Yi).

To the right of Kuafu's room there is an elevator that connects to Apeman Facility (Elevator), New Kunlun Central Hall, and Central Transport Hub.

## Story

### Past Events

With the Eternal Cauldron Project, Kuafu designed Power Reservoir to gain enough energy to launch New Kunlun into the air and travel 500 light years away to the Pale Blue Planet. Radiant Pagoda is meant to act as the main control center of the Power Reservoir, ensuring that Power Reservoir is holding the energy it should. It is possible to activate a storage mode from Radiant Pagoda, putting operations at minimum level and shutting down most bridges. It is rumored that Kuafu has not left Radiant Pagoda after New Kunlun's launch.

### Game Events

Initially, the light bridge to enter the pagoda in has been disabled. This forces Yi to restore power from the outside to gain access.

Upon entering the Radiant Pagoda, and stepping into the main room, Yi is met with Yingzhao, the defender of Radiant Pagoda, who immediately engages the Fangshi.

After defeating Yingzhao, Yi is able to enter Kuafu's personal room, and confronts his former coworker. After taking Seal of Kuafu, Yi may enter the elevator to exit the area.
