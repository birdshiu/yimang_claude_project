**Power Reservoir (Central)** is a main area in the Power Reservoir where the bridge to the Radiant Pagoda can be powered on. To access the entire area, Yi must enter from both the Power Reservoir (East) and Power Reservoir (West) sections of the Power Reservoir.

The first time a transporter is taken to this location a Heng cutscene will play where Yi and Heng were traveling by the rhizomatic columns when they were young.

## Subrooms

- Control room where Yi hacks into the system to power on the bridge. Upon exiting a combat sequence is initiated.

## Connections

- Power Reservoir (East)
- Power Reservoir (West)
- Radiant Pagoda

## NPCs

- Shanhai 9000 (Power Reservoir)
