**Central Transport Hub** is a section of the Power Reservoir zone. The first time Yi rides the lift down, a combat sequence takes place while descending.

To unlock access to the elevator on this level jump down the elevator shaft from New Kunlun Central Hall, or defeat Yanren.

## Subrooms

A platforming area where Yi avoids lasers to reach a chest containing Dark Steel.
