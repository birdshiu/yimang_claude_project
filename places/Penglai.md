Penglai is a habitable planet located about 500 light-years from Pale Blue Planet, and is the homeworld of the solarians. It is said to be just as large as Pale Blue planet, although it doesn't have as much water as Penglai: approximately 90% of its surface is covered by oceans, leaving a number of small continents. These continents are home to nine kingdoms: Guifang, Shang, Yumin, Jie, Yih, Yan, Xia, Chan and Ying kingdom. Besides these continents, there is also a Kunlun island, inhabited by Kunlun tribe and where Fusang is located.

Before the events of the game, Penglai was afflicted by a pandemic known as Tianhuo, which caused a severe crisis among solarian society and prompted the Eternal Cauldron Project on the renovated by Tiandao Council Kunlun island – New Kunlun – where the events of the game take place.
