**Penglai** (traditional Chinese: 蓬萊; pinyin: *pénglái*) is the home planet of solarians. Approximately 90% of its surface is water, the remaining 10% consisting mostly of barren land. It is located about 500 light-years from Pale Blue Planet that's said to be just as large as Penglai, although having less water.

## Geography

### Nine Kingdoms

During gameplay, characters reference nine kingdoms of Penglai. They are also labeled on the map found in Jiequan's Vital Sanctum room in the Solarian Language.

#### Guifang Kingdom
Source of Red Guifang Clay and the Turtle Scorpion. Has a cold climate, red soil, and sparse population.

#### Shang Kingdom
Source of Unknown Seed. Known for its inhabitants' capability of withstanding low-altitude flight and vertical cities.

#### Yumin Kingdom
Source of Unknown Seed. Known for its inhabitants' capability of withstanding low-altitude flight and vertical cities.

#### Jie Kingdom
Jie Kingdom's capital is Biyou (碧游都). Tianshan Mountains of Jie Kingdoms separate the two kingdoms on the continent, in which precious Dark Steel is naturally found.

#### Yih Kingdom
Source of Gutwrench Fruit.

#### Yan Kingdom
Source of Wall-Climbing Gecko.

#### Xia Kingdom
Qiankun was invented here, according to Jietong's remark from The Way of Lear manga.

#### Chan Kingdom
Chan Kingdom's capital is Yushu (玉虛都). Azure Sand was invented here and where Qiankun originated from, according to the game's description.

#### Ying Kingdom
Known for its great craftsmen.

### Kunlun
Yaochi (now: Lake Yaochi Ruins) was the main living region of Kunlun people, and also Daybreak Temple's location, where Sol and Fusang were worshipped. During the Turbulent Era, it became base of the Fangshi Guild, who primarily located themselves in one of its mountains (Grotto of Scriptures). Later, during the Origin Era, the abandoned Fangshi facilities were renovated and other parts of the island were used as the base of the Tiandao Council and the island was renamed to New Kunlun.

Kunlun is an island located at the center of Penglai. Kunlun is inhabited by a Kunlun tribe and it is where Fusang is located. In one of the cave painting near coffin of Great Sage Kunlun island is shown standing on Fusang's stem. It's a tenth landmark of Penglai, but never referred to as a kingdom.

During the Turbulent Era, Lear had founded Fangshi Guild with a group of solarians at the island in attempts to make peace in Penglai and in opposition to Jie kingdom growing conquers against all of Penglai's kingdoms.

During the Origin Era, group of scientists and former nobles founded a Tiandao Council in opposition to Taoism and renovated the technology left behind by Fangshi Guild, renaming the Kunlun to New Kunlun.

Eternal Cauldron Project included the entire island of Kunlun lifted off of Penglai and converted into an interstellar spacecraft using energy from Fusang and rhizomatic pillars. During the events of the game, New Kunlun acts as a space station and orbits around the Sun at the center of a large web formed by Fusang, absorbing a huge amounts of energy to power the New Kunlun.

It is divided into a number of different Zones administrated by the Sols of the Tiandao Council.

### Fusang

Fusang, also known as "Primordial Roots", is a large plant-like organism originating on Penglai. It is the oldest known plant on the planet.

## Culture

### Language

Solarians speak and write using a fictional language inspired and produced by mixing the tones of these languages:

- Hokkien/Taiwanese
- Hakka
- Cantonese
- Middle Chinese

## History

Solarian history is divided into a number of Eras, which are delineated by significant historical events.

### Turbulent Era

The **Turbulent Era** is the earliest era mentioned in the game, and was marked by constant warfare among the various kingdoms of Penglai. It lasted for several millennia; the year 3773 is known to have been near the end of the era. The Turbulent Era saw the popularization of many advanced technologies such as Azure Sand and genetic transmutation, which were often used for military purposes. Eventually, the Jie Kingdom emerged as a major power, attempting to unite all the kingdoms by force under emperor Jietong. The Fangshi Guild, founded and led by Lear, opposed the Jie Kingdom and aimed to bring about world peace. They pioneered technologies such as the Jade System, Root Nodes, and improved farming techniques. Eventually, with military successes and the invention of the Rhizomatic Bomb, the Jie Kingdom was forced to surrender, thus establishing world peace and ending the Turbulent Era.

### Origin Era

The next era was the **Origin Era**, marked by a surge of Taoism and a distrust of most advanced technologies due to their association with war. This was in part due to Lear's Inaction Declaration, where he announced that the Fangshi Guild would be disbanded and its dangerous military technologies sealed away. The Origin Era lasted 468 years. The Tiandao Council was eventually established as a scientific organization, but operated in secret due to public mistrust of technology. A mistake in the council's immortality research resulted in the creation of Tianhuo, which grew into a catastrophic pandemic, prompting the Council to go public and begin the Eternal Cauldron Project. In the final phase of the project, New Kunlun was launched toward the Pale Blue Planet as an interstellar ark, marking the end of the Origin Era.

### Foundation Era

The **Foundation Era** was the next era and contains the main events of the game. During this time, solarians aboard New Kunlun used cryosleep to slow the effects of Tianhuo while efforts to cure the disease continued. In order to prevent brain-death during cryosleep, the Soulscape virtual dream system was used, requiring the harvest of Apemen brains to provide computing power. The game is set sometime after year 500 of the Foundation Era.
