**Apeman Facility (Monitoring)** is the first location visited after the story introduction ends and normal gameplay begins. This area is in the Apeman Facility zone.

Dying for the first time in this location before reaching Apeman Facility (Elevator) will take Yi to Limitless Realm where Warrior trains Parry and Qi Blast with him.
