**Apeman Facility (Elevator)** is the area in the Apeman Facility in which Yi first encounters a miniboss, Red Tiger Elite: Baichang. Defeating him unlocks the elevator to New Kunlun Central Hall.

## Connections

- Apeman Facility (Monitoring)
- Central Transport Hub
- Apeman Facility (Depths), requires Tai-Chi Kick

Elevator system:

- New Kunlun Central Hall
- Central Transport Hub
- Radiant Pagoda

## Contents

There are 3 items:

- Basic Component x2
- Stasis Jade (dropped by Baichang)
