**Apeman Facility (Depths)** is a section of the Apeman Facility, accessible from Apeman Facility (Elevator) after acquiring the Tai-Chi Kick skill.

## Connections

- Apeman Facility (Elevator)
- Power Reservoir (West)
- Factory (Underground) via cargo container, access is unlocked from the Factory side

## Contents

There are 8 items:

- Basic Component x1
- Standard Component x1
- Medicinal Citrine
- Tao Fruit x1
- Bearing Jade
- Herb Catalyst x1
- Soul-Severing Blade
- Fusang Amulet
