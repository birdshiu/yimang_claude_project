**Lake Yaochi Ruins** is an entry point to the Agricultural Zone. This area has three structures: The Daybreak Tower, and two small buildings with Goumang's seal on their entrances.

The stranger in Daybreak Tower will give you Ancient Sheet Music, which Abacus will be able to decipher into Ancient Penglai Ballad with corresponding notes to solve the bell puzzle.
