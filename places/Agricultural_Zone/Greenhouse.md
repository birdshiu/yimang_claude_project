**Greenhouse** is the center of the Agricultural Zone. A few yellow water walls block passage throughout the area, and can be turned off by hacking consoles located on the upper floor.

Before ringing the large bell to summon the miniboss, a shortcut may be opened to make respawning more convenient.

## Connections

- Water and Oxygen Synthesis - by falling into pit by the Goumang's statue

## NPCs

- Shanhai 9000 (Agricultural Zone)
