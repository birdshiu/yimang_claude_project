**Water & Oxygen Synthesis** is a part of the Agricultural Zone that houses plants and equipment to create a livable environment in New Kunlun. Similar to Lake Yaochi Ruins, there is a sequence where Yi rides a boat carried by a Yinglong Dragon while being attacked by enemies jumping out of the water and those lurking on platforms above.
