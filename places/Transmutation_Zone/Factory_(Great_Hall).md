**Factory (Great Hall)** is an area in the Transmutation Zone.

Open areas of the Factory Great Hall are periodically bathed in a deadly radiation that deals continuous damage. The exhausters release radiation heat waves after every **third strike** of the Core Agitators at the furnace core that are located at the center of the location. The strikes are shown by the red blinking and shaking in the open areas. Core Agitators' strike counts as a crimson attack and can hit Yi.

The radiation can be hidden from in the rooms and walled off areas or can be temporarily shielded from by striking an Anti-radiation Device placed sparingly in in the middle of the open sections.

## Connections

- Cortex Center
- Factory (Underground) - by falling down into a pit beneath the platform in an open area to the right of the Core Agitators. Can also be accessed through an elevator in the left side of the location
- Outer Warehouse - via a bridge that can only be opened from the Warehouse side
- Factory (Production Area) - up an elevator at top left side, requires destroying Heavy Crossbow Cannon Wall with the Unbounded Counter. Can also be accessed through an elevator at the top right side near the Platform Switch Puzzle door, which needs to be first unlocked from the Production Area side

## Characters

- Chiyou - before the bridge at left side of the location
