**Factory (Production Area)** is in the Transmutation Zone. It is accessible after acquiring the Unbounded Counter skill and destroying the Heavy Crossbow Cannon Wall in the top left of the Factory (Great Hall).

Similarly to Factory (Great Hall), the open areas of the Factory Production are occasionally bathed in deadly radiation after every third strike of the Core Agitators. Hiding in rooms and using Anti-Radiation Device shields Yi from taking the continuous damage. The radiation waves can be stopped permanently by Unbounded Countering the Core Agitators that are located in top floor of the location.

Before battling Wuqiang, the four Bolt Commanders on the platforms above can be dispatched stealthily, without drawing aggression of the miniboss, by using the upper entrance and by platforming to the other side of the room, making the fight be one-on-one.

## Subrooms

Pharmacy Storage subroom is sealed by two Council Statues. A hammer one is on the top platform at the left large area which is covered by two Eclipse Sniper Corporal. The other, shield statue, is in the upper left room with timed platforming to activate fire exhausts and conveyor belts and destroy all 8 Geno-guard Embryos roaming around, that prevent from hacking the statue.

Inside the room are many cabinets with transmutation medicine, some of which are broken in. Gene Eradicator can be retrieved from one of them.
