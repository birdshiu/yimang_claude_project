**Prison** is one of Transmutation Zone locations, administered by Sol Jiequan.

During the prison break sequence, until Yi rests at the Root Node, he remains in a weakened state that's commonly called Prison State. It decreases the maximum health and limits most of his skills and actions, forcing him to proceed with caution and in stealth.

Yi can use large pots around the area to hide from the guards and avoid detection. If any guard spots Yi, then they sound the alarm and alert the prison's Warden, a formiddable enemy during the weakened state, who appears to prevent him from escaping. After getting spotted, if any of the guards or Warden is nearby, then hiding in large pots won't drop their aggression, and they can still hit him inside it, throwing him out. It is necessary to create distance from the Warden and other guards, then quickly hiding in a pot before the Warden teleports nearby to have a chance to drop the aggression (Warden sometimes still finds Yi after teleporting nearby, likely if not hiding quickly enough).
