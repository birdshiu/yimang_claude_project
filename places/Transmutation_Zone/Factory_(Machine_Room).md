**Factory (Machine Room)** is a lower part of the Transmutation Zone. It can be entered early from Factory (Underground), but a door near the elevator locks access to it. It can be passed through only after escaping Prison.

After following the only available path and learning Unbounded Counter, the location can be fully explored and cleared by countering bolts of the Heavy Crossbow Cannon Walls and destroying them, opening the other paths.

After going up the elevator to Factory (Undeground), a cutscene starts and the story progresses.

## Connections

- Prison - by elevator
- Factory (Underground) - by elevator
