**Shengwu Hall** is the final area in the Transmutation Zone.

There is an interactable injection port before the transmutation crucible, within which Jiequan resides. It can be interacted and if the Gene Eradicator is obtained, then it can be inserted, but only before activating the transmutation crucible. It poisons Jiequan and makes his first phase easier, as well as unlocks the Fight Dirty achievement.
