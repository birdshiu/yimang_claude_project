**Transmutation Zone** is a red-colored zone that is administrated by Sol Jiequan.

Transmutation Zone Chip is the map data chip unlocking details on this zone. It can be obtained from the Shanhai 9000 in the Factory (Production Area) that gets hostile upon detecting the intruder and shoots a crimson laser at Yi, requiring the use of Unbounded Counter to shut it off and allow the chip to be picked up.
