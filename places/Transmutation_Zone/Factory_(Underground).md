**Factory (Underground)** is a location of the Abandoned Mines (Zone) and in-between Transmutation Zone. It does not appear to be administered by any of the Sols.

The contents of the location are displayed after giving Shanhai 9000 the Abandoned Mines Chip, which is located on the upper path toward Abandoned Mines (Location) after passing Kuiyan.

## Connections

- Factory (Great Hall)
- Outer Warehouse - accessible after acquiring Air Dash
- Apeman Facility (Depths) - using transport crate
- Abandoned Mines (Location)

## Contents

- Anomalous Root Node - Air Dash

There is 1 database entry:

- 20 - Evacuation Notice for Miners

There are 6 items:

- Swift Descent Jade - the chest is located beneath the pit from [Factory (Great Hall)](Factory_(Great_Hall) "wikilink")
- Basic Component x1
- Standard Component x2
- Advanced Component x1
- Abandoned Mines Chip
