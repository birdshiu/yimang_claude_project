**Galactic Dock** is a space dock now containing one ship for interplanetary flights. Presumably, this vessel was used to capture the first apemen for the Livestock Area. In the left part of the location there is a cell to confine them, which is closed until the completion of the "Save the village" apemen sidequest.

## Connections

- Central Transport Hub
- Livestock Harvesting Platform, accessible after "Save the village"

## Details

Despite appearing on the map, the door is not interactable.
