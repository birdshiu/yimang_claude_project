**Livestock Harvesting Platform** is a location in the Livestock Area where the harvesting cutscene happens during the introduction. This is when the veil is lifted and where the player learns truth about Peach Blossom Village.

It is later visited in a quest to save the people of the village with a Red Tiger Elite patrolling the right edge of the area, most likely being in the charge of the attack.

## Connections

- 95th Livestock Pen
- Apeman Facility (Monitoring) - only during the intro
- Galactic Dock - after defeating Lieguan

## Contents

There is 1 item:

- Dark Steel x1 (dropped by Lieguan)
