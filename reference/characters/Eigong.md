## Boss

**Eigong** is the Sol boss of Research Institute. She holds the Seal of Knowledge.

### Combat Appearance

#### Regular

Eigong is a tall light gray solarian with a red orb ornament on her forehead that connects to her "hair" which is composed of Fusang roots and tied up in a braided bun.
Her innermost clothing consists of a tan bodysuit martial arts uniform. Around it, she wears a samurai-esque coat of muted dark green color with gold lining that leads into double pauldrons on her shoulders. The coat is held by a dull green belt pointed up like a chevron. On her wrists are guards that match her belt with gold edges.
She wields a straight red glowing broadsword with black edges and no guard, similar in appearance to a lightsaber. The hilt matches the color scheme of her wrist guard with a tan tassel on the end.

#### Mutation

Same general appearance and clothing, but the ends of her Fusang bun have the pink appearance of the Tianhuo mutants. This fleshy substance expands across her body while the third phase is loading.

### Combat

Eigong can only be stunned by Cloud Piercer X arrow, lower levels do not stun her.

#### Arena

Flat area with no walls and loops while traversing. An invisible barrier at the edges of camera prevents Yi or Eigong from traveling too far from each other.

#### Phase 1

Next attacks can be used:

- **Slow starter**: Runs toward Yi with a wide upward sword slash, followed by a step forward with a wide downward sword slash. The next attack is often the crossup combo or the triple poke.
- **Crossup combo**: Steps forward with a downward sword slash, followed by stepping forward past Yi with a forward sword slash. She will turn around to continue attacking, hence the name "crossup combo". Often repeats once, or is followed by the triple poke.
- **Triple poke**: Stabs 3 times with sword. Sometimes the third stab is omitted. Often repeats up to 2 more times.
- **Talisman grab**: Charges up a talisman and performs a talisman dash. If caught, Yi will be immobilized until the talisman detonates. The talisman dash can be Tai-Chi Kicked or Skull Kicked, but cannot be unbounded countered. Very likely to repeat multiple times, or lead to the crimson ball as the next attack.
- **Delayed slash**: Dashes forward past Yi. Three quick sword slashes will then appear in the space that she just dashed through, while she remains crouched and sheaths her sword. She stands up and draws her sword again before continuing the fight. If Yi is on the ground, he must be standing with his back toward her in order to successfully parry the slashes.
- **Crimson downslash**: Dashes upward into the air, charging up a crimson attack, and slams vertically down to the ground leaving a fire floor. Followed by the delayed slash.
- **Crimson ball:** Charges up a crimson ball, jumps up, and releases it as a large circular crimson explosion upon landing. Leaves a fire floor. (If Yi is so far away that it nearly misses, then the fire floor will not be cancelled even with an Unbounded Counter.) The initial moments of the charging process look similar to the talisman charging, but differences appear early enough for the player to recognize each one and react accordingly.
- **Dash/Teleport**: Not an attack but a simple dash maneuver during which she is briefly intangible. May be directed away to gain more distance from Yi, or toward and past Yi to get behind him.
- **Shuffle**: Not an attack but an evasive maneuver where she dashes slightly backward and then slightly forward again. Often followed by talisman grab.

#### Phase 2

Retains all attacks from Phase 1. Next attacks are added and changed:

- **Wind blade combo**: Always starts phase 2 with this attack. Performs a distinctive charging animation before swinging her sword, releasing a large, slash-shaped, horizontally travelling projectile with each sword slash. The first is crimson, and is followed by 2 non crimson ones. They count as melee attacks, meaning that using Unbounded Counter against them will deal internal damage to her. Alternates between two follow-up options:
  1.  Another **single crimson wind blade** (this option is always used in the phase opening combo)
  2.  Delayed slash
- **Talisman grab** is now accompanied by a temporary patch of fire that follows Eigong. Depending on the next actions, she follows up with a certain combo:

- - If the Talisman grab is Tai-Chi Kicked, after stagger, Eigong follow-ups with one of the two **Upper combos** or a **Crimson downslash** at equal chances (can do them multiple times in a row):
    1.  **Spike combo**: Upward slash during which Eigong jumps up, that knockbacks Yi into the air. Followed by a second aerial slash that knocks Yi back to the ground and finished with **Finishing Crimson downslash** (this Crimson downslash aims diagonally). *The upward slash and finishing crimson downslash are both preceded by a clicking sound effect.*
    2.  **Geyser combo**: Upward slash during which Eigong jumps up, that knockbacks Yi into the air. Eigong then performs a **Crimson geyser smash** downward that is followed by a narrow **Crimson geyser burst** (they deal separate damage). After landing, she charges up and executes **Crimson ball** (unless the geyser attacks are countered, interrupting and staggering the boss). *No attack are preceded by a clicking sound in this combo, unlike the Spike combo.*
    3.  **Crimson Downslash**: Executes swiftly after stagger instead, with little to no room to Counter it. Can be followed with Delayed Slash, Crimson Orb, Talisman or be the last attack in the combo.
  - If the Talisman grab is NOT Tai-Chi Kicked, then she always follows up with **Spike combo**.
- **Retaliation**: If she is staggered and falls on the ground, she has a chance to quickly get up and perform the delayed slash.
- **Crimson downslash** is no longer always followed by **Delayed slash**.
- Eigong may teleport above Yi and do a **Crimson downslash**, if he is far from her, near the edges of the screen, while she is in the walking state before any attacks.
- **Delayed slash** is followed by **Talisman grab** if Yi doesn't talisman first. *(Needs verification, she may always do it regardless)*
- **Shuffle** now leaves fire floor trail behind. She often performs the shuffle to avoid Yi's talisman dash.
- After crimson ball, if it isn't countered, has a chance to instantly teleport behind Yi and perform a talisman grab, crimson ball (won't repeat), or slow starter.

#### Phase 3

*Note: During phase 3 theme changes to The Path for Us All, which is Yi's theme.* Only happens in True Ending.

Eigong can't be hit during the Judgement cut combos.

Retains the same attacks as Phase 1 and 2, except **Crimson downslash**. New additions:

- **Judgement cut**: Disappears and does 2 steeply diagonal screen wide slashes which both have upward knockback. Followed by dashing across the entire screen, briefly pausing in the middle, and leaving behind a third screen-wide slash which can be either of the following variants:
  1.  **Crimson third slash:** Shallowly inclined screen wide crimson slash, that is repeated a second time after a brief delay, even though only one visual is seen (this variant is always used in the phase opening combo).
  2.  **White third slash**: Horizontal screen wide white slash which has upward knockback.
- **Wind blade combo** now has three follow-up options (cycled in this order):
  1.  Judgement cut with crimson third slash.
  2.  Delayed slash.
  3.  Single crimson wind blade.
- **Crossup combo** now reaches further.
- **Retaliation** with Delayed slash is no longer used. Instead after using Unbounded Counter against her Crimson ball or Spike combo, she may disappear and do a Judgement cut with Crimson third slash.
