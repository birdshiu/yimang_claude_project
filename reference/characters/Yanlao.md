## Boss

**Yanlao** is the Sol boss of the Warehouse Zone. He holds the Sol Seal of Legacy. He connects to and operates the **Sky Rending Claw** during his bossfight.

### Combat Appearance

#### Yanlao

Yanlao eyes bleed when connecting his brainwave to Sky Rending Claw. In battle his eyes turn yellow.

#### Sky Rending Claw

The Sky Rending Claw is a large, metal (jade colored), three pronged claw that is powered by rhizomatic energy and can be linked with user’s brainwaves. A red band glows on the top along with an engraved, cylindric core in its middle. Three smaller prongs are directly below the core.

### Combat

Sky Rending Claw can't be staggered from attacks such as Cloud Piercer Arrows or Enhanced Full Control, unlike most other enemies.

#### Arena

A simple room with laser traps emerging from top and climbable walls moving in from sides of the arena during the fight. It's displayed as being overseen by Yanlao from his soulscape room behind the right wall.

#### Phase 1

- Crimson downwards grab. If Yi is grabbed, it will shoot him with laser and throw him to the ground and continue flailing with a laser at small radius. It can hit Yi again at place of the fall again
- Jumps to sides of the arena and swings towards Yi, sometimes jumping to other side before swinging again. Can hit by slamming down on Yi
- At the start sequence of both of those attacks, 2 vertical lasers appear at the sides of the arena that last for seconds or number of attacks before turning off with attacks continuing.
- Floats to the center of the arena and turns on 4 vertical lasers across the arena, which move from side to side.
- Floats to the top center of the arena and begins charging big crimson laser attack.
  - Two green climbable walls move out to quarter of arena from both sides of the room.
  - Takes aim with 3 volleys of 4 sniper shots before full charge.
  - If the Claw is hit by a sniper shot, it will get stunned and fall to the ground. Sniper Shot deals 10 damage to Claw, unlike 60 damage that are dealt to Yi.
  - If the Charge is done, the Claw will release a flailing crimson laser attack across the arena *3-4 times?*. Can be completely avoided by staying in the left edge corner of the arena.

#### Phase 2

- Attacks stay the same, with few exceptions:
  - At the start sequence of Claw swings and Crimson grab, additionally to 2 lasers at the sides a new laser appears in the center of the arena and moves from one side to the other. Lasers disappear when middle laser goes from one side to the other.
  - Claw break with 4 lasers attack gains 2 extra lasers and they are grouped more tightly

## Trivia

### Gameplay

- Sky Rending Claw's Crimson grab attack can't be Unbounded Countered as well as the laser traps, but laser shot from the Claw can be.

### Lore

- Yanlao's name (奄老) consists of:
  - Yan (奄) can be interpreted as reference to proverb *奄奄一息* (yan yan yi xi) at one's last gasp, on one's deathbed, on the point of dying, on the verge of death, within an inch of life, at the gate of death, moribund. It also coincides with Yanlao's home kingdom - Yan.
  - Lao (老) can be interpreted as aged, experienced, elder. It is used as prefix before the surname of an elder person, a numeral indicating the order of birth of the children in a family or to indicate affection or familiarity.
- Yanlao's appearance reminiscent of an Exotic Shorthair cat.
- The hat Yan Lao wears is inspired by the Zi Bu Guan, traditionally donned during rituals or coming-of-age ceremonies in ancient times. Incorporating this element into the game highlights Yan Lao's refined, elder persona.
- In Taoist culture, a fuchen is used in rituals to symbolize purity and the dispelling of worries. While Yan Lao wields it to project a sense of calm and wisdom, the irony lies in how his true thoughts and actions contradict this serene facade.
- The "sniper" that Yanlao uses in the chase in Boundless Repository is actually called howitzer in solarian (h) on the control screen during the scene where Yanlao initiates it (it's also called howitzer many times internally).
- The "Sky Rending Claw" and its remote control base—collectively called the "Sky Rending System"—trace their origins to an ancient Penglai Turbulent Era engineering apparatus. Initially employed for large-scale gold mining, they were later repurposed for sappers conducting remote operations during wartime. By linking directly to the operator's neural network, they enable trench excavation, fortification construction, and even direct combat—showcasing remarkable versatility. Yan Lao employs these ancient weapons from his warehouse to avoid direct confrontation with Yi, reflecting his timid nature.
- When designing the Sky Rending Claw, one source of inspiration came from Taiwan's ubiquitous claw machines, lending a distinctive flair to its overall style.
- **Chung Tai Sun (孫中台)** is Taiwanese voice actor for Yanlao. Other famous characters voiced by him include:
  - Tachibana Jin (Atashin'chi)
  - Hantengu (Demon Slayer / Kimetsu No Yaiba)
  - Sanji (ONE PIECE)
  - Professor Oak and Narration (Pokémon TV anime series)
