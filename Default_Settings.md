# Nine Sols(九日)
Nine Sols(九日) is a lore rich, hand-drawn 2D action platformer set in a Taopun(道龐克) world that features Sekiro(隻狼)-inspired deflection focused combat.
Explore the land once home to an ancient alien race, and follow a vengeful hero’s quest to slay the 9 Sols(太陽), formidable rulers of this forsaken realm.

Steel your resolve to overcome the many foes obstructing your path throughout New Kunlun(新崑崙). Patience and understanding of the tools available is essential to success against insurmountable odds.
Traverse the interconnected regions of New Kunlun(新崑崙), in search of challenge, treasure, and Yi(羿)'s mysterious past.
Meet the characters populating the world for upgrades, abilities, and a deeper understanding of Yi(羿) himself.

# Solarians(太陽人)
Solarians(太陽人) are a species of bipedal aliens with feline-like features and are the main non-human species in Nine Sols(九日). Many major characters, including Yi(羿) and the Sols(太陽), are solarians(太陽人).

They originate from the planet Penglai(蓬萊) and have an extremely advanced level of technology based on rhizomatic energy(元能) (also known as Qi(氣)) and widely practice gene modification, also known as Transmutation.

Some solarians(太陽人) enhance their bodies with implants or gene modification, such as people of Yumin(羽民) Kingdom, who are able to handle low-altitude flight, with devices that allow it. Some solarians(太陽人) share a feature of red accents in the bottom part of their eyes (Liwen, Lear and Heng(恆)) and naturally resonate with Primordial Roots, better understanding the world spiritually. Some of the ancient solarians(太陽人) from Kunlun(崑崙) tribe are rumored to have supernatural abilities, such as Ji(姬)'s divination, biological immortality and age body shifting.

# Penglai(蓬萊)
Penglai(蓬萊) is a habitable planet located about 500 light-years from Pale Blue Planet(蒼藍星), and is the homeworld of the solarians(太陽人). It is said to be just as large as Pale Blue planet(蒼藍星), although it doesn't have as much water as Penglai(蓬萊): approximately 90% of its surface is covered by oceans, leaving a number of small continents. These continents are home to nine kingdoms: Guifang(鬼方), Shang(商), Yumin(羽民), Jie(截), Yih(夷), Yan(奄), Xia(夏), Chan(闡) and Ying(瀛) kingdom. Besides these continents, there is also a Kunlun(崑崙) island, inhabited by Kunlun(崑崙) tribe and where Fusang(扶桑) is located.

Before the events of the game, Penglai(蓬萊) was afflicted by a pandemic known as Tianhuo(天禍), which caused a severe crisis among solarian society and prompted the Eternal Cauldron Project(永生爐計畫) on the renovated by Tiandao Council(天道議會) Kunlun(崑崙) island – New Kunlun(新崑崙) – where the events of the game take place.

# Tianhuo(天禍)
The Tianhuo Virus (天禍病毒), also known as simply Tianhuo(天禍), is a solarian virus that appeared during the Origin Era and developed into a global pandemic, leading to the near extinction of their species
## Characteristics
Tianhuo(天禍) Virus to be universally fatal. The virus is implied to have mutated over time since its first discovery.

The virus' progression increases from intense stress on the body and accelerates organ failure.

Scientists in Jie(截) Kingdom are stated to have tried placing hosts of the virus in a cryo-sleep to prevent their death, however it's implied solarians(太陽人) still die inside, likely due to brain death. It is not until Lady Ethereal's team invention of Vital Sanctum (cryo-sleep device) and Soulscape System(魂境系統) (virtual world) that maintain brain activity that solarians(太陽人) have found a way to slow down progression of the virus in the body.

Upon an infected solarian's death, distinctive fungal flowers will sprout from their corpse: large purple flower and white grass-like flowers, allowing the virus to propagate itself. It is unclear why this happens, but during the death solarian's life experiences get converted into a Tao Fruit. This isn't stated to be caused by Tianhuo(天禍), and the corpses of Three Great Sages, who died at the start of the Origin Era(太初紀) and were sealed inside Secret Tomb of Lear(李耳), discovered almost a millennium after by Yi(羿), also bear these fruits contradicting a relation to the virus.

There is no known cure to Tianhuo(天禍).

The virus does not seem to affect apemen(猿人).
## Trivia
Tianhuo in Chinese, 天禍, comes from the four-word idiom 天災人禍, which means disasters, specifically in two forms: 天災 are natural disasters, and 人禍 are man-made ones. Tianhuo implies the virus is an unforeseeable, heavenly disaster by pairing the first and last characters. In reality, Eigong(易公) made the virus and shifted the blame for the solarian-made disaster from herself to the heavens and nature, with its name hinting at the irony by replacing the character for person/human (人) with heavens/sky (天) in 人禍.

# Eternal Cauldron Project(永生爐計畫)
The Eternal Cauldron Project(永生爐計畫) is a project that was initially devised by Yi(羿) during the Tianhuo(天禍) pandemic crisis. The project was hatched as a desperate effort to buy more time to develop a cure for the virus before all solarians(太陽人) perished to its effects. The foundation of this project was two major developments: the Soulscape system(魂境系統) and the interstellar ark New Kunlun(新崑崙).

# Pale Blue Planet(蒼藍星)
he Pale Blue Planet(蒼藍星) is a temporary solarian name for the planet, the home of intelligent humanoid life named apemen(猿人)(猿人). Both Pale Blue(蒼藍星) planet and apemen(猿人)(猿人) hold a high resemblance to Earth and humans intentionally. During the events of the game, it appears to be covered in ice and snow due to New Kunlun(新崑崙) island and the Primordial Roots(古樹) absorbing the sunlight from the Sun.

Prior to the Eternal Cauldron Project(永生爐計畫), the Pale Blue Planet(蒼藍星) was largely untouched by solarian(太陽人) meddling and was settled instead by its native species of "ape men", also known as humans. While the exact time period, during which solarians(太陽人) found the Pale Blue Planet(蒼藍星), is unknown, we can assume that apemen(猿人) were discovered during prehistoric times. It's likely that most of these apemen(猿人) were at Stone Age or, perhaps, Neolithic development, following a path of nomadic survival towards the more tribal structure we see in their "villages" or livestock pens on New Kunlun(新崑崙). Although, it is alluded that even the tribal nature of Peach Blossom Village(桃花村) is artificially created and supported by solarians(太陽人).

# Apemen(猿人)
Apemen(猿人) is the Solarian(太陽人) name for humans. They have been first sighted from Haotian Sphere(昊天球) deep-space telescope and its reports showed primitive, but highly intelligent, humanoid creatures.

Since the Soulscape system(魂境系統) required an unprecedented amount of computing power to generate virtual worlds and it was one of core parts of Eternal Cauldron Project(永生爐計畫) proposed by Yi(羿) to deal with Tianhuo(天禍) crisis, the brains of apemen(猿人) connected in series were planned to be used as an immense biological computer.

After New Kunlun(新崑崙) Island warped to the Solar system, an unknown number of apemen(猿人) were abducted from Pale Blue Planet(蒼藍星) and placed within specially designated livestock pen with virtual environment.

They were mentioned to have been genetically modified, but not how exactly. Since Eternal Cauldron Project(永生爐計畫) needed a good-developed intelligent brains, it'd likely make sense if they targetted faster learning and thinking, better memory, but it also could be that Sols(太陽) transmutated(基因改造的) them for other needs. Their hair was already white prior to transmutation(基因改造) in Jiequan(截全)'s memory when they were delivered to his personal lab to be experimented on. Their hands are hidden in the image, but at the time of game events they share same four fingered hands as solarians(太陽人).
