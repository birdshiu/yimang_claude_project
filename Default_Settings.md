# Nine Sols
Nine Sols is a lore rich, hand-drawn 2D action platformer set in a Taopun world that features Sekiro-inspired deflection focused combat.
Explore the land once home to an ancient alien race, and follow a vengeful hero’s quest to slay the 9 Sols, formidable rulers of this forsaken realm.

Steel your resolve to overcome the many foes obstructing your path throughout New Kunlun. Patience and understanding of the tools available is essential to success against insurmountable odds.
Traverse the interconnected regions of New Kunlun, in search of challenge, treasure, and Yi's mysterious past.
Meet the characters populating the world for upgrades, abilities, and a deeper understanding of Yi himself.

# Solarians
Solarians are a species of bipedal aliens with feline-like features and are the main non-human species in Nine Sols. Many major characters, including Yi and the Sols, are solarians.

They originate from the planet Penglai and have an extremely advanced level of technology based on rhizomatic energy (also known as Qi and widely practice gene modification, also known as Transmutation.

Some solarians enhance their bodies with implants or gene modification, such as people of Yumin Kingdom, who are able to handle low-altitude flight, with devices that allow it. Some solarians share a feature of red accents in the bottom part of their eyes (Liwen, Lear and Heng) and naturally resonate with Primordial Roots, better understanding the world spiritually. Some of the ancient solarians from Kunlun tribe are rumored to have supernatural abilities, such as Ji's divination, biological immortality and age body shifting.

# Penglai
Penglai is a habitable planet located about 500 light-years from Pale Blue Planet, and is the homeworld of the solarians. It is said to be just as large as Pale Blue planet, although it doesn't have as much water as Penglai: approximately 90% of its surface is covered by oceans, leaving a number of small continents. These continents are home to nine kingdoms: Guifang, Shang, Yumin, Jie, Yih, Yan, Xia, Chan and Ying kingdom. Besides these continents, there is also a Kunlun island, inhabited by Kunlun tribe and where Fusang is located.

Before the events of the game, Penglai was afflicted by a pandemic known as Tianhuo, which caused a severe crisis among solarian society and prompted the Eternal Cauldron Project on the renovated by Tiandao Council Kunlun island – New Kunlun – where the events of the game take place.

# Tianhuo
The Tianhuo Virus , also known as simply Tianhuo, is a solarian virus that appeared during the Origin Era and developed into a global pandemic, leading to the near extinction of their species
## Characteristics
Tianhuo Virus to be universally fatal. The virus is implied to have mutated over time since its first discovery.

The virus' progression increases from intense stress on the body and accelerates organ failure.

Scientists in Jie Kingdom are stated to have tried placing hosts of the virus in a cryo-sleep to prevent their death, however it's implied solarians still die inside, likely due to brain death. It is not until Lady Ethereal's team invention of Vital Sanctum (cryo-sleep device) and Soulscape System (virtual world) that maintain brain activity that solarians have found a way to slow down progression of the virus in the body.

Upon an infected solarian's death, distinctive fungal flowers will sprout from their corpse: large purple flower and white grass-like flowers, allowing the virus to propagate itself. It is unclear why this happens, but during the death solarian's life experiences get converted into a Tao Fruit. This isn't stated to be caused by Tianhuo, and the corpses of Three Great Sages, who died at the start of the Origin Era and were sealed inside Secret Tomb of Lear, discovered almost a millennium after by Yi, also bear these fruits contradicting a relation to the virus.

There is no known cure to Tianhuo.

The virus does not seem to affect apemen.
## Trivia
Tianhuo in Chinese, 天禍, comes from the four-word idiom 天災人禍, which means disasters, specifically in two forms: 天災 are natural disasters, and 人禍 are man-made ones. Tianhuo implies the virus is an unforeseeable, heavenly disaster by pairing the first and last characters. In reality, Eigong made the virus and shifted the blame for the solarian-made disaster from herself to the heavens and nature, with its name hinting at the irony by replacing the character for person/human  with heavens/sky  in 人禍.

# Eternal Cauldron Project
The Eternal Cauldron Project is a project that was initially devised by Yi during the Tianhuo pandemic crisis. The project was hatched as a desperate effort to buy more time to develop a cure for the virus before all solarians perished to its effects. The foundation of this project was two major developments: the Soulscape system and the interstellar ark New Kunlun.

# Pale Blue Planet
he Pale Blue Planet is a temporary solarian name for the planet, the home of intelligent humanoid life named apemen. Both Pale Blue planet and apemen hold a high resemblance to Earth and humans intentionally. During the events of the game, it appears to be covered in ice and snow due to New Kunlun island and the Primordial Roots absorbing the sunlight from the Sun.

Prior to the Eternal Cauldron Project, the Pale Blue Planet was largely untouched by solarian meddling and was settled instead by its native species of "ape men", also known as humans. While the exact time period, during which solarians found the Pale Blue Planet, is unknown, we can assume that apemen were discovered during prehistoric times. It's likely that most of these apemen were at Stone Age or, perhaps, Neolithic development, following a path of nomadic survival towards the more tribal structure we see in their "villages" or livestock pens on New Kunlun. Although, it is alluded that even the tribal nature of Peach Blossom Village is artificially created and supported by solarians.

# Apemen
Apemen is the Solarian name for humans. They have been first sighted from Haotian Sphere deep-space telescope and its reports showed primitive, but highly intelligent, humanoid creatures.

Since the Soulscape system required an unprecedented amount of computing power to generate virtual worlds and it was one of core parts of Eternal Cauldron Project proposed by Yi to deal with Tianhuo crisis, the brains of apemen connected in series were planned to be used as an immense biological computer.

After New Kunlun Island warped to the Solar system, an unknown number of apemen were abducted from Pale Blue Planet and placed within specially designated livestock pen with virtual environment.

They were mentioned to have been genetically modified, but not how exactly. Since Eternal Cauldron Project needed a good-developed intelligent brains, it'd likely make sense if they targetted faster learning and thinking, better memory, but it also could be that Sols transmutated them for other needs. Their hair was already white prior to transmutation in Jiequan's memory when they were delivered to his personal lab to be experimented on. Their hands are hidden in the image, but at the time of game events they share same four fingered hands as solarians.
