**Fortune Teller** is an interactable machine crafted by Shuanshuan in Four Seasons Pavilion using components from the standard New Kunlun Shanhai 9000 models and prototype Shanhai 1000 brought to him and dismantled by Chiyou.

## Requirements

All of the next conditions need to be met for Shuanshuan to create Fortune Teller:

- Tiandao Academy Periodical was given to Shuanshuan and the cutscene where he creates the day-night cycle switch had been seen.
- Virtual Reality Device was given to Shuanshuan and VR addiction cutscene (only happens after defeating Lady Ethereal) had been seen.
- The Map Data Chips of all standard zone Shanhai 9000, from who you can request the chips (Reservoir, Greenhouse, Warehouse, Cortex, Empyrean and Grotto) were bought. Using the "Forcibly remove the chip" option prevents the creation of the Fortune Teller. Transmutation, Abandoned Mines and Research Institute chips are not required.

## Description

Fortune Teller appears on a table as New Kunlun Shanhai model's torso in light blue, pink and yellow color scheme with large spherical aquarium-like head; wires are sticking outside from where its shoulders should be. There is a Yin-yang symbol drawn on the table's cloth. In the background to the left (in the game) stands a banner with solarian text that presumably translates as "Fortune Teller" or "Future Predictor".

Upon interacting with the machine, it lights up with a projection of the random previously encountered Shanhai robot and delivers a line of dialogue with that robot's personality. Next Shanhai can be selected:

- Shanhai 9000 (Power Reservoir)
- Shanhai 9000 (Agricultural Zone)
- Shanhai 9000 (Warehouse Zone)
- Shanhai 9000 (Transmutation Zone)
- Shanhai 9000 (Central Core)
- Shanhai 9000 (Empyrean District)
- Shanhai 1000 (Grotto of Scriptures)

The first interaction with Fortune Teller also grants the player the Repurposed achievement.
