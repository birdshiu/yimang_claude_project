One of the Ten Sols in the Council and responsible for overseeing the Transmutation Factory. He is the final heir to the Jie Kingdom and prefers fighting over all other forms of communication. Jiequan is one of the Sols of the Tiandao Council. He holds the Sol Seal of Discipline.

# Appearance
Jiequan is a large, muscular solarian with a red skin, a dark gray mane, and long white eyebrows. His eyes are black with red pupils. He has two holes on his abdomen, which connect to white pipes that circle around his body. His clothing resembles dark grey sweatpants from the waist down, and green armour from the waist up, that exposes much of his arms and chest, and features large red spikes on the cuffs and shoulders, these red "beast horns" on his shoulder guards having been taken from past conquests[1]. Black spots across Jiequan's body allude to the traditional Chinese practice of cupping therapy, a folk remedy believed to improve health.

# Story
## Early Life
Born into the storied Jie family, a young Jiequan developed a staunch reverence for his ancestors' legacy. In particular, he looked up to his ancestor Jietong, the final emperor of the Jie Kingdom, who had historically attempted to unite the nine kingdoms of Penglai into one. Lamenting the decline of his once-legendary clan, Jiequan decided to make it his life's mission to restore the Jie Clan to its former glory. However, although Jiequan revered the might of his family's legacy, in his youth he lacked the means to embody it. Since the Turbulent era, the Jie family was forced to endure humiliation and stay in hiding, likely as a result of Jietong's defeat by the Fangshi Guild. In one instance of humiliation, Jiequan was beaten by a duo of bullies, who also desecrated a statue of Jietong: the bullies decapitated the statue's head, destroyed the statue's weapon, and damaged the rest of the statue.

Likely as a result of this childhood bullying, as well as his desire to restore his family's glory, Jiequan developed a near-fanatical obsession with transmutation. He repeatedly consumed Enhancement Elixirs and subjected his body to transmutation, to such levels that he would exceed the experimental limits of transmutation. He even went as far as to forcibly modify his waist, adding ports that he could use to quickly link with a Transmutation Crucible. These treatments pushed his physique to extremes: he developed massive, almost unnatural musculature, and in the process also halted his hair growth. Additionally, his staunch belief in his family's precept "heroes are forged in agony" caused him to form a grisly obsession with pain, which he eagerly shared with others.

## Life as a Sol
At some point Jiequan was invited to become a Sol of the Tiandao Council, being bestowed a Sol Seal signifying Discipline.

In the first council meeting that Yi proposed the Eternal Cauldron Project, Jiequan complimented Yi, calling him charming. Unlike Goumang, Jiequan was willing to hear what Yi had to propose. When Yi proposed placing solarians in cryo-sleep to delay the progression of Tianhuo, Jiequan interjected that scientists in Jie had already considered such an experiment and had been unsuccessful. Yi responded that Lady Ethereal had discovered a solution to making the project work, in the form of Vital Sanctums and Soulscapes.

In the second council meeting that Yi proposed the Eternal Cauldron Project, Yi explained that the brains that would be used to form biological calculators to create the Soulscapes that powered the Eternal Cauldron Project would be sourced from primitive, humanoid creatures living on the Pale Blue Planet located five hundred lightyears away from Penglai. Jiequan expressed his approval and excitement at the plan, in particular the possibility of hunting down the creatures, stating that the "Jie Clan was itching to get its muscles moving". As a part of the project, Jiequan was assigned responsibility over the Transmutation Zone, which would be responsible for the creation of genetically modified guards.

Some time during the Tianhuo pandemic, Eigong apparently informed Jiequan, along with the rest of the Sols besides Yi, that she was responsible for the creation of the Tianhuo. Jiequan chose to continue cooperating with Eigong, concealing the origin of the Tianhuo from Yi.

Following New Kunlun's launch and warp to the Pale Blue Planet, Jiequan and his soldiers were involved in the kidnapping of apemen for usage in the Eternal Cauldron Project. He directly oversaw this process, commanding his servants to "be careful with his precious experimental materials".

As the Sol overseeing the Transmutation Zone, Jiequan also oversaw the Prison, which was located within the zone. One of the prisoners held within the prison, Kanghui, became the subject of Jiequan's inhumane and unfathomably cruel torture and experimental transmutation. During Kanghui's time in the prison, someone informed Jiequan of Kanghui's special regenerative abilities. It was found that Kanghui possessed a special gene, which would later be dubbed the "Kanghui regeneration gene", that allowed genetically modified guards to withstand multiple rounds of genetic modification. Owing to this utility, Kanghui was kept alive by Jiequan and effectively used as a guinea pig to create stronger genetically modified guards. Jiequan's experiments eventually transformed Kanghui into a gigantic, aberrant creature.

After awakening due to Yi's activity on New Kunlun but before encountering Yi for the first time, Jiequan was visited by Ji. Ji prophesized that Yi would make Jiequan meet his end.

# Game Events
Yi is first contacted by Jiequan immediately after obtaining his third Sol Seal. Jiequan laughs and mockingly compliments Yi for the "outrageous spectacle he's been performing all over New Kunlun". Yi tells Jiequan to spare him of the fake friendliness, leading Jiequan to pivot and begin insulting Yi. Jiequan insults Yi both for "playing the hero" and for having struggled in his efforts for revenge, as Jiequan considers Yi's previous opponents to be weak: Yanlao for being a dying old man, and Goumang for being just a farmer. Jiequan concludes that Yi is in need of stimulation and offers to meet Yi in order to have an 'intimate duel'. claiming that he's also prepared gifts for Yi no matter the outcome. To convince Yi that he isn't setting a trap, Jiequan deactivates the traps in the Transmutation Zone and sends Yi his location, which is the bottom floor of the Factory's Great Hall in front of the Great Hall's agitators.

True to Jiequan's word, Yi finds that the Transmutation Zone's traps have been disabled, and that Jiequan is where he said he would be. After a short conversation in which Jiequan expresses joy at seeing Yi again, and then compares himself to Yi, the fight begins. This fight is unwinnable; no matter how hard Yi tries, he is eventually defeated by Jiequan, falling to the ground and losing consciousness. Jiequan takes Yi to the Prison, restraining Yi in an electrical torture device.

Jiequan forcibly awakens Yi by shocking him with the electrical torture device. After confirming that Yi is awake, Jiequan proceeds to shock the Fangshi again, remarking that the torture device hasn't been used since the Kanghui affair, and that he's surprised that it still works. Jiequan comments that Yi's body is "quite fascinating", as it can endure countless blows, but still feels pain in spite of its resilience. Yi attempts to provoke Jiequan but Jiequan refuses to be provoked, recognizing that if he kills Yi it would be likely that he would regenerate from a Root Node. Jiequan expresses jealousy that Yi can withstand immense amounts of pain but not die, as he believes that "one can only become a whole person after enduring mental and physical trials". Jiequan's faith to this principle is such that he considers Yi's torture to be "helping" Yi: the suffering will lead Yi to embrace the truth of who he is. A person who, according to Jiequan, "is even more twisted and bloodthirsty than Jiequan is". Jiequan concludes his explanation by torturing Yi into unconsciousness.

The next day, Jiequan forcibly awakens Yi again with a shock. He informs Yi that nobody can reach Eigong, and that the council meeting about Yi will have to be postponed. Jiequan jokes that Eigong and Yi, like mentor and student, "like pulling off disappearing acts". However, Jiequan shrugs this off, appreciating that Eigong's absence means that he can continue to "play" with Yi. Jiequan then explains to Yi something interesting that he's noticed: having taken a stroll around the 95th Livestock Pen, Jiequan noticed that the pen was missing two people: Shennong and Shuanshuan. Based on Yi's reaction, Jiequan deduces that Yi was responsible for their disappearances, along with the disappearance of Kuafu. Jiequan excitedly proclaims that he plans to unite Yi with the missing three, and implies that he intends to harvest Shennong and Shuanshuan's brains. Laughing at Yi's suffering, Jiequan marches away after torturing Yi into unconsciousness again.

After leaving Yi, Jiequan commands guards to invade and attack Peach Blossom Village in order to aggravate Yi, as Jiequan deduced that Yi had previously hidden in the village.

Some time later, Jiequan is visited by Ji while resting within Shengwu Hall. Jiequan calls the person "seer" and informs that their predictions were wrong, saying that Yi had been taken care of and their prophecies could be inaccurate after all, stating that their fatalism doesn't work on him. The seer informs Jiequan that the hexagrams are set in place, and that the fate that they previously foresaw for Jiequan remains unchanged. Jiequan diverges from the topic of prophecy, declaring his disinterest in continuing to speak about the predictions, and proclaims to the seer that he will eliminate his enemies and protect the council. Soon, Jiequan notices that he's due for a transmutation session, in response seer remarks that they never imagined that Jiequan would be able to get stronger by using transmutation. Jiequan rebuffs this sentiment, calling those that consider transmutation to be against the laws of nature alarmists and cowards. He bids farewell to the seer and enters his Transmutation Crucible to become stronger.

When Yi arrives at Shengwu Hall, he pulls Jiequan out of transmutation early. Jiequan expresses amusement that Yi managed to escape, declaring that he's eager to fight.

If Yi does not use Gene Eradicator, Jiequan immediately commences the fight. Gene Eradicator can then be recycled for 1600 Jin at the 3D Printer.

If Yi uses the Gene Eradicator on the Transmutation Crucible, after exiting, Jiequan's speech gets interrupted by bloody coughing, but he then realizes what has happened and cackles, applauding Yi for finally playing dirty just like him. Jiequan asserts that playing dirty is a noble solarian tradition, and that evil is engraved in solarian genes. Jiequan goes as far as to state that "solarians must learn to be more selfish, ruthless, and rugged than anyone else". Yi rejects this notion, saying that this thinking is how the Jie Kingdom brought about its own destruction. Jiequan reiterates his intent to restore the Jie kingdom once the virus is cured, and offers to turn Yi into a guard so that he can see it, as Jiequan intends to kill Yi. After this, the fight commences.

Yi manages to beat Jiequan down to his knees. Jiequan mocks Yi for thinking that the fight is over, chugging an Enhancement Elixir and thanking Yi for inflicting pain upon him. Jiequan utters his family's precept - that heroes are forged in agony - moments before his attempt to transmutate himself backfires. The transmutation causes Jiequan's body to disintegrate and explode into a white fungus-infested pile of flesh, ending his life. His liquified corpse immediately blooms a fungal flower bearing a Greater Tao Fruit.

# Chamber
Reflecting Jiequan's obsession with his legacy, his chamber in the Shengwu Hall contains reminders of his family's ancestral roots and his attempts to fulfill his legacy. The statue of Jietong, which had been desecrated by bullies in the past, stands in the center of Jiequan's chamber in front of a map of the Nine Kingdoms of Penglai. This statue, along with the maps, signifies the Jie family's values as well as the family's unfulfilled quest to dominate and unite the Nine Kingdoms. Scattered across the Jiequan's Vital Sanctum chamber are many enhancement elixirs that Jiequan would consume to transmutate his body, in his ill-fated attempts to become as strong as possible.
