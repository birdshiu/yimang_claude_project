**Fuxi** (traditional Chinese: 伏羲; pinyin: *Fúxī*,One of the Ten Sols in the Council and administrator of the Empyrean District along with his twin sister. Fuxi hails from the Feng Clan, an ancient aristocratic family of the Shang Kingdom.) and **Nuwa** (traditional Chinese: 女媧; pinyin: *Nǚwā*,One of the Ten Sols in the Council and administrator of the Empyrean District along with his twin sister. Fuxi hails from the Feng Clan, an ancient aristocratic family of the Shang Kingdom.
One of the Ten Sols in the Council and administrator of the Empyrean District along with her twin brother. Nuwa's aristocratic background allows her to live a life of decadent luxury.) are twins and two of the ten Sols in the Tiandao Council. Both serve as administrators of the Empyrean District, holding Sol Seals of Integrity and Selflessness, respectively. They hail from the Feng Clan (traditional Chinese: 風氏; pinyin: *Fēng shì*), an ancient aristocratic family of the Shang Kingdom.

## Appearance

The Fengs are solarians with light gray fur, their ears pinned directly up in intricate dark blue, gold and dark green headpieces. They both wear red eyeliner and blue eyeshadow, and have slitted black pupils with narrow eyes. Fuxi wears beige robes with large decorative white and gold hood, and red, dark blue and dark green decorative garments around his shoulders and around his neck. Nuwa shares similar clothing, with a slight design changes. Fuxi sports an incredibly large, stocky build, while Nuwa sports a small, thin build.

When encountered in the Banquet Hall where their boss battle takes place, Fuxi has a mask covering his face under the headpiece, with a red symbol glowing over it after Nuwa plays her flute. He removes the red cloak to reveal large, pink pustules of the mutation covering much of his body, and his right hand being disproportionately large in comparison to his body, featuring massive claws. The garments he retains are slightly simpler formal clothing in the same style as his previous garments. Nuwa exchanges the formal attire for a simple dress that exposes her hips, legs and arms. Additionally, she sits upon an floating emerald green pillow able to float much like Kuafu's own.

## Story

### Past

Twins Nuwa and Fuxi were born as the heirs of the esteemed Feng Clan, an ancient aristocratic family of the Shang Kingdom. Unlike another ancient aristocratic family, the Jie Clan, who obtained and exerted power through martial prowess, the Feng Clan obtained their wealth and power through being merchants.

As they grew up, the two siblings became inseparable. One way their closeness visibly manifested was in the form of acting, which developed into a lifelong passion for the both of them. Fuxi and Nuwa both became opera performers, despite disapproval from their mother, whom considered acting to be lower-class. Fuxi would even help Nuwa apply makeup. One particularly significant opera in which the two performed was a romantic one. The Fengs cherished these times greatly, and kept many mementos associated with their time as opera performers.

At some point, Fuxi became the head of the Feng Clan. He took his duties seriously, and leveraged the wealth of his family in order to strengthen the Feng Clan. Feng wealth was used to fund Eigong's immortality research project; it is unclear if this investment choice was personally made by Fuxi himself or if it was made by someone else.

### Life as Sols

While the Tianhuo pandemic caused unfathomable devastation, Fuxi viewed the pandemic as an opportunity to strengthen the Feng Clan. The crisis created a situation where the Feng Clan could take advantage of the crisis and use their accumulated wealth to obtain greater power. At some point, both Fuxi and Nuwa were invited to become Sols of the Tiandao Council; Fuxi was granted the Sol Seal signifying Integrity, while Nuwa was granted the Sol Seal signifying Selflessness. This appointment was likely the result of bribery.

The Fengs were present for the two council meetings in which fellow Sol Yi proposed the Eternal Cauldron Project. In the second meeting, Nuwa responded disdainfully to Yi's proposal to harvest brains from beings located on the Pale Blue Planet as ingredients for biological calculators, as the planet in question was located five hundred lightyears away from Penglai. Being so far, it appeared impossible to reach the planet in time before all Solarians succumbed to the effects of Tianhuo, with Nuwa remarking that they didn't even know if they could make it to the next decade before dying to Tianhuo. Yi addressed Nuwa's concern by explaining additional details of his project: that they would turn the island of Kunlun into a vessel capable of using rhizomatic energy to instantly warp to the planet.

In accordance with this plan, the Fengs were assigned responsibility and administration over the Empyrean District, which became a luxurious district of the passenger area of New Kunlun. Although Nuwa was technically assigned responsibility and administration over the Empyrean District as well, only Fuxi was actually in charge, overseeing its planning and construction. In addition to managing the Empyrean District, Fuxi established more power for the Feng Clan on New Kunlun by placing confidants all across New Kunlun and having his personal goods stored in the Hexacrem Vault of the Boundless Repository.

At some point, the Fengs were apparently informed by Eigong about the truth behind the Tianhuo pandemic: that she accidentally created and unleashed the Tianhuo Virus; they chose to continue cooperating with Eigong, concealing the knowledge from Yi.

Some time before New Kunlun launched, a nobleman aboard the vessel named Kanghui apparently offended the Fengs; this resulted in Kanghui's imprisonment.

Sometime after the completion of the Empyrean District, around Foundation Era Year 2, Fuxi's life would come to a tragic end: he succumbed to an early death to the Tianhuo Virus. Shortly after Fuxi's death, Eigong visited a grieving Nuwa holding Fuxi's hand. In seemingly an act of benevolence, Eigong informed them that she could make an exception for Fuxi and allow him to try an experimental cure for the Tianhuo. Desperate for any means to return life to her brother, Nuwa graciously accepted, and began to regularly administer Tianhuo Serum to Fuxi in hopes of curing his condition. The serum effectively reanimated Fuxi's corpse; while it did not return him to his prime, it brought him back to life enough to partially speak with Nuwa.

Although the experimental serum reanimated Fuxi's corpse, it proved to be far from a cure. While Eigong originally believed that it possessed the potential to cure the Tianhuo, after centuries of researching its effects in Fuxi and the Dusk Guardians, she discovered that 90% of those who received the serum failed to recover from the virus, while the remaining 10% became Tianhuo Mutants. Fuxi was one of the few who became a Tianhuo Mutant, losing what little sanity he still possessed and swelling into a gigantic, pustule-ridden monster.

In spite of the abundant evidence that her brother was effectively dead, Nuwa was unable to accept that Fuxi was gone. She used a mask and heavy robes to hide Fuxi's twisted form. Although eventually Fuxi could only make garbled, inhuman noises, Nuwa interpreted these noises as speech. Additionally, like many solarians, Nuwa chose to numb her emotions and ignore reality by descending into a lifestyle of extreme hedonism; she constantly left her Vital Sanctum in order to hold extravagant, drug-fueled banquets even though doing so accelerated the advancement of Tianhuo infection.

### Game Events

When Yi arrives at the Empyrean District for the first time, he is initially wary, as the area seems too quiet and has lax security. Soon he encounters horrors beyond imagination: the area is infested with Tianhuo Mutants: aberrant, violent creatures. Eventually, after fending his way through these mutants, Yi finds himself in Fuxi's chamber. A looming figure is seated within the chamber, watching a looping recording of Fuxi and Nuwa's romantic opera, but Yi does not confront the figure; instead, Yi proceeds to Fuxi's Vital Sanctum and seizes his Sol Seal.

Upon disconnecting from Fuxi's Vital Sanctum, Yi no longer sees the looming figure around. Nuwa immediately contacts him by transmission, asking him when he became a thief and snidely informing him that stealing Sol Seals is considered a capital offense. Yi fires back a thorny response of his own, mockingly asking if the Feng Clan has finally taken an interest in the law; Yi also states that she's changed as well. Nuwa curtly states that she "couldn’t care less about whatever [his] deal is" and that she's only contacting him because "her brother complained that [Yi was] disturbing [their] play"; this confirms to Yi that the looming figure in the room was Fuxi.

Nuwa proposes a deal to Yi: he can quickly grab whatever he's looking for, but in exchange, he would have to leave her and Fuxi alone so that Nuwa could continue planning her banquets. Yi questions how Nuwa can plan banquets, given the state of the Empyrean District. Nuwa proceeds to interpret Yi's words as him insulting the construction quality of the Empyrean District. She agrees with this sentiment, remarking that the design and architecture ended up being tacky, that the district's restaurants were atrocious, and that she regrets not recruiting more chefs onto New Kunlun. Despite these issues, Nuwa disdainfully asserts that they have to make do with what they have, and accept living in sub-par conditions, hence her continued planning of banquets.

Yi heatedly corrects Nuwa, telling her that he's talking about the apocalyptic conditions of the Empyrean District, and how it's covered with living corpses. Nuwa rebukes Yi's assertation that Tianhuo Mutants are problematic, brushing them aside as simply passengers who couldn't adapt to their Soulscapes, and states that the issue is one that the guards or the Dusk Guardians can handle. Shortly thereafter, she takes her leave and ends the transmission, as she's sleepy and has her brother and guests waiting for her. She tells Yi that instead of coming up to bother her and Fuxi, he should go and bother Eigong instead.

As Yi continues through the Empyrean District, he, Kuafu, and Ruyi attempt to identify the cause behind the Tianhuo Mutants. They identify that the monsters appear to be mutated from solarians, and that the mutation appears to be infectious, capable of even infecting solarians within their Soulscapes. Kuafu questions if Nuwa or Fuxi had something to do with the outbreak of mutants but Yi disagrees, as Nuwa seemed unaware of the gravity of the situation. Additionally, Yi doesn't believe that either twin could pull off anything like it.

Eventually, Yi arrives at the Nobility Hall, which houses Nuwa's chamber and her ongoing banquet. Besides Nuwa and Fuxi, a number of other solarians are present, all intoxicated beyond reason. Nuwa informs Yi that the banquet is private, and tells him that he can't be there without an invitation. Yi questions how Nuwa can still be in the mood for holding a banquet, when an apocalypse is occurring outside in the rest of the Empyrean District. Nuwa responds that under the apocalyptic conditions, banquets are precisely what they should be in the mood for. She believes that it's better to choose joy rather than sorrow, and considers optimism to be a talent.

Enraged at Nuwa's nonchalance toward the disaster unfolding in the Empyrean District Yi reprimands her, telling her that she needs to see the conditions for herself, and that they need to act quickly and wake everyone up to handle the situation. Nuwa completely rejects Yi's pleas, again reiterating her belief that the Tianhuo Mutant situation isn't serious. She explains they've always been low on resources to maintain Soulscape operations. As a result, many people have already passed away within their Soulscapes. Scornfully, Nuwa asks Yi if the Eternal Cauldron Project was going as he planned, implying that Yi's responsible for the terrible events that have unfolded. Yi denies responsibility, as he wasn't aware of the flaws within the project and also wasn't in control of the project following Eigong's betrayal. In return, Nuwa calls Yi "the one with [his] head in the sand all along", insinuating that he's the one who's been ignorant this whole time.

Derisively, Nuwa tells Yi not to worry as "[she and Fuxi] still love New Kunlun, the second home of their dreams", since it's the only place where they can "party for all eternity... with never-ending music and ever-present guests". Having had enough of Nuwa's revolting hedonism, Yi cuts to the chase, and states that he's only there to collect the remaining Sol Seals.

Yi demands that Nuwa hand over her and Fuxi's Sol Seals, and that after she does he'll leave them alone to "rot in [their] own decadence". At first, Nuwa is willing to hand them over; however, she is interrupted by Fuxi's unintelligible mumbling. Despite being incoherent, Nuwa interprets the mumbling to be Fuxi's denial of Yi's demands. She claims that Fuxi said that Yi is erratic, hates the rich and powerful, and that giving Yi their Sol Seals could hurt them in the future. Furthermore, Fuxi apparently said that Yi even had the potential to destroy New Kunlun. Now considering Yi to be a threat to their lives, Nuwa and Fuxi engage him in battle to defend their Sol Seals.

Yi successfully defeats the Fengs, dismembering Fuxi's left arm and destroying Fuxi's mask in the process. This reveals Fuxi's lifeless face, covered in fleshy lumps and drooling. A distressed Nuwa cries out at the destruction of Fuxi's mask, frantically trying to get to Fuxi. In the process, Nuwa tumbles off of her floating cushion, and is forced to crawl over to Fuxi; while crawling, she wails about how much effort she put into his makeup. A concerned Yi recognizes the advanced decay of Fuxi's body and attempts to ask Nuwa if Fuxi was already in a terrible state before New Kunlun's departure, but is sharply told to shut up by Nuwa. She insists that despite the fact Fuxi may have been in a poor state before departure, that Fuxi will be eventually be fully cured as along as he keeps receiving his shots.

A sobbing Nuwa cradles Fuxi's face, imploring her brother not to listen to Yi; she comforts Fuxi and tells him that New Kunlun has eliminated sickness, old age, and death. She recites their lifelong promise: that they'll stay together until the end of time. As Nuwa embraces him in a hug, Nuwa tells him that once he gets better, she wants to go to the theater together with him. Soon thereafter, the two meet their end when Fuxi's body spontaneously mutates and erupts spikes. These spikes skewer Nuwa to death, leaving both twins with agonized expressions painted upon their faces.

The defeat of the Fengs allows Yi to proceed to Nuwa's chamber, where he claims her Sol Seal. Besides her Sol Seal, Yi also finds the Tianhuo Serum, which he deduces were given to Fuxi in some sort of treatment, seemingly to cure him of Tianhuo. Yi presents this serum to Ruyi, who analyzes it for its composition. Ruyi determines that rather than acting as an antidote to the Tianhuo, the serum instead acts as a catalyst for the continued mutation of the virus. Yi determines that the serum was created by Eigong, and resolves to find out why she made the serum when he next encounters her.

When Yi gains access to the Super Mutant Buster crafted from the Tianhuo Serum and Ji's Hair, he can put the Fengs fully to rest. This can be accomplished by returning to the Nobility Hall and slaying a Mutant Core that has emerged from Fuxi's remains. Doing so destroys what remains of Fuxi's body, save for his head, and allows Nuwa's body to be taken over by white Tianhuo fungus. The fungal flower that erupts from Nuwa's corpse bears a Twin Tao Fruit, a unique variant of Tao Fruit that exists nowhere else.

### Chambers

Nuwa's Vital Sanctum chamber inside the Nobility Hall, is filled with mementos that each relate to her cherished memories. The most visible mementos are ones originating from her time as an opera performer: opera costumes and theatrical makeup. These mementos symbolize how she and Fuxi are both carrying on an endless theatrical act, in spite of the impending apocalypse before them.

In contrast, Fuxi's Vital Sanctum chamber inside the Empyrean District (Living Area) contains little to nothing; the only identifiable memento present in his chamber is a looping recording of an opera he and Nuwa starred in. This possibly symbolizes how, in spite of losing his sanity, the part of Fuxi that cherishes and loves his sister still remains.

## Boss

**The Fengs** are the Sol bosses of the Empyrean District. They hold Sol Seals of Integrity and Selflessness.

### Combat Appearance

#### Nuwa

- Footbinded feet
- Matching cyan opera headpiece with Fuxi, two long spikes that curve inwards with gold rings that cover sections of the spikes, headpiece wraps over ears around chin
- Neck wrapped in a gold padaung with a gold necklace made up of concentric half circles
- Wears a red scapular with gold trims and navy blue hems
- Wrists and ankles are wrapped in multiple gold rings
- Plays an ornamented recorder (might be a xiao flute)

#### Fuxi

**In past:**

- Matching necklace with Nuwa, one more half circle
- Cyan robes that match headpiece and reach down to hide feet, decorated with gold geometric shapes on side of leg, held by ornate gold belt
- Uses a Zhu(wp:Zhu_(string_instrument))

**After Tianhuo mutation:**

- Has pink cysts (mostly on the right shoulder and the clawed hand)
- Mask (with a red symbol that appears when Nuwa plays) that hides his face
- Holds the Zhu like a baton and wields it as a weapon

### Combat

#### Arena

A spacious hall featuring a large boss and attacks. The battle happens during the banquet organized by Nuwa and solarians lost in ecstasy are seen in the background.

#### Phase 1 attacks

Fuxi attacks:

- Charges up a crimson slam, which, if not parried, will leave a long lasting fire floor
- Jump slams the ground with his hand followed by a scratch along the ground or a zhu slam (scratch hitbox is behind)
- Does a downwards slam with zhu sometimes followed by a stab with it
- Uppercuts with hand followed by zhu downwards slam
- Runs across the arena dragging his arm behind

#### Phase 2 attacks

In phase 2 Nuwa joins in with her attacks (they combo their attacks):

- 9 consective blast fields on top of and following Yi (always uses this at start of phase 2). Only hit once, unlike thunderburst explosions. Usually followed by Fuxi's Jump Slam, unless interrupted by stagger or turning.
- 2 homing needles in tandem with Fuxi's attacks:
  - Crimson slam
  - Runs across the arena arm dragging behind
  - Jump slam
  - Scratch
  - Zhu slam
- Homing purple orb (Tai-Chi Kick- or Unbounded Counter-counterable):
  - Jump slam
- Serpents moving horizontally across the screen from one, then other direction:
  - Uppercut
- Serpent columns from the ceiling in alternating pattern, repeating 3 times:
  - Uppercut
  - Runs across the arena arm dragging behind
- Wall of 6 Serpent columns approaching Yi from the ceiling from one side, which cover around ~80% of the arena. Can be prematurely avoided by dashing through with precise timing:
  - Jump slam

## Trivia

### Gameplay

- On earlier versions of the game, by going to the left door of the arena during second phase transition, it was possible to lock Fuxi in his idle walking animation without Nuwa attacking, making possible to kill him with slashes without needing to fight. This was due to the door closing the arena on the left being a narrower type, which allowed to get out of Nuwa's detection area, which covers all arena, except that space. Without her ever starting attacks, Fuxi is stuck in following player state. However, getting too far into door with slash movement can cause Yi to be pushed away from the wall into the detection, so it requires some precision.
- Purple orbs Nuwa summons in second phase are parriable using Unbounded Counter, but crimson snakes aren't.

### Lore

- The game heavily implies that an incestuous relationship exists between Fuxi and Nuwa:
  - Fuxi and Nuwa in Chinese mythology are both siblings and are married to one another.
  - The opera costumes worn by Fuxi and Nuwa draw inspiration from classical Peking Opera costumes, but also elements of traditional Chinese wedding gowns.
  - Nuwa suffers from deformed feet, a nod to the ancient Chinese practice of foot binding among noblewomen. Beyond implying her privileged birth, it also alludes to the deformities often associated with close-kin marriages.
  - Fuxi and Nuwa starred in at least one romantic opera, in which the two played characters are romantically intertwined.
  - A common tradition in traditional Chinese weddings is the ritual of three bows, which is used to officially recognize a couple's union. This involves the couple performing three bows: one bow in respect of the heavens, one bow in respect of the couple's parents, and one bow in respect to one another. The Sol statues depicting Fuxi and Nuwa found in the Empyrean District are wearing their opera costumes while bowing to one another, very likely alluding to this ritual.

### Inspirations

- Fuxi is a culture hero in Chinese mythology, credited along with his sister and wife Nüwa with creating humanity and the invention of music, hunting, fishing, domestication, and cooking, as well as the Cangjie system of writing Chinese characters around 2900 BC or 2000 BC. Fuxi was counted as the first mythical emperor of China, "a divine being with a serpent's body" who was miraculously born, a Taoist deity, and/or a member of the Three Sovereigns at the beginning of the Chinese dynastic period.
- Nüwa is a mother goddess, culture hero, and/or member of the Three Sovereigns of Chinese mythology. She is a goddess in Chinese folk religion, Chinese Buddhism, Confucianism and Taoism. She is credited with creating humanity and repairing the Pillar of Heaven.
- The intertwined serpent and needle attacks performed by Nuwa reference the ancient Chinese myth "Nuwa Mends the Heavens"; the intertwined serpents represent the shared serpentine imagery of both Fuxi and Nuwa, while the needles represent mending.
  - The motif of intertwined serpents is also abundant elsewhere in relation to Fuxi and Nuwa. Banners and other decorations in the Nobility Hall bear the intertwined serpent.
- The Feng's appearances are similar to Oriental Shorthair cats.
