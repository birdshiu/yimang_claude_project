**Heng** (traditional Chinese: 恆; pinyin: *Héng*) is the younger sister of Yi. During the events of the game, the player receives messages from her and experiences flashbacks of their past.

## Appearance

### Young

In her youth, Heng was a short Solarian with pale yellow fur. Two curls of fur on either side of her head were styled after a bob hairstyle. She wore a blue outfit, similar in appearance to her brother Yi's.

She has large dark eyes and a small nose. Her eyes have red accents, highlighting her connection and natural resonation with Primordial Roots.

### Adult

As an adult, Heng was still short for a Solarian but taller than Yi. She also had more curls of fur on her head, representing a longer hairstyle and giving her a more mature appearance. Her outfit, still blue and green, was more elaborate.

## Story

### Early Life

Heng was born to a Taoist family in the Xia Kingdom, with her older brother Yi. Even as a child she was one of a few Solarians with a strong connection to the Primordial Roots. She was always very fond of Yi and followed him around often in her youth. During her childhood, Heng had a strong sense of curiosity and a stubborn will. At some point she was in possession of the Azure Bow.

### Peach Blossom Village

Heng's first appearance is during the opening cutscene on the outskirts of Peach Blossom Village, where she is briefly seen in Yi's hallucination, playing her flute while surrounded with white fungal flowers and two Tianhuo flowers.

### Messages

*The order of these cutscenes may vary depending on which locations you visit first.*

#### Power Reservoir

Heng's first message to Yi is received when riding the transporter to enter the [Central Power Reservoir](Power_Reservoir_(Central) "wikilink") for the first time. A flashback occurs, wherein a young Yi and Heng discuss the rhizomatic towers of Penglai over a game of Qiankun. During this scene, the track Memory of You plays.

#### Lake Yaochi

Heng's second message is received when climbing a tower to the east of Lake Yaochi in the Lake Yaochi Ruins. A flashback occurs, wherein a young Yi and Heng discuss the Primordial Roots and the concept of energy conversion. During this scene, the track Stargazer plays.

#### Warehouse Zone

Heng's third message is received when riding the container transporter from the Outer Warehouse to the Inner Warehouse for the first time. A flashback occurs, wherein a young Heng discovers a young Yi hiding in a pot to avoid their parents. During this scene, the track Hide and Seek plays.

#### Factory

Heng's fourth message is received after losing Jiequan's duel in the [Factory (Great Hall)](Factory_(Great_Hall) "wikilink"). Yi experiences a brief flashback regarding the creation of the Mystic Nymph. During this scene, the track My Dream plays.

#### Dream

The continuation of Yi's flashback about the Mystic Nymph occurs after defeating Lady Ethereal in her Dream. When Yi injects rhizomatic energy into the Mystic Nymph (as Heng calls it), the process backfires and causes a massive explosion. Yi uses his body to protect Heng, who survives unscathed. Unfortunately, Yi is less lucky and is ripped into pieces by the sheer force. Heng sobs over his mangled body. As she cries, a green glow begins to emanate from her and the Primordial Roots used in the experiment begin to creep closer to Yi's body. A green field of energy (possibly Qi) surrounds the pair, and the Roots completely envelop it.

#### Grotto of Scriptures

Heng's fifth message is received upon approaching the altar and statue of Lear in the [Grotto of Scriptures (West)](Grotto_of_Scriptures_(West) "wikilink"). Heng starts the message by mentioning that Peace Day and Lear Day are coming up and that people are preparing to celebrate the festival as usual. She tells how she discovered that those who have lost more family members tend to present more abundant offerings as she visited a few of their neighbors. Since their meals have always been simple, she doesn't worry about much, and thanks to the peach tree their Dad have planted at Yi's birth, they were able to make sweet peach blossom wine almost every year.

Yi experiences a flashback where he approached his parents' home soon after being coronated a Sol and tried to persuade them into coming with him to New Kunlun with promise of saving his family, but was harshly declined by his parents. Memory starts with his Mom shutting Yi off, telling that they won't change their minds and will not go and that neither will Heng. She tells him to "never put Heng in harm's way again" and reminds him how much trouble he cause the last time (explosion accident during Mystic Nymph's experiment), after what she leaves. His Dad tries to stop and calm her down, but is unable. He's disappointed they can't have an exchange lasting more than a few minutes. He tells Yi that "[he] should know in [his] age" and to stop being so stubborn with his mother, after what he follows her.

After they're gone, Heng, who heard most of the conversation, comes out from behind the peach tree, and asks if he will stay for dinner, but Yi doubts they'll be able to eat together without starting another quarrel. She tells him to cheer up and reminds him of the cause for celebration - he was finally made a Sol after all years of work in the Tiandao Council, but Yi says to forget about it, since for his parents this doesn't seem to be something to celebrate. He's baffled with his parents' decision to stay and await death, while the entire world is desperate for a place on the island. Heng speaks about how group of Taoists believe that they should accept this arrangement and return to Penglai's soil, following their faith of inaction that they believe since the era of peace began. If science is the answer for Yi, then for them inaction is the truth. But Yi calls it a delusion of fanatics, reminding that Lear and Fangshi Guild were a scientific organization in the first place before superstitious Taoist drive took over. He exclaims that with his plan, he'll not only ensure their survival, but also create a paradise and Heng agrees, responding that she always believed in him.

Putting all of that aside, she asks Yi again if he will stay to drink the peach blossom wine that they closed last year, saying how she looked forward to trying it with him for the whole year. He tells her that they'll do it next time as he needs to hurry back and make a report to his mentor, he tells her that he'll come back soon and asks Heng to take care of herself and to help convince Mom and Dad.

Heng finishes the message, reminded of that batch of wine still being sealed for who knows how many years. She says that if the four of them could sit together again under the peach tree in their yard, that's always strewn with flower petals during this time of year, drinking wine, chitchatting... It wouldn't be that far off from his paradise.

#### Empyrean District

Heng's sixth message is received upon entering the large room in the Empyrean District. Yi experiences a flashback to a meeting with Heng in front of their parents' graves, who succumbed to Tianhuo virus, and a conversation between them shortly before the departure of New Kunlun. Heng talks about how Tianhuo is 'not the end' and rather the transition, while Yi makes it clear that he does not intend to leave Heng on the dying planet, Penglai. The scene ends with Yi promising to return and pick her her up once everything is ready.

#### Eigong's Vital Sanctum

Heng's seventh message is received during the final part of the Eigong vital sanctum cutscene. Yi experiences a flashback about his final moments with Heng, whom tells Yi how she wants to stay on Penglai. Outraged by this statement and in a fit of rage, Yi tells Heng that he will come back one final time before the departure of New Kunlun, but if Heng should decide to not come with Yi, she will be gone to him. The scene concludes on a very emotional note, with Heng asking Yi why he never came back for her. Neither Yi nor Heng knew that he would only wake up 500 years later.

#### Galactic Dock

Heng's eighth and final message is received at the galactic dock during the ***Save the Apemen*** ending, commonly referred to as the True Ending. It is revealed that Heng has sent the messages that Yi has been receiving hundreds of years ago, aware that they would never reach him in time. She tells Yi that her time has come, and that this will be her final message. Still devastated by the fact that he never had a chance to save Heng, Yi, for the first time, opens up about his feelings, and promises Heng that he'll take the time to tell her his story when destiny reunites them.

## Trivia

- Heng is inspired by Chang'e in Chinese mythology, originally known as *Heng'e*, who is the goddess of the Moon and wife of Hou Yi, the great archer, — although, in the game she was changed to be his sister.
- The red accents in Heng's eyes are also shared by Lear. This signifies their connection to the Primordial Roots.
- Heng has the most tracks from the OST dedicated to her. Most of them start with a flute leitmotif, with piano and violin accompanying the themes.
