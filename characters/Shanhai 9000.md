**Shanhai 9000** (traditional Chinese: 山海9000; pinyin: *Shānhǎi Jiǔqiān*) is a unique robot of an older Shanhai 9000 assistive model made by solarians. It's a prominent character across many mediums of Nine Sols universe's story.

In the game, Shanhai 9000 is located broken in the Four Seasons Pavilion and, after repairing it for 100 Jin, is capable of extracting data about resources on New Kunlun from Map Data Chips of the Shanhai robots in other zones, either by buying a copy for 400 Jin or taking the chips by force from them. It immediatly unlocks data for the New Kunlun central zone.

Shanhai 9000 also appears as one of the main characters in the Shan-Hai Archive manga and one of the main characters of the Project Daybreak ARG.

## Appearance

Shanhai 9000 appears as a short humanoid robot with a large round fish tank-like head. Its outfit includes a hat, a coat with loose sleeves and a skirt with knotted ties at the front, consisting of blue, light blue, pink and lavender colors. Its design is meant to remind a butler, based on a palace maid from the feudal era, wearing a style of Hanfu. Possibly, a dachang (大氅) or a yichang (衣裳) attire. Its chest features an indelible graffiti mark drawn by Chien. It is an older refurbished model of the Shanhai 9000 differing from the newer made standard New Kunlun models. It's rear screen is usually black or shows static, but sometimes reveals its secret activities.

### Game Events

Shanhai 9000 is encountered broken at the entrance room of the Four Seasons Pavilion and can be repaired for **100 Jin**.

After being repaired and rebooted, Yi realized how strange it is for a broken drawn-on robot to be in the Pavilion and threatens it to identify itself. Shanhai 9000 assures that it won't hurt him and offers a service to Yi: in each zone of New Kunlun there is a Shanhai 9000 with the corresponding Map Data Chip of that zone; if given to it, it will hack and analyze the data, locating any resources in the area for him. Yi stilled his suspicions, but does not overlook how hastily and proactively Shanhai 9000 offers its help, which is very weird for an assistive model. He still decides to cooperate, since knowing key data about locations on New Kunlun will prove highly beneficial.

## Trivia

- The name of **Shanhai 9000 (山海9000)** comes from the Classic of Mountains and Seas (山海經), also known as Shanhai Jing.
- Shanhai 9000 is shown multiple times to travel freely, but the standard New Kunlun Shanhai 9000 models and the prototype Shanhai 1000 are only ever seen stationary, even Factory Shanhai remains stationary in combat mode.
- Wen An Chia (賈文安) is the Taiwanese voice actor of Shanhai robots. Famous characters voiced by him include: Rui (Demon Slayer/Kimetsu No Yaiba), Senku Ishigami (Dr.STONE), Fujimaru Ritsuka (Fate/Grand Order), Woo Jinchul and Yoo Jinho (Solo Leveling).
