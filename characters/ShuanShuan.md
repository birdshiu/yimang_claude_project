Shuanshuan is one of the main characters in Nine Sols. He is a resident of Peach Blossom Village

# Appearance
Shuanshuan is a small human boy, with olive skin and short, shaggy white hair that covers his eyes and has a cowlick on the top of his head.

# Story
In the beginning of the game, Yi is awoken from his 500-year-long dormant state by Shuanshuan playing the flute. The boy ran to the forbidden cave for the first time after his parents' departure at the ceremony, without ever expecting to see someone to "pop out from the stone". Over the next two years, he and Yi take care of one another as Yi regains his strength. They await the next harvest ceremony.

As the day of the ceremony arrives, Shuanshuan gets anxious. Yi tries to talk him out of participating, but Shuanshuan says that he cannot and that it is a blessing to be chosen by the Sols.

During the night of the ceremony, Yi joins the crowd and watches as the three chosen are harvested, Shuanshuan being the third. As he walks up, he thanks Yi for taking care of him and telling that it's time for him to go. As he is lifted into the air by the machine, Yi jumps up and blows it up using a Talisman, saving Shuanshuan's life. Shuanshuan is left confused by this action, as Yi departs without explaining himself, only saying that he's not who Shuanshuan thinks him to be.

Later, when Yi reaches the Four Seasons Pavilion, Shuanshuan manages to catch up to him, where Yi allows him to take up residence for the time being and appoints Abacus to watch over him while he's gone.

Over the course of the game, Yi can give Shuanshuan items, which serve to deepen his understanding of the Solarian world and teach Shuanshuan about their culture. Creating a closer bond between Yi and Shuanshuan by giving him artifacts and interacting with him in cutscenes is required to complete the Apemen Questline and is one of requirements towards the True Ending(Shooting Star ending).

In the True Ending(Shooting Star ending), Shuanshuan is left to be a pioneer of the scientific knowledge for humanity as they return to earth. In the Normal Ending(Home, Sweet Home ending), it is unknown what happens to him beyond becoming Yi's assistant.
