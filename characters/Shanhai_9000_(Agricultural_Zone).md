**Greenhouse (or Agricultural) Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive Shanhai robot model found in the Greenhouse, location of the Agricultural Zone administrated by Sol Goumang. It provides Agricultural Zone Chip.

## Description

Similarly to other standard New Kunlun Shanhai, Greenhouse Shanhai appears as a short humanoid robot with large spherical head made of a glass-like material, reminding an aquarium, and has body in light blue, pink and yellow color scheme.

Its spherical screen projects a purple pufferfish.

It has tsundere personality.
