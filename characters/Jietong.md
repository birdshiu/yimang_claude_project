Jietong was the last king of the Jie Kingdom and an ancestor of Jiequan. He is responsible for developing advanced transmutation technology, Jiangshi Soldiers, and the Battlelords Xingtian and Chiyou.

# Appearance
Jietong is a tall, muscular solarian with a red skin, black claws, and a long white mane and beard. He is clad in green armor with pale golden accents, black pants, and a worn red cape.

The in-game artistic depiction of him has him holding a weapon resembling a Guandao in his right hand, and a model of the Haotian Sphere in his left.

# Story
Long before the Turbulent Era,Jietong was born to the Imperial Jie Clan, the largest nation on Penglai. At that time, the region was a desolate land scarce of any industry besides mining. Forced to live under ridicule by its neighboring kingdoms, the Jie Empire built up their culture and beliefs around their miserable background, with the creed being, "Endure what others can't, for heroes are forged in agony."

Jietong was not only an ambitious industrialist, he was also a ruthless and efficient general and an outstanding calligrapher. With the high demand for Jin and Azure Sand, this gave the Jie Kingdom resources to mass-produce weapons systems, develop advanced transmutation technology, and revolutionize warfare. Seizing the opportunity to enact revenge on their neighbors, Jietong rallied his forces to unite all kingdoms of Penglai under his Kingdom by force. During his conquests, Jietong would casually rely on overwhelming the enemy defenses with sheer numbers, showing how little he cares for the lives of his troops. Anyone who refused to comply with his orders were then subsequently beheaded on the spot.

To further the Jie Kingdom’s power, Jietong launched several projects such as innovation of Jiangshi Soldiers for their military campaigns, where many civilians were drafted into the program. Brains of Jiangshi are modified, removing any self-consciousness, and pre-programmed talismans helping to reduce the need for training. They do not feel pain, cannot feel fear, and will never desert. Such use of the technology sparked a powerful public backlash.

Jietong's rule saw the the pioneering of many experimental weapons, such the construction of large Rhizomatic towers to house the ever-expanding need for energy. Other inventions and discoveries included custom-made defensive swords, the Sky Rending Claw, and three veins of extremely valuable Dark Steel – nearly indestructible metal — in the Tianshan Mountains (speculated to have aided the military as well, but the time period of finding is unknown).

But the most aspiring plan ever created was the Haotioan Sphere. Initially a scientific satellite for extraterrestrial research, Jietong planned to build a weaponized station spanning 9000 Li, with three extreme-range rhizomatic cannons powered by countless rhizomatic columns, capable of razing any nation on Penglai's surface. Led by Liwen and Lijing, many outstanding craftsmen,scientists, architects, and astrologers were commissioned to build it, many hailing from the provinces of Ying and Xia.

The early established Fangshi Guild led by Lear and the Three Great Sages: Yin Jifu, Luyan, and Guiguzi.

In the late Turbulent Era, when Jiangshi Soldier technology efficiency started to decrease, he initiated the Battlelord Project which used highly developed transmutation technology to fuse Solarians with animal genes, combined with mechanical skeletons to turn them into war machines. However, they lacked independent cognition and often went berserk, which led to the project's termination.

Near the end of the Turbulent Era, when it was clear that the Jie Kingdom would not give up arms, Luyan led Martial Division of the Fangshi Guild, counting hundred warriors to take control over the Haotian Sphere. The battle resulted in tens of thousands of casualties for the Jie and the destruction of the satellite.

To force Jie Kingdom to surrender, the Fangshi Guild had invented the Rhizomatic Bomb, capable of razing an entire island and far surpassing any technology or weapons the Jie Kingdom had on-hand. As Lear's forces advance on his palace, Jietong wrote the Parting Missive, pouring every ounce of his sorrow and indignation into every brilliant brush stroke. Once he finished writing the missive, Jietong took his own life in front of his seer, Ji.

Ten years after Fangshi Guild's formation[2], they achieved victory over the Jie Kingdom, marking the end to the Turbulent Era and start to a peaceful time of the Origin Era. Jiangshi Soldier technology was banned, and both the Battlelord and the Haotian Sphere Projects were terminated. In the following decades, Jietong was condemned a tyrant by public and Jie family was countlessly humiliated and forced into hiding.

Later, Lear made the decision to disband the Guild with his Inaction Declaration, and all military innovations made by the Fangshi Guild were banned as well, such as extremely lethal Thunderburst Bombs and especially Rhizomatic Bomb which left an eternal stain on the Fangshi Guild; at the same time, any developed technology beneficial to all solarians was made common property for all to use. All Fangshi were collectively to go into hiding, returning to nature and finding true meaning of Tao.
