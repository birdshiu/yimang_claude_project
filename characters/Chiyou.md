An ancient genetically modified weapon that developed self-awareness for unknown reasons. A self-titled scholar, it has wandered through New Kunlun for many years, possessing a variety of goods with obscure origins. **Chiyou** (traditional Chinese: 蚩尤; pinyin: *Chīyóu*) is a side character who acts as a travelling merchant. Chiyou is a non-violent and self-titled scholar. Having wandered through New Kunlun for many years, he possesses a variety of goods with obscure origins.

## Description

Chiyou is a humanoid creature with turquoise and red skin, horns, a single eye, and thin lips that leave his teeth exposed. He often wears a yellow robe and a metal backpack, which has an attached transparent panel shading his head.

Despite his nature as a genetically modified weapon, Chiyou is nonviolent and has a flowery style of speech using a large vocabulary. He displays interest in both written and visual arts.

Chiyou may appear in many locations throughout the game as it progresses.

## Story

Chiyou was originally constructed by the Jie Kingdom. In the late Turbulent Era, as Jiangshi Soldier efficiency began to decline, the Jie Kingdom initiated the Battlelord Project. Using genetic transmutation, they fused solarians with material from Taotie beasts and combined them with mechanical skeletons to augment their destructive power as war machines. However, these creations lacked independent cognition and often went berserk.

The project was eventually terminated at the end of the war, after which Chiyou and his brother Xingtian were kept in the Jie family's vault. After reading the Parting Missive for many years, Chiyou abruptly gained sentience and the ability to retain memories. At some point, he escaped from storage and began wandering New Kunlun, collecting a variety of treasures.

When Yi encounters Chiyou during the game, Chiyou offers to trade his treasures to Yi in exchange for Jin, as it is a material needed to repair himself. Chiyou eventually locates his brother Xingtian in the Warehouse Zone. Yi gets there first, but is forced by Yanlao to battle the non-sentient Xingtian, resulting in the destruction of Xingtian's head. With Yi's help, Chiyou is able to recover his brother.

Chiyou considers sapience as the criterion of life, and pities the many guards and other creatures in New Kunlun that cannot think for themselves. He believes that non-existence is better than enslavement in a non-sapient condition. Since Xingtian appears unable to gain consciousness, Chiyou wishes to destroy him and requests materials from Yi to arm himself in battle against his brother.

At the conclusion of Chiyou's story, he is found in the Tiandao Research Center having been unsuccessful in battle against Xingtian, and succumbs to his injuries after a final conversation with Yi. Honoring Chiyou's dying wish, Yi once again faces Xingtian in battle, this time destroying him and thereby bringing him peace.

An alternative ending to Chiyou's story occurs if his questline is not fulfilled. In this case, Chiyou never attempts battle against Xingtian due to lacking suitable weapons. Instead, Yi finds Chiyou dead as a casualty of Eigong's attack on the Four Seasons Pavilion, having apparently refused to defend himself from the attackers.

## Gameplay

Chiyou is usually found sitting peacefully in various locations. Once he is found in a new place, he cannot be found again at an older location. Upon interacting with him, he offers powerful items for sale such as Poisons, Computing Units, and Jades. After Yi escapes from the Prison, Chiyou settles down in the Four Seasons Pavilion and adds additional items to his shop.

After the Point of No Return, if some of Chiyou's items have not yet been bought, his backpack persists in the Four Seasons Pavilion despite the absence of Chiyou himself. The backpack can still be used as a shop, with no change to the available items or prices.


### Gifts

Chiyou will provide certain items for free if Yi completes small side quests or gives him some items:

- The Harness Force Jade and, optionally, 1 Basic Component in exchange for Shuanshuan's Portrait of Yi
- The Revival Jade in exchange for activating the bridge from the Outer Warehouse to the Factory (Great Hall)
- The Iron Skin Jade in exchange for The Legend of the Porky Heroes

### Quest Conditions

The conclusion of Chiyou's plot line is unlocked by giving him the Firestorm Ring, and can be accessed in the top left of the Tiandao Research Center after the Point of No Return.

- Upon buying more than 70% of his items (which equals approximately all his stock before Prison escape, he will ask Yi for the Soul-Severing Blade, which can then be given to him.
- Upon buying all his items, he will further ask Yi for the Firestorm Ring, which can then be given to him.


## Trivia

- **Chiyou (蚩尤)** is a legendary figure in Chinese mythology, often depicted as a powerful warrior and tribal leader of the Nine Li tribe (九黎) in ancient China. Chiyou is famously remembered for his battles against the Yellow Emperor. According to legend, Chiyou possessed supernatural powers and could control the weather and summon thick fog to confuse his enemies during battles. He is also credited with inventing various military technologies, such as weapons and armor. According to this legend, Chiyou had a bronze head with a distinct metal forehead. He had four eyes and six arms, wielding terrible sharp weapons in every hand, similar to a description of fangxiangshi. In some sources, Chiyou had certain features associated with various mythological bovines: his head was that of a bull with two horns, although the body was human, and his hindquarters were those of a bear.
