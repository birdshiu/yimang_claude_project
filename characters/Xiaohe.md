**Xiaohe** (traditional Chinese: 小何; pinyin: *Xiǎo Hé*) was a member of the Soulscape research team and lover of Lady Ethereal.

## Appearance

Xiaohe is a short, slim Solarian with tan fur (stylised as white in Soulscape memories) and distinctive long eyelashes. Her fur is longer behind her head and resembled a bob hairstyle. She is the rightmost bathing solarian with a towel around her head in the hot spring in the Dream. In Lady Ethereal's soulscape memories, she wears a uniform bearing the Tiandao Council sigil, similar to the rest of the research team.

## Story

Xiaohe was Lady Ethereal's lover and a member of the research team tasked by the Tiandao Council with developing the Soulscape project. She had faith in Lady Ethereal's designs and acted as a test subject for the Soulscape system over a period of 30 days, which drove her insane upon emerging from hibernation. While their colleagues either committed suicide, acts of self-harm or experienced mental breakdowns, a weeping Xiaohe ended up strangling Lady Ethereal. Some time after this incident, Xiaohe died.

## Trivia

- She is referred to as Xiaohe by Lady Ethereal. "小 (Xiao)" is a term of endearment/close relationship, similar to ah-nong. "何 (He)" could be one of the characters taken from her full name.
- The only other character of their scientist group, who is mentioned, is "uncle Cao", but it isn't specified who exactly that is.
- Xiaohe's character could possibly be a reference to He Xiangu (born He Qiong). She is a Chinese mythological figure and one of the Eight Immortals in the Taoist pantheon. She is often seen as the only female among the Eight Immortals.
