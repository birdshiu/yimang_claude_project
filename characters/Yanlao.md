**Yanlao** (traditional Chinese: 奄老; pinyin: *Yǎn Lǎo*) is one of the Sols of the Tiandao Council. He holds the Sol Seal of Legacy.

## Appearance

Yanlao is a red-skinned solarian with one of the smallest statures in the game. He has large yellow eyes with moles under his left eye, a yellow nose, and a long, droopy white mustache. He is the oldest character in appearance, and wears a greenish-yellow and black robe with beige hems and a high collar that surrounds the sides and back of his head. He also wears a hanfu scholar's hat with his Sol Seal imprinted on the front. A device known as the Eternal Garment sits below Yanlao's hat and wraps around the back of his head, with a metallic tube connecting it to the hat. Hidden beneath Yanlao's sleeves is a device known as the Vital Injector, which is intravenously connected to the elder's body. The hat, Eternal Garment, and Vital Injector together serve as life-support apparatuses that ensure Yanlao's survival outside of his Soulscape through continuous administration of elixirs, as Yanlao would otherwise be unable to survive due to his late-stage Tianhuo infection.

To move around, Yanlao employs a large, flying fuchen (dusting whisk).

## Story

### Early Life

Yanlao was born in the Yan Kingdom. Yanlao's family guarded a horde of treasures left behind by their ancestors. His father would often admonish him and tell his son that they couldn't lose a single scrap of the treasure. In his youth, Yanlao saw little to no value in this guarding, considering the treasures to be no more than junk that nobody cared about. However, as he grew older, he gradually recognized and even started venerating the teachings of his predecessors, eventually joining them in their commitment to maintain those traditions. This steadfastness earned him recognition from Eigong. who considered the knowledge he possessed to be a treasure to all solarians.

### Life as a Sol

At some point Yanlao was invited to become a Sol of the Tiandao Council, being bestowed a Sol Seal signifying Legacy.

Yanlao was present for the two council meetings where Yi proposed the Eternal Cauldron Project. He expressed skepticism and shock at the incredulous nature of the project. Yanlao was eventually assigned responsibility over the Warehouse Zone of New Kunlun, a zone that was originally designed to house energy generators that could power Soulscapes.

Some time during the Tianhuo pandemic, Eigong apparently informed Yanlao, along with the rest of the Sols besides Yi, that she was responsible for the creation of the Tianhuo. Yanlao chose to continue cooperating with Eigong, concealing the origin of the Tianhuo from Yi.

Some time prior to New Kunlun's launch, Yanlao paid respects to his father by burning incense for him at an offering table bearing his father's portrait. Yanlao would later bring this offering table along with him aboard New Kunlun, storing it in his chamber.

As the Sol responsible for the Warehouse Zone, Yanlao commanded workers to handle management of the Hexacrem vault, a vault used to store treasures. Yanlao and his workers received and stored treasures such as the Jie family's Battlelord Prototype Storage and the Feng Clan's belongings.

### Game Events

Yanlao is awoken by Yi's activity on New Kunlun. Upon Yi's entry into the Warehouse Zone, Yanlao speaks to him through projector screens present in the zone. Yanlao attempts to dissuade Yi from enacting revenge by lecturing him, stating that although his anger is justified, his behavior will only cause destruction. Yi angrily rebukes the elder's words, considering it to be a joke that murderers wanted to lecture him about forgiveness, and demands that Yanlao hand over his Sol Seal. Yanlao refuses, ending the conversation. Although he doesn't speak to Yi any further, Yanlao can be seen monitoring Yi via screens present throughout the area.

Yi encounters Yanlao for the first time in person at the Boundless Repository, coming across Yanlao as he's attempting to activate Xingtian. Yanlao stalls for time by rambling to Yi; this proves to be a success, as he manages to activate Xingtian and trap Yi with the ancient weapon. This proves to only be a temporary roadblock, as Yi beheads Xingtian and is able to continue pursuing Yanlao. Yanlao panics upon discovering the destruction of Xingtian, as the ancient weapon belongs to the Jie family and is considered priceless, and worries that an investigation into the destruction of the ancient weapon will be the end of him. Refusing to sit and wait to die, Yanlao starts operating the Sky Rending System to try and remotely kill Yi with volleys of shots. Despite his fervent efforts, Yi evades death and manages to enter the room before Yangu Hall, where the Sky Rending System and Yanlao's Vital Sanctum are located.

Yanlao makes one final attempt to dissuade Yi from enacting revenge, claiming that Yi has a bright and promising future ahead of him and that the elder will spare his life if he stops. Yi refuses again, causing Yanlao to lash out in anger, as he considers Yi's actions to be threatening to his guarding of the treasures stored within the Hexacrem vault. Yi insults Yanlao's vault, calling it a "useless, power-hungry warehouse" that could have been used to house energy generators - energy generators which could have been used to house more solarians in New Kunlun. Yi further disparages Yanlao by claiming that most of Yanlao's supposed treasures are just things that belong to the rich and powerful, and that Yanlao allowed these private possessions aboard New Kunlun as a way to secure himself a ticket on the island. Concluding his tirade, Yi announces to Yanlao that "it's time to reform and to sweep away the corrupt", signaling his continued intent to defeat Yanlao and take his seal.

With no other options left, as his health is rapidly deteriorating from being outside of his Soulscape, Yanlao is forced to take control of the Sky Rending Claw in a last-ditch effort to defeat Yi. The process of taking control of the Sky Rending Claw causes Yanlao to bleed from his eyes. Despite his best efforts, Yanlao fails to defeat Yi with the Sky Rending Claw. Yi manages to destroy the Sky Rending System and hack into the Sky Rending Claw, using the claw to blast a hole into the the room where Yanlao is located. After Yi enters the room, Yanlao coughs and tells Yi that he doesn't want to die, before collapsing and dying from the Tianhuo virus. Upon his death, a fungal flower bearing a Greater Tao Fruit immediately erupts from his corpse.

### Chamber

Yanlao's Vital Sanctum chamber inside Yangu Hall, reflects his beliefs about tradition and legacy. It houses various relics from bygone eras, such as ancient scrolls, ceramics, and precious antique vinyl records. Each of these relics carry a long history, thus serving dual purposes for Yanlao: they serve as collectibles to be admired and preserved, but also serve as a means for Yanlao to connect to his late father's teachings. The centerpiece of the chamber is an offering table that bears Yanlao's father's portrait, which is used to honor those who have passed.

## Boss

**Yanlao** is the Sol boss of the Warehouse Zone. He holds the Sol Seal of Legacy. He connects to and operates the **Sky Rending Claw** during his bossfight.

### Combat Appearance

#### Yanlao

Yanlao eyes bleed when connecting his brainwave to Sky Rending Claw. In battle his eyes turn yellow.

#### Sky Rending Claw

The Sky Rending Claw is a large, metal (jade colored), three pronged claw that is powered by rhizomatic energy and can be linked with user’s brainwaves. A red band glows on the top along with an engraved, cylindric core in its middle. Three smaller prongs are directly below the core.

### Combat

Sky Rending Claw can't be staggered from attacks such as Cloud Piercer Arrows or Enhanced Full Control, unlike most other enemies.

#### Arena

A simple room with laser traps emerging from top and climbable walls moving in from sides of the arena during the fight. It's displayed as being overseen by Yanlao from his soulscape room behind the right wall.

- At the start sequence of both of those attacks, 2 vertical lasers appear at the sides of the arena that last for seconds or number of attacks before turning off with attacks continuing.
- Floats to the center of the arena and turns on 4 vertical lasers across the arena, which move from side to side.
- Floats to the top center of the arena and begins charging big crimson laser attack.
  - Two green climbable walls move out to quarter of arena from both sides of the room.
  - Takes aim with 3 volleys of 4 sniper shots before full charge.
  - If the Claw is hit by a sniper shot, it will get stunned and fall to the ground. Sniper Shot deals 10 damage to Claw, unlike 60 damage that are dealt to Yi.
  - If the Charge is done, the Claw will release a flailing crimson laser attack across the arena *3-4 times?*. Can be completely avoided by staying in the left edge corner of the arena.

  - At the start sequence of Claw swings and Crimson grab, additionally to 2 lasers at the sides a new laser appears in the center of the arena and moves from one side to the other. Lasers disappear when middle laser goes from one side to the other.

## Trivia

### Gameplay

- Sky Rending Claw's Crimson grab attack can't be Unbounded Countered as well as the laser traps, but laser shot from the Claw can be.

### Lore

- Yanlao's name (奄老) consists of:
  - Yan (奄) can be interpreted as reference to proverb *奄奄一息* (yan yan yi xi) at one's last gasp, on one's deathbed, on the point of dying, on the verge of death, within an inch of life, at the gate of death, moribund. It also coincides with Yanlao's home kingdom - Yan.
  - Lao (老) can be interpreted as aged, experienced, elder. It is used as prefix before the surname of an elder person, a numeral indicating the order of birth of the children in a family or to indicate affection or familiarity.
- Yanlao's appearance reminiscent of an Exotic Shorthair cat.
- The hat Yan Lao wears is inspired by the [Zi Bu Guan](wikipedia:Guan_(headwear) "wikilink"), traditionally donned during rituals or coming-of-age ceremonies in ancient times. Incorporating this element into the game highlights Yan Lao's refined, elder persona.
- In Taoist culture, a fuchen is used in rituals to symbolize purity and the dispelling of worries. While Yan Lao wields it to project a sense of calm and wisdom, the irony lies in how his true thoughts and actions contradict this serene facade.
- The "sniper" that Yanlao uses in the chase in Boundless Repository is actually called howitzer in solarian (h) on the control screen during the scene where Yanlao initiates it (it's also called howitzer many times internally).
- The "Sky Rending Claw" and its remote control base—collectively called the "Sky Rending System"—trace their origins to an ancient Penglai Turbulent Era engineering apparatus. Initially employed for large-scale gold mining, they were later repurposed for sappers conducting remote operations during wartime. By linking directly to the operator's neural network, they enable trench excavation, fortification construction, and even direct combat—showcasing remarkable versatility. Yan Lao employs these ancient weapons from his warehouse to avoid direct confrontation with Yi, reflecting his timid nature.
- When designing the Sky Rending Claw, one source of inspiration came from Taiwan's ubiquitous claw machines, lending a distinctive flair to its overall style.
- **Chung Tai Sun (孫中台)** is Taiwanese voice actor for Yanlao. Other famous characters voiced by him include:
  - Tachibana Jin (Atashin'chi)
  - Hantengu (Demon Slayer / Kimetsu No Yaiba)
  - Sanji (ONE PIECE)
  - Professor Oak and Narration (Pokémon TV anime series)
