**Chien** (traditional Chinese: 小倩; pinyin: *Xiǎo Qiàn*) is a solarian appearing as side-character in the game and as one of the main characters in the Shanhai 9000 Prequel Manga.

## Appearance

### Child

Chien has black medium-length fur and round head. Her eyes are white, her pupil and iris are only defined by rows of lines drawn from the center of the eyes. She wears a jade token, gifted from her mother, on her neck.

### Adult

Adult Chien has a shorter fur and her eyes are light-gray, with defined circle lines. She wears a standard Dusk Guardian suit: red and black dress, white headdress and gloves. There is an ornament on her head with slightly glowing jade-colored token inside. Her arm muscles are exposed, with braces on her shoulders.

## Story

### Childhood

Chien was born to the Huang family , possessing an illness that caused her sight to rapidly deteriorate as she aged. Despite the debilitating disability, Chien grew up into a polite, affectionate, and innocent child.

To help Chien navigate society, Chien's mother bought her an assistive Shanhai 9000 that was supposed to help her by chatting with her. Chien developed a deep bond with her Shanhai 9000, as it served as both caretaker and playmate to her. However, this Shanhai 9000 had a defect: it lacked the sound function necessary to assist Chien with her failing eyesight. Chien's mother tried to have it replaced, but Chien refused to allow her to do so. One night, to prevent her mother from returning the Shanhai 9000, Chien drew a cartoon character on its chest.

Shortly before the creation and launch of New Kunlun, Chien's mother bore another daughter, making Chien an older sister.

During the Tianhuo pandemic, Chien's family was assigned two seats aboard New Kunlun. Chien's mother was forced to decide which members of the family would be allowed to board New Kunlun. Because of Chien's disability, Chien's mother chose to abandon her, choosing to board New Kunlun with Chien's sister. Before departing from their home, Chien's mother bid farewell to Chien, leaving behind a jade token for her. To prolong Chien finding out that she had been abandoned, Chien's mother made phone calls instructing Chien to remain at home, telling her that she would be returned for after getting Chien's sister onto New Kunlun.

While at home alone, Chien encountered a group of bandits breaking into her home, seeking food and first aid supplies. The bandits discovered Chien's Shanhai 9000, remarking that it was worth a lot of money due to scarcity. One bandit attempted to activate Shanhai 9000, deeming it defective after it failed to follow commands and speak. This quickly changed, after one of the bandits hurt Chien by pulling on her ear; Shanhai 9000 immediately attacked the bandit that hurt Chien, causing them to flee from the home. After repelling the bandits, Shanhai 9000 copied Chien's mother's voice, allowing it to trick Chien into believing that it was her mother. Shanhai 9000 then put on a disguise, setting off with Chien toward New Kunlun.

After five hours of travelling, Chien became tired, telling Shanhai 9000 that she was tired, cold, and hungry. Hearing Chien's words, a friendly old man named Old Chang stopped the pair, inviting them to his home to rest for the night. Old Chang provided them food and drink, as well as instructions on how to reach New Kunlun for their journey ahead. Additionally, he provided them with the contact information of a relative working on New Kunlun, who could possibly help them board the island. Chien asked Shanhai 9000 if they could take Old Chang with them to New Kunlun, but Old Chang politely declined, expressing his wishes to die on Penglai and merge with the Tao, and his acceptance of his fate to come.

The following morning, Shanhai 9000 discovered that Old Chang had died during the night to the Tianhuo virus, leaving behind some fruit and a lunch box for Chien. Shanhai 9000 buried Old Chang's body, adorning his grave with the disguise it had been wearing. When Chien asked where Old Chang had gone, Shanhai 9000 told her that he was sleeping.

Chien and Shanhai 9000 eventually reached one of New Kunlun's piers, but were stopped by an employee informing them that passengers without a travel pass had to wait at the end of the queue. While attempting to navigate the situation, the pair were almost crashed into by a palanquin carrying a VIP passenger, revealed to be Goumang. Taking pity upon Chien, Goumang fed Chien an optoberry and allowed her and Shanhai 9000 to board the palanquin and ride closer to New Kunlun. Deducing that they were trying to illegally board New Kunlun, Goumang instructed her servant to dispatch someone to monitor the pair.

Following Old Chang's instructions, Shanhai 9000 located Old Chang's relative, a man named A-Biao. A-Biao told them that since they were sent by his relative, he was willing to try and help them board the island, but demanded that they provide him payment due to the difficulty of the task. Shanhai 9000 offered him an orange as payment, angering A-Biao and causing him to revoke his offer of help. A-Biao started to insult the pair, telling them that it was hard enough for him - an able-bodied worker - to secure a seat on New Kunlun, and that there would be even less of a chance for a crippled little blind girl to do so. He cruelly remarked to Chien that she should stop dreaming, called her a blind defect, and told her to "stop taking up resources meant for normal people".

In response to A-Biao's heartless words, Shanhai 9000 punched him in the face, knocking him out. A nearby guard immediately noticed the sudden commotion and came over to investigate. To avoid trouble, Shanhai 9000 quickly stole A-Biao's work permit and picked up Chien, entering New Kunlun using the clearance provided by the permit. Upon entering the island, they quickly found Chien's mother, who was shocked that they had somehow managed to reach the island.

Chien rejoiced in finally reaching New Kunlun. Tragically, this joy proved short-lived, as her mother immediately betrayed her by pushing her down a flight of stairs to prevent her from interfering with the boarding process, as they hadn't boarded yet. Enraged by the betrayal of Chien's love and trust, Shanhai 9000 attempted to attack Chien's mother, but was quickly intercepted: the person dispatched by Goumang to investigate Chien and Shanhai 9000 immediately identified Shanhai 9000 as the one who had previously bothered Goumang, and had Shanhai 9000 shot in the head. Following this, Chien and Shanhai 9000 were separated, as workers collected Shanhai 9000 to recycle its parts.

### Adulthood

Somehow by fate, Chien was saved by the Sols and remained on New Kunlun. Chien matured into a serious, disciplined adult, losing her energetic and naive disposition, and eventually became a Dusk Guardian.

After joining, she endured extensive transmutation to make her body fit for operations, even partially recovering her sight. Additionally, as a part of her Dusk Guardian duties, Chien was given operation-specific gear: this gear included gloves and masks, as their daily tasks included the cleaning and maintenance of equipment and systems on New Kunlun. Chien was also given a tactical light blade, a weapon reserved only for high-ranking operatives.

For a long time Chien, like her colleagues, believed Eigong's fabrications and promises about the Dusk Guardians: that being a Dusk Guardian was noble work to help solarians and that after enough work, Dusk Guardians would eventually be allowed to permanently retire to Soulscapes to rest. However, at some point, Chien discovered the truth about the Dusk Guardians: that Eigong had formed the organization solely as a means to have a group of solarians that she could use as test subjects for her experiments. Having discovered this truth, Chien wrote a **Personal Diary** to share the truth behind the Dusk Guardians, and vowed in this diary to save everyone.

#### Personal Diary

Chien's personal diary is found after Point of No Return in the bottom right of the Tiandao Research Center, under the hallway to the elevator.

> It is a noble job being a Dusk Guardian.
> Our eyes are always open, and we never sleep.
> It is through our sacrifice, that you can all have a good night's sleep. We take care of the night.
> I know there's nothing we can do about it... But it's time everyone learned the truth.
> There is no elixir to slow down the disease. The Ascension Palace was a total fiction.
> The Sols... Eigong... Everything they promised was a lie.
> We're just a bunch of cheap lab rats.
> I mean... At the end of the day, we never deserved to board the island...
> I remember... Mom... Shanhai 9000...
> Wait for me...
> I will save everyone... Every last one of us.

Tragically, this never came to be, as she was injected with Tianhuo Serum and was subsequently imprisoned within an experimental pod of the Tiandao Research Center along with most other Dusk Guardians.

### Present

Chien, transformed into a tianhuo mutant, can be found banging on the pod, earliest from any other mutant pods, at the bottom left of the Tiandao Research Center.

After completing Shanhai 9000 quest requirements and helping it gather map info about New Kunlun, Shanhai 9000 finds Chien in an experimental pod in the bottom left corner of the Tiandao Research Center. It then goes for her after Point of No Return.

Yi warns Shanhai 9000 to not mess with the experimental pod. Shanhai 9000 informs Yi that Chien's coordinates are inside the experimental pod. Yi determines that Chien is a Dusk Guardian, and deduces that Chien has likely undergone Tianhuo mutation as Dusk Guardians were Eigong's test subjects. Yi tells Shanhai 9000 to not be impulsive, but Shanhai responds that it *"Doesn't matter. Chien. Is. Chien. Alive. Or. Dead."* and asks Yi as a friend to not stop it. It unlocks the tank, allowing the Mutated Chien to burst out, knocking down and making a crack on Shanhai 9000's head, shutting it down. She then immediately targets Yi.

Defeating Mutated Chien makes her drop a worn out photo carefully hidden in her uniform. It shows young Chien hugging Shanhai 9000.

## Mutated Chien

**Mutated Chien** appears as a variation of Chaser Mutant.

### Mutant Appearance

Chien's body is covered in purple tumors. Her head is almost completely taken up by a large purple eye. Her arms mutated into large claws.

### Combat

She produces loud screams when alerted, and alternates between a slow shuffle and rapid running.

Attacks:

- Lunge
- Acid spit - can't be parried, but can be Unbounded Countered or evaded

## Trivia

- A bossfight was planned against Mutated Chien, but it was cut due to time restraints.
