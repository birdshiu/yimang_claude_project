**Kanghui** (traditional Chinese: 康回; pinyin: *Kānghuí*) is a solarian imprisoned by the Tiandao Council for offending the Fengs, who was found out to have a highly increased regeneration in prison during tortures. He is the boss encountered before exiting the Prison.

## Appearance

Kanghui is a Solarian trapped in a pillory. He has red skin, with his face and hands being thin to the bone. His ears poke out of a jade-colored headwear, a visor blocks his bloodied eyes and a metal gag device of the same color keeps his mouth open. He wears a golden ring with a purple gem on the middle finger of his right hand, it being the Noble Ring that the player obtains after his death.

## Story

Kanghui was a solarian coming from a prominent family, married to a daughter of a noble. Sometime after arriving on New Kunlun, he was imprisoned, according to him, after "having offended the Feng clan due to a trivial matter". In jail, he wrote a letter in a very humble matter, asking for help from his respectable elder. He explained the situation and hoped for his father-in-law to mediate his release over in Jie Kingdom (Chinese: Shang Kingdom), apologizing for disturbing him repeatedly during such troubling times. The letter was never sent.

After angering the Fengs and being captured by the Council's special forces, during his torture Jiequan was informed about his regeneration ability. Kanghui is stated to age very slowly, resisting Tianhuo Virus from advancing in his body.

Eigong considered Kanghui's "source of vitality", but ultimately moved to another source. It was later decided that his special regeneration gene, although apparently not useful to Eigong, would be used in technology for genetically-modified guards.

Kanghui remained imprisoned and was transmutated (as implied by the yellow liquid in banks near his pillory) into a monstrous being, his body used to spawn prison guards and his "regeneration gene", allegedly said to be provided by a generous donor, is used to produce guards who are able to withstand multiple rounds of transmutation.

When Yi got captured by Jiequan and restrained by an electric torture device, he also mentions that it hadn't seen use since the Kanghui affair.

After Yi eventually frees himself, he confronts Kanghui near the exit of the Prison. After breaking his gag device, Kanghui stops attacking and asks Yi to kill him, who complies and finally ends his suffering.

## Boss

### Combat

Although Kanghui has 1820 health, bringing it to half (910 HP) will open the nearby liquid banks hack points, progressing the fight. Both direct and internal damage count for this.

#### Arena

- Kanhgui is pilloried in the center with hack points on both of his sides.

#### Phase 1 attacks

Kanghui spawns enemies by spitting out levitating flesh mass, forming into various Prison Guards

- Spawns Monstrous Beast, then Monstrous Piercer
- Spawns 4 Monstrous Piercers in succession
- Heals unless the pillar is hacked

#### Phase 2 attacks

In addition to previous attacks:

- Warden spawns besides Kanghui at the start of the phase
- When Warden's hp is depleted, it doesn't die, instead losing its form and falling as flesh mass covered by the shield. If Warden is downed, Kanghui throws up blood that then at once extends into a giant mass of spikes, retracting back into the ground after a while
- After the Spike Wall retracts, flesh blob moves to Warden and resurrects it, meanwhile another blob spawns a Monstrous Piercer
- Heals unless the pillar is hacked

#### Phase 3 attacks

Attacks mostly stay the same:

- Resurrects Warden with 2 blobs of flesh instead, and spawns 2 Monstrous Piercers at both sides.

After both hack points are hacked and Kanghui's health is brought down to half for the third time, he will beg Yi to kill him and it’s possible to activate the execution sequence by interacting with the pillory, ending the fight.

## Trivia

### Gameplay

- The dropped execution axe actually deals damage only to Kanghui and deals 1820 damage, his whole health pool.

### Lore

- Gonggong (共工), also known as **Kanghui (康回)**, is a Chinese water deity from ancient mythology. Often depicted with a copper human head, red hair, and a serpent body, he's associated with destruction and disasters. According to legend, Gonggong's actions, such as striking Mount Buzhou in anger, resulted in cataclysmic shifts in the heavens and earth. In all interpretations, Gonggong ends up being sent into exile or killed by another major deity such as the fire god Zhurong.
- Both Yi and Kuafu know about Kanghui being imprisoned and his regeneration, despite only Fengs, Jiequan and Eigong dealing with him first-hand, which implies that he was discussed at one of the Tiandao Council meetings, and that all of the Sols knew the truth about him.
- It's not said if Kanghui was completely immune to the Tianhuo Virus, but there weren't any signs of fungal white grass or flower on his corpse, unlike Yi's corpse that grows both after his death.
- It is implied that Kanghui have been in pillory for 500 years, without the use of Vital Sanctum. It's unknown if his regeneration gene allowed such long life or the monstrous gene-modification done on him.
- There are implications that Kanghui was either born blind or had been blinded. Nine Sols artbook shows Kanghui, seemingly unrestrained, possibly before being captured and the torture visor was worn onto him, still wearing some cloth or metal-based eye visor. There are also unused sprites of Kanghui without the torture visor: one has his eyeline turned into a fleshy mass with countless small eyes and another sprite is drawn without eyes.
- The artbook refers to Kanghui as "Kang Hui the Sinner".
