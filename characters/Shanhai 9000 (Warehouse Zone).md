**Warehouse Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive Shanhai robot model found in the Outer Warehouse, location of the Warehouse Zone administrated by Sol Yanlao. It provides Warehouse Zone Chip.

## Description

Similarly to other standard New Kunlun Shanhai, Warehouse Shanhai appears as a short humanoid robot with large spherical head made of a glass-like material, reminding an aquarium, and has body in light blue, pink and yellow color scheme.

Its spherical screen projects a orange clam.

It has overly worried and cowardly personality.

## Trivia

- The name of **Shanhai 9000 (山海9000)** comes from the Classic of Mountains and Seas (山海經), also known as Shanhai Jing.
- Wen An Chia (賈文安) is the Taiwanese voice actor of Shanhai robots. Famous characters voiced by him include: Rui (Demon Slayer/Kimetsu No Yaiba), Senku Ishigami (Dr.STONE), Fujimaru Ritsuka (Fate/Grand Order), Woo Jinchul and Yoo Jinho (Solo Leveling).
