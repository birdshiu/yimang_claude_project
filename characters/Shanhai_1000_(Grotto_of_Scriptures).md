**Grotto Shanhai 1000** is the prototype Shanhai model found at the Ancient Stone Pillar, location of the Grotto of Scriptures administrated by Sol Ji. It provides Grotto of Scriptures Chip.

## Description

Shanhai 1000 is a humanoid robot with large spherical head made of a glass-like material, reminding an aquarium, with an ellipsoid part or cap wrapping around the top of the head. It appears shorter compared to modern models, has an open part at the chest and doesn't have legs, standing on a trapezoidal-shaped base. Its body is in blue and white color scheme.

Its spherical screen does not project a fish and only shows static and loading animations.

Shanhai 1000 is a testing model for the future Shanhai robots and had been developed by the Fangshi Guild. Ji was the one who wanted to put a real fish inside its head, but opted to a display due to a too high potential maintance cost. He also wanted to fill it with water to represent the seas and mountains of Penglai, but Lear insisted on keeping the features practical for mass-production. This implies that Shanhai 1000 may have had a fish projection once, but it stopped working after a millennium of years passed.

It has a very basic talking module and commands recognition compared to the modern 9000 models.
