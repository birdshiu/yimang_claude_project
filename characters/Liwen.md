Liwen was the mother of Lear, the Chief Architect of the Haotian Sphere, and wife of Lijing.

## Appearance

Liwen is a Solarian with cream-colored fur and long, straight hair of the same color. Her hairstyle includes two long strands that fall to her shoulders and a ponytail secured by a ring-like hair tie at the end. She wears small round glasses and has sharp black eyelashes, black eyes with pale red accents, and a light orange snout.

Her uniform consists of a greyish green blouse and pants beneath a white loincloth and a white tunic bearing the Jie Kingdom crest. Liwen is often seen using a floating mobility device similar to Kuafu's Hover Engineering Chair, featuring a flower-shaped base and cushioned half-sphere seat with an integrated arm-and-backrest rail.

## Story

Originally from the Xia Kingdom, Liwen and her husband, Lijing, were victims of the Jie Kingdom’s invasion. They were buried under the rubble of their capital during the attack, resulting in severe injuries. This incident caused Lijing to lose his jaw, and Liwen her legs.

While trapped, Liwen saw what she later described as the most beautiful starry sky of her life, that view led the couple to dedicate themselves to astronomy, although Liwen later realized the clarity was caused by the capital’s destruction and the darkening of countless homes.

Relocated by force to the Jie Kingdom, along with many other architects, Liwen led the development of the Haotian Sphere as its Chief Architect. She designed it with the intent of peaceful space research, but later discovered King Jietong had repurposed it into a massive planetary weapon, using its hollow interior to house Rhizomatic Cannons ranging the entirety of Penglai.

Upon learning of Lear’s growing involvement with the King and the militarization of their work, Liwen became more protective and anxious. After Lear's failed plea to Jietong to rescind the weaponization order, the Li family was summoned to the palace, at first, Liwen thought it was an important announcement, but quickly noticed something amiss.

Liwen and Lijing then initiated a plan, while Jietong was at the entrance of the Orbital Elevator, they sabotaged the Haotian Sphere, causing the Rhizomatic Cannons to fall and the structure to collapse. Lijing watched the event from a shore, while Liwen placed Lear in a boat while he was asleep, in an attempt to lead him to safety.

However, as she talked to the sleeping Lear, Lijing warned about a squad coming down the hill, he urged the sailor to take Lear to the sea, leaving the couple behind. Lear awoke at sea just in time to witness his parents' execution by Jietong, charged with treason.

## Trivia

- Liwen is a double amputee below the kneecaps, and as such is never seen walking and consistently uses a floating mobility device. It is implied that she lost her legs during the Jie Kingdom’s invasion of Xia, when she and her husband were trapped under the rubble.
- While Liwen’s mobility device shares a general resemblance to Kuafu’s Hover Engineering Chair, it features a distinctly more elaborate design. Her model includes a curved arm-and-backrest rail, as well as a flower-shaped base, both absent from Kuafu’s version.
  - The rail design, however, appears visually similar to his multi-purpose ear guards. These differences may suggest that such devices are customized to suit the specific needs or roles of their users.
