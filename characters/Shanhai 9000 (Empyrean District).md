**Empyrean Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive Shanhai robot model found in the [Empyrean District (Living Area)](Empyrean_District_(Living_Area) "wikilink"), location of the Empyrean District administrated by Sols Fuxi and Nuwa. It provides Empyrean District Chip.

## Description

Similarly to other standard New Kunlun Shanhai, Empyrean Shanhai appears as a short humanoid robot with large spherical head made of a glass-like material, reminding an aquarium, and has body in light blue, pink and yellow color scheme.

Its spherical screen projects a red fish, reminding ranchu, oranda or [lionhead](wp:Lionhead_(goldfish) "wikilink") species of goldfish, that share a bubble-like "hood" on their heads.

It has laid back, polite personality and served as the bartender at a bar at the Living Area.

## Trivia

- The name of **Shanhai 9000 (山海9000)** comes from the Classic of Mountains and Seas (山海經), also known as Shanhai Jing.
- Wen An Chia (賈文安) is the Taiwanese voice actor of Shanhai robots. Famous characters voiced by him include: Rui (Demon Slayer/Kimetsu No Yaiba), Senku Ishigami (Dr.STONE), Fujimaru Ritsuka (Fate/Grand Order), Woo Jinchul and Yoo Jinho (Solo Leveling).
