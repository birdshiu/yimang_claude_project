**Fengxian** (traditional Chinese: 鳳仙; pinyin: *Fèngxiān*) is a female solarian singer and composer from the Turbulent Era. She is only mentioned by other characters and not encountered personally.

She released the song *"Millennium Moment"* in year 2986 of the Turbulent Era during the Millennium of Inquiry - a time on Penglai when Solarians were trying to find a way to stop endless wars. It was used as a theme in bestselling movie "Grand Strategist's Day Off", one of the many beautiful films and shows in that era.

Yi finds an Antique Vinyl Record of her song in the Hexachrem Vault of the Boundless Repository and can gift it to Shuanshuan.
