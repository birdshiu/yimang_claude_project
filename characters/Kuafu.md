Kuafu (traditional Chinese: 夸伏; pinyin: *Kuāfú*) is one of main characters, Sols of the Tiandao Council and Yi's loyal friend. He helps Yi on his quest, modifying and upgrading his equipment.

## Appearance

Kuafu is a large solarian with orange fur. He has small, round eyes and a pudgy face.

He wears dark green robes under his green craftsman's work suit with a plate on his front and back. The suits are typically designed with pockets for tools on the front plate, but Kuafu turned the built-in pouch into beverage holder and container for bubble tea instead, as he believes constant sugar supply is more crucial than quick access to tools. The back plate is emblazoned with Kuafu's Sol Seal.

Light-green multi-purpose ear guards are resting around his head on the shoulders, serving both as engineering ear protection that reduces noise and as headphones during free time. Its design is inspired by Eastern scarves and shawls. The separate picture in the artbook seems to feature speakers (3 straight lines) at the back of the ear guards on each side near the curves, somewhere behind the ears.

He sits and moves on a hover engineering chair in green color tones with black plush cushions, featuring a hover mechanism, only barely seen as white-glowing panel or energy underneath. It is said to be very comfortable, allow Kuafu to move and adjust height effortlessly during work and let him handle most daily tasks without ever leaving his seat.

## Story

### Early Life

Kuafu was born in a small village called Baigong (traditional Chinese: 百工; lit.: Hundred Craftsman) in the Ying Kingdom. During the Turbulent Era, Ying Kingdom was famous for cultivating and producing many excellent craftsmen, but When Penglai National War ended and the beginning of the Origin Era came, Ying Kingdom was falling behind in the present because of the traditional skills of artisans and craftmans were ignored due to the development of science and technology. Kuafu hoped to revitalize his hometown by joining the Tiandao Council. On the day he left, he bid adieu to his friends and family, promising to return and help his hometown with everything he learned, and set off on his motorcycle to the Kunlun Island.

During Kuafu's time with the Tiandao Academy, Kuafu met Yi, a fellow scientist and Eigong's brilliant student. They quickly became friends with similar interests and ideals. Kuafu became one of the few friends that Yi valued in his life.

Kuafu gradually developed his skills by learning with his colleagues at the Academy, finally succeeding in repairing the power storing Rhizomating Columns on New Kunlun. He soon also developed engineering theories related to the rhizomatic energy systems, and by applying them, he designed the **Radiant Pagoda** that connects power storage columns, allowing to manage the energy and delivers the power across all the facilities. These theories later earned him the praise of Eigong; she appreciated his ability and recommended him as a member of the Ten Sols.

Although Kuafu felt burdened by taking on a responsible position, but Yi was encouraging Kuafu and requested that he remained with the Council until the peace has been restored and they could return home in a blaze of glory. Kuafu was deeply impressed and influenced by Yi's vow, Accordingly, Kuafu was vowing to achieve their goals together by supporting Yi's back. The bond between the two was close enough that they considered each other to be brothers.

### Life as a Sol

Kuafu was eventually chosen as a Sol of the Tiandao Council, and was granted his own Sol Seal with the tenet of Loyalty.

Kuafu was present during the two Council meetings during which Yi proposed the Eternal Cauldron Project, a project intended to delay the progress of the Tianhuo in solarians in order to buy time to find a cure. In the second meeting, Kuafu voiced his support of Yi's plans to turn the Kunlun island into an interstellar ark that could warp to the Pale Blue Planet, stating that he had done calculations and determined that his plans were possible. Based on his calculations, Kuafu determined that the Primordial Roots possessed enough energy to make the warp, and that it would only take a few years to build the rhizomatic energy columns and engines necessary to perform the warp.

As a part of the project, Kuafu made improvements to the rhizomatic column interconnections, linked up Kunlun's various power plants, and created the Power Reservoir - an entirely new energy storage paradigm. Controlled through the Radiant Pagoda, the Power Reservoir's infrastructure was designed to store energy for interstellar transportation needs and supply converted rhizomatic energy to all of New Kunlun. The Power Reservoir's administration was later assigned to Kuafu.

But Yi and Kuafu vow was disrupted. At some point before New Kunlun's departure from Penglai, Eigong informed each Sol of the truth behind the Tianhuo pandemic: the Tianhuo was accidentally synthesized by her. Like the rest of the Sols, Kuafu accepted this knowledge and continued to cooperate with Eigong, likely out of pragmatism, as Eigong was the only person capable of discovering a cure. As a part of this, Kuafu withheld the knowledge from Yi, whom Eigong wished to keep ignorant. Despite Eigong's attempts to prevent him from discovering the truth, Yi managed to find out anyways, resulting in a heated confrontation between the two. This confrontation led to Yi being pursued by the members of the Tiandao Council. While Kuafu thought there was a misunderstanding between Yi and the Council, he believed that the Tiandao Council pursued Yi with the intent to bring him back in order to sit down and talk, trying to persuade him and reinstate Yi's position.

But the situation ultimately escalated into tragedy: a fight broke between the student and the mentor, leading to Eigong fatally wounding Yi and driving him off to a cliff in the Livestock Zone. The surveillance footage captured Yi's body falling off the cliff into the chasm, seemingly confirming that the young Fangshi had died. Kuafu greatly despaired after the loss of his dear best friend, despite trying to keep stoic image.

After New Kunlun's launch, Kuafu served dutifully as a Sol, but remained haunted by the events surrounding Yi's death, constantly reliving the nightmarish day in his Soulscape. Although he wasn't directly involved in the events leading up to Yi's death, Kuafu came to blame himself, believing that had he intervened he may have been able to prevent Yi's supposed death; this, he considered to be his greatest sin.

Although the Council destroyed almost all of Yi's belongings, Kuafu helped preserve some of them. He convinced the Council that Yi's personal assistant and artificial intelligence system, the Abacus - Ruyi, was still useful, and successfully negotiated that Ruyi's systems instead just be throttled down. Additionally, Heng asked someone to deliver Azure Bow, a Fangshi ranged weapon and their familial artifact, to Yi before launch that ultimately ended up with Kuafu who kept it safe in his possession.

During roughly five hundred years before Yi's return, an unnamed individual frequently visited Ruyi to chat, and was responsible for the increasing and locking of Ruyi's sentimental value to 90% as well as the activation of a built-in therapist module. While Ruyi refused to disclose the identity of this individual to Yi, as the individual had requested that Ruyi kept their identity private, the only person on New Kunlun who would have the means and motive to do so was Kuafu.

In his final soulscape fragment, Kuafu dreams of following Yi until their people will be saved, and they can return to his home and use rhizomatic column designs to power his hometown in the Ying Kingdom, making the craftsmen village prosper once more.

### Game Events

#### As a Boss

Following the disturbance of the apemen harvest, Kuafu is awakened by the activation of New Kunlun's security system.

Upon Yi's intrusion into the Power Reservoir zone, Kuafu contacts him via transmission and initially mistakes Yi for a lost awakened solarian; Kuafu informs him that it's a restricted area, and that according to law, all trespassers are to be removed. Kuafu warns him to leave as soon as possible and that Dusk Guardians have been summoned to help him. Yi declares his identity and requests that Kuafu disarms the security system, but Kuafu reacts in anger, believing the intruder to be a prankster donning a crude disguise. He warns Yi to leave again and dismissively ends the transmission.

Kuafu contacts Yi again after he activates the light bridge to the Radiant Pagoda. Since Yi refused to leave when Kuafu was acting nicely, he informs Yi that he will now have to use force to deal with him. Yi snarkily questions Kuafu's threat, asking when a craftsman like Kuafu learned martial arts. Kuafu briefly questions if Yi is the genuine article, given his snark, but quickly dispels this notion. Deploying geno-guards, Kuafu informs Yi that the guards are his final warning, and that he'll be on his own to deal with them.

Despite this final warning, Yi manages to reach the Radiant Pagoda, where Kuafu and his chamber awaits. In a last ditch effort to stop Yi, Kuafu activates General: Yingzhao, who engages Yi in a battle. Following General Yingzhao's defeat, Yi is finally able to confront Kuafu face-to-face; Kuafu is shocked to discover that the supposed imposter is truly his presumed deceased friend.

Turning off the surveillance to his chamber, Kuafu begins to cry, expressing his relief and joy that Yi is still alive. Yi responds coldly to this friendliness, threatening Kuafu and demanding to know if he is in league with the Council. Kuafu confesses that in a sense he is, but explains his deep regrets over how he failed Yi, telling him that he doesn't deserve forgiveness and that he's prepared to accept whatever revenge Yi has in mind for him.

Instead of enacting revenge, Yi chooses to forgive Kuafu, recognizing that Kuafu lacked options in the situation and that Yi hasn't fulfilled the promise they made. Yi tells Kuafu that if he truly wishes to atone for his actions, he can help Yi by betraying the Council and helping to retrieve all of the Sol Seals. Kuafu expresses outrage at these plans as well as Yi's audacity to leverage Kuafu's loyalty to recruit him into such dangerous plans, but still chooses to cooperate. Kuafu allows Yi to take his Sol Seal and moves to the Four Seasons Pavilion, telling him to meet him there after, so that he can return something important to him.

#### Chamber

The contents of Kuafu's Vital Sanctum room in the Radiant Pagoda, represents Kuafu's nostalgia and his sentimental nature. Mementos from his hometown, such as his motorcycle and a windmill iconic to Baigong Village, are prominent features in his room. Photos of old friends are plastered across the walls of the room, such as a photo of friends from his hometown and a picture of him and Yi. Additionally, his room reflects his nature as a tinkerer: the room is filled with work tools, machine parts, and an intricate assistive mechanical arm that Kuafu uses for his work.

#### As Yi's Support

After settling into the Four Seasons Pavilion, Kuafu returns the Azure Bow and the Cloud Piercer Arrow to Yi, informing him that Heng entrusted it to someone else, but that it somehow ended up in his possession. Subsequently, Kuafu provides him assistance in the form of engineering and equipment upgrading. The workbench at the Four Seasons Pavilion allows Kuafu to cut, weld and 3D print during his work. Kuafu also assists Yi by sharing his knowledge of the Sols and various other developments on New Kunlun that Yi is unaware of.

One of Kuafu's most prized possessions is the sophisticated Multi-tool Kit, which he personally endorses. The kit bears the mark of Ying and is created with impeccable craftsmanship; this has made it coveted by even the most accomplished artisans, who consider it a dream come true to possess one. The Multi-tool Kit ultimately remained in Shuanshun's possession after Yi handed it to him and Kuafu begrudgingly agreed with him having it, instructing on how to properly maintain the tools in shape.

When Kuafu first met Shuanshuan, whom Yi was protecting, he was wary of the boy, mentioning the superiority and inferiority between the solarian and apemen races, so he could not easily get rid of prejudice and considered Shuanshuan like a monkey. However, while the game is going on, Kuafu gradually paid attention to Shuanshuan's versatile talents, takes care him by teaching crafts & engineering skills and making necessary tools for him. He became inspired by Shuanshuan's goodness, and gradually started to feel compassion for Shuanshuan's situation. At the end, Kuafu became able to abandon his prejudice against apemen and looked at Shuanshuan as an intellectual person equal to solarian.

### Point of No Return

After the Point of No Return, Yi decides the course of their fates in a conversation with Kuafu; the choices Yi makes in this conversation, as well as other choices Yi made during his journey across New Kunlun, determine the fates of all solarians and apemen.

#### Home Sweet Home Ending

In the Home Sweet Home Ending, Yi decides with Kuafu that the solarians are a more important issue than the apemen and that he intends to warp back to Penglai after he deals with Eigong, continuing the attempts of developing a cure for Tianhuo at their home planet. Yi immediately departs towards the New Kunlun Control Hub, where he encounters and neutralizes Eigong in an intense battle. Subsequently, Yi charts a warp back to Penglai, taking the apemen back with him and Kuafu.

Kuafu's fate in this ending is left uncertain, as he never makes an appearance again after his conversation with Yi.

Although it is unclear, it is theorized that Kuafu dies in this ending, in assumption that the tianhuo flower Yi bows to at the beginning of Home Sweet Home section is Kuafu's. This is supported by a dialogue with Kuafu about Ji. He once had his fortune read by him. Ji foretold that Kuafu would spend the rest of his life away from his hometown. Additionally, he spent an extended time outside Vital Sanctum during Yi's journey, which could have led to such outcome.

However, the flower could also belong to Heng, as it is near Yi and hers parents' graves. And Kuafu could've just entered his Vital Sanctum to continue hibernating, while Yi works on a cure.

#### Shooting Star Ending

In the Shooting Star Ending, Yi informs Kuafu that he wishes to help the apemen survive. This involves returning all of the apemen to the Pale Blue Planet, where they were originally taken from. Yi states that if Heng was alive, this outcome would have been what she wanted. Kuafu questions Yi's choice, reminding him that by doing so, he would doom the Eternal Cauldron Project and effectively waste the many lives sacrificed for the project, but Yi argues that Kuafu's reasoning only supports why they *should* help the apemen. By ending the project, they would put an end to making sacrifices. Finally, Kuafu questions if Yi believes that apemen are more deserving of redemption compared to solarians; Yi responds that he believes that apemen will have the chance to live better lives. Kuafu laughs at Yi's newfound sympathy for the apemen and accepts Yi's decision to save the apemen; he also suggests that Shuanshuan has influenced Yi. Yi returns the sentiment, telling Kuafu that he's also been influenced by Shuanshuan, compelling him to agree but refuse to explain himself.

Kuafu then asks Yi what will happen after returning the apemen to the Pale Blue Planet. Yi states that the plan will be empty talk if they are unable to stop Eigong. This prompts Kuafu to inform Yi about plans he has for the Rhizomatic Bomb Yi gave him: the creation of a new arrow, which if made, could "tear apart heaven and Earth". According to Kuafu, this arrow could tip the scales in Yi's favour if a battle with Eigong became inevitable. While still working on it, Kuafu informs Yi that the arrow he's making can only be fired once.

To complete their plans Yi and Kuafu split up, with Yi waiting for the completion of the Rhizomatic Arrow, and Kuafu heading down to the Galactic Dock to take Shuanshuan to Shennong and help the apemen aboard the Galactic Dock's ships.

Soon Yi, Kuafu, Shuanshuan, and Shennong all reunite at the Galactic Dock. Kuafu chides Yi, stating that despite his incredible smarts, he somehow managed to overlook the fact that none of the dock's ships can move unless a solarian is present at the helm. Jokingly, Kuafu questions if Yi intended for the apemen to fly themselves to the Pale Blue Planet. Yi responds that Kuafu is the best pilot he knows, leading Kuafu to react in indignation to the revelation that he's been co-opted into Yi's plans yet again. Offended, Kuafu emphasizes that the choice to return the apemen is his own, and not one that Yi made for him. Yi tries to thank Kuafu but Kuafu immediately throws this appreciation back into Yi's face; he refuses to be thanked and states that he just wants Shuanshuan to have a real home, in addition to taking responsibility for his role in the apemen's subjugation.

Kuafu tells Yi to get his affairs in order, and vows that even if it's just the two of them, he wants to go back to Penglai together with Yi; Yi agrees and promises to do so. Soon the time for departure approaches, and Kuafu bids farewell to Yi.

Following Eigong's suicide and the subsequent infection of the Primordial Roots Core by the mutation, Yi dials a transmission to Kuafu and asks how far away they are, and Kuafu informs him that they are about 300,000 [li](wp:Li_(unit) "wikilink") away from New Kunlun. Kuafu asks if everything is okay on New Kunlun, and Yi lays out the grave truth: Eigong has infected the Primordial Roots and Yi must destroy them with the Rhizomatic Arrow. Shocked by Yi's plans, Kuafu verbalizes his analysis of the situation, and Yi confirms: using the Rhizomatic Arrow will destroy the entire island. However, Yi accepts this conclusion, remarking that perhaps his actions will finally allow Kunlun and everything upon it to return to nature.

Yi apologizes to Kuafu, telling Kuafu that he will have to take care of everything else and that he's been a terrible friend, always making messes that Kuafu's had to clean up. A tearful Kuafu tells Yi that his words aren't true, and informs him to not worry about it. Yi then asks Kuafu to put Shuanshuan on call, so that they can share one last conversation.

As Yi sacrifices himself to fire the Rhizomatic Arrow, Kuafu watches from a distance as the arrow detonates, unleashing a massive explosion that rends New Kunlun apart. He comforts a sobbing Shuanshuan, who is grieving the loss of Yi. Soon thereafter, Kuafu lands the ship upon the Pale Blue Planet. After landing, the apemen establish a settlement upon the Pale Blue Planet.

Following the destruction of New Kunlun, Kuafu is left as the lone survivor of his species.

The fortune Ji foretold for Kuafu, that he would spend the rest of his life away from his hometown, does come true in the Shooting Star Ending: with the destruction of New Kunlun, Kuafu is unable to return to Penglai, only having a Yuanbao-class ship that cannot handle interstellar travel, and consequently never returns to his home.

## Gameplay

After Kuafu moves into Four Seasons Pavilion, he returns the Azure Bow and Cloud Piercer arrow to Yi, which he took after his death and kept it safe, adding a limited use high-damage, crowd control ranged weapon to Yi's arsenal.

Kuafu also frequently interacts with Shuanshuan in the Pavilion, helping with his crafts and teaching him.

### Shop

Kuafu also acts as a vendor, providing upgrades to Yi's equipment. This functionality becomes available once Kuafu moves into the Four Seasons Pavilion, and can be accessed by interacting with him at the forge on the left side of the Pavilion's second floor.

In the Endings, there are periods during which Kuafu is absent from the Four Seasons Pavilion. However, the forge still provides access to purchase any remaining upgrades with the necessary Jin and Materials.

## Trivia

### Inspirations

- **Kuafu (夸伏)** is inspired by Kuafu (夸父) from Chinese mythology. He was a giant who attempted to chase after the sun, drank up all rivers and lakes he came across during so, but still ended up dying from dehydration before reaching another water source. The wooden club he was carrying grew into a vast forest of peach trees after his death.
- Kuafu's appearance resembles a British Shorthair cat.
