# Shanhai 9000

**Shanhai 9000** (traditional Chinese: 山海9000; pinyin: *Shānhǎi Jiǔqiān*) is a unique robot of an older Shanhai assistive model made by solarians. It is a prominent character across multiple story formats in the Nine Sols setting, and the Shanhai family includes both the standard New Kunlun 9000 models and the older prototype 1000 model.

In the game, Shanhai 9000 is found broken in the Four Seasons Pavilion. After being repaired for 100 Jin, it can extract resource data from the various Shanhai Map Data Chips across New Kunlun. This allows Yi to locate key resources more efficiently, and it immediately unlocks data for the central zone.

Shanhai 9000 also appears as a key character in the Shan-Hai Archive manga and in the Project Daybreak ARG. It is notable for being unusually mobile and proactive compared with the standard stationary New Kunlun Shanhai units.

## Appearance

Shanhai 9000 appears as a short humanoid robot with a large round fish tank-like head. Its outfit includes a hat, a coat with loose sleeves, and a skirt with knotted ties at the front, using blue, light blue, pink, and lavender color tones. Its design evokes a palace-maid or butler-like Hanfu silhouette. Its chest bears an indelible graffiti mark drawn by Chien. It is an older refurbished model, differing from the newer standard New Kunlun models.

The rear screen is usually black or static-filled, but sometimes reveals hidden activity. The standard New Kunlun Shanhai models share a similar look: short humanoid robot bodies, glass-like spherical heads resembling aquariums, and a light blue, pink, and yellow palette.

## Story

### Shan-Hai Archive

Shanhai 9000 is one of the main characters in the Shan-Hai Archive manga. At the end of the story, it is found broken outside New Kunlun before launch and is later brought inside as a precious machine, where it proves useful for later data extraction and analysis.

### Game Events

Shanhai 9000 is encountered broken in the entrance room of the Four Seasons Pavilion and can be repaired for 100 Jin.

After being repaired and rebooted, Yi realizes that the robot is suspiciously proactive. Shanhai 9000 explains that each zone contains a Shanhai 9000 with a corresponding Map Data Chip; if Yi gives those chips to it, it can analyze and locate resources in that zone. Despite its odd behavior, Yi agrees to cooperate because the information will be highly useful.

## Shanhai Family Variants

### Shanhai 1000 (Grotto of Scriptures)

**Grotto Shanhai 1000** is the prototype Shanhai model found at the Ancient Stone Pillar in the Grotto of Scriptures, under the administration of Sol Ji. It provides the Grotto of Scriptures Chip.

This model is shorter than the newer 9000 units, has an ellipsoid cap on its head, and stands on a trapezoidal base instead of legs. Its body is primarily blue and white, and its spherical screen does not project a fish. Instead, it displays static and loading animation. It was a testing model created by the Fangshi Guild and has a much simpler talking and command-recognition module than the later 9000 models.

Ji once wanted to place a live fish inside its head to represent the sea and mountains of Penglai, but Lear insisted on keeping it practical for mass production. This suggests the prototype may once have had a fish projection that stopped functioning after centuries.

### Central Core Shanhai 9000

**Cortex Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive robot found in the Cortex Center, administrated by Sol Lady Ethereal. It provides the Central Core Chip.

Its spherical screen projects a blue catfish. It has a polite and friendly personality, though its speech is distorted in the Cortex Center because of a bad signal.

### Agricultural Zone Shanhai 9000

**Greenhouse Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive model found in the Greenhouse of the Agricultural Zone, administrated by Sol Goumang. It provides the Agricultural Zone Chip.

Its spherical screen projects a purple pufferfish. It has a tsundere personality.

### Empyrean District Shanhai 9000

**Empyrean Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive robot found in the Empyrean District (Living Area), under the administration of Sols Fuxi and Nuwa. It provides the Empyrean District Chip.

Its spherical screen projects a red goldfish-like fish, reminiscent of a ranchu or lionhead style. It has a laid-back, polite personality and once served as the bartender at a bar in the Living Area.

### Power Reservoir Shanhai 9000

**Reservoir Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive model found in the Power Reservoir (Central), under the administration of Sol Kuafu. It provides the Power Reservoir Chip.

Its spherical screen projects a cyan koi fish. It has an overly enthusiastic and friendly personality.

### Transmutation Zone Shanhai 9000

**Factory Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive robot found in the Factory (Production Area) of the Transmutation Zone, administrated by Sol Jiequan. It provides the Transmutation Zone Chip.

Its spherical screen projects a green and yellow fish reminiscent of a porcupinefish or pufferfish. When Yi is recognized as an intruder, it turns hostile and attacks using a crimson laser similar to that of Eclipse Sniper Corporals. This laser can be reflected using the Unbounded Counter, and destroying it in this way grants the Transmutation Zone Chip without cost. It has a spiteful predictive personality in the Fortune Teller Machine.

### Warehouse Zone Shanhai 9000

**Warehouse Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive model found in the Outer Warehouse of the Warehouse Zone, under the administration of Sol Yanlao. It provides the Warehouse Zone Chip.

Its spherical screen projects an orange clam. It has an overly worried and cowardly personality.

## Trivia

- The name of **Shanhai 9000 (山海9000)** comes from the Classic of Mountains and Seas (山海經), also known as the Shanhai Jing.
- Shanhai 9000 is shown multiple times travelling freely, but the standard New Kunlun Shanhai 9000 models and the prototype Shanhai 1000 are typically seen stationary. The factory Shanhai remains stationary in combat mode.
- The Shanhai robots are generally associated with assistive functions and map/resource data extraction, but the factory variant shows that some of them can be weaponized or turned hostile under certain conditions.
- Wen An Chia (賈文安) is the Taiwanese voice actor for Shanhai robots. He is also known for voicing characters such as Rui from Demon Slayer, Senku Ishigami from Dr. STONE, and Fujimaru Ritsuka from Fate/Grand Order.
