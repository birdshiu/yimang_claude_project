**Cortex Shanhai 9000** is the standard New Kunlun assistive Shanhai robot model found in the Cortex Center administrated by Sol Lady Ethereal. It provides Central Core Chip.

## Description

Similarly to other standard New Kunlun Shanhai, Cortex Shanhai appears as a short humanoid robot with large spherical head made of a glass-like material, reminding an aquarium, and has body in light blue, pink and yellow color scheme.

Its spherical screen projects a blue catfish.

It has polite and friendly personality. Its speech is distorted in a comical manner at the Cortex Center due to a bad signal, but it speaks normally in the Fortune Teller Machine. This implies that the standard New Kunlun Shanhai robots use some collective database for their speech and reports and that the serialized brain supercomputer creates strong enough waves to interfere the wireless network "beams" used by solarians.
