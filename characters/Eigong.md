One of the Ten Sols of the Council and head of the Research Center, Eigong holds the most prestige in the Tiandao Council. She is the Council's de-facto leader and Yi's mentor. **Eigong** (traditional Chinese: 易公; pinyin: *Yì Gōng*) is the head of the Sols and the Tiandao Council. She holds the Sol Seal of Knowledge. She is the main antagonist of the game, the story starting with a cutscene at the end of the battle between Yi and Eigong: Yi, severely wounded, barely holds off of cliff as Eigong steps forward with blade in hand and says something, inaudible to Yi, as his grip loosens and he falls down.

## Appearance

Eigong is a tall gray-skinned solarian with horizontal ears, narrow eyes and a slender stature. She is one of the visibly older characters. On her forehead is a jade metal circular device with a hemisphere garnet implanted in it, connected to a cut of Fusang Roots on the back of her head. Her outfit consists of a dark green robe, loose-fitting beige pants, gold padding and tight dark green cuffs with golden accents. Additionally, she has a small grey "hairpiece" in the roots on her head, which is the hilt of her extending red-bladed sword.

## Story

### Origin Era

Eigong was born in Yih Kingdom as a descendant of a renegade Fangshi, from whom she inherited many lost combat techniques. With the use of ancient texts from the Fangshi Guild era to hone her combat skills further in order to eventually establish a near-mystic mastery over Qi-Blade manipulation. In addition to her combat prowess, Eigong was also exceptionally talented when it came to the sciences.

At some point in her life, Eigong became a member of the Tiandao Council, a organization founded by a group of scientists and former nobles who were opposed to the dominant teachings of Taoism in Penglai. Because the Tiandao Council ran counter to Taoism, it had to operate in secrecy due to efforts to suppress its activity.

In **Origin Era Year 434,** Eigong was tasked with heading a research project called the "immortality project". It is unknown if this project was personally initiated by Eigong herself, or if she was assigned to the project by the Council; it is known, however, that Eigong considered the project to be a "personal dream of [hers]". With the pressure of her peer's expectations and the possible loss of funding on her shoulders, Eigong devoted herself to finding success. She began the project by researching the cells of the Primordial Roots for clues to immortality. To document the progression of her research, Eigong began to record time-stamped research logs.

Ten years into the project, in **Origin Era Year 444,** Eigong and her team made a critical breakthrough; they discovered a connection between solarian cells and the Primordial Roots' regeneration cells. With this breakthrough, they began using gene editing processes in solarian test subjects in an attempt to induce immortality.

This would become an ill-fated endeavor: two years later, in **Origin Era Year 446,** Eigong found that test subjects had developed symptoms of rejection and were dying one after the other. The cause of death in these test subjects was identified as a virus that had been accidentally synthesized during the gene editing processes, which was codenamed "Tianhuo". Eigong and her team determined that the virus was contagious, but were unable to isolate it or find its transmission vectors. They found that when those infected with the virus passed away, fungal flowers would bloom from their corpses, which allowed the virus to propagate.

Within four years, the newly synthesized virus became a planet-wide disaster. In **Origin Era Year 450,** Eigong remarked that she had "never seen a catastrophe like this", stating that the virus would spread throughout the entirety of Penglai without sparing anyone. Despite the catastrophe of death looming over the entirety of Penglai, there was one silver lining: the chaos created by the Tianhuo Virus allowed the secretive Tiandao Council, and by extension Eigong, to emerge into the open and maneuver themselves to the top of Penglai's social order. This secured an unprecendented amount of support and resources for the Council, which the Council intended to leverage in order to bring back all Fangshi technologies as well as discover a cure for the Tianhuo Virus. Now carrying the hopes of all solarians that a cure would be found, Eigong dedicated herself further to her research.

In **Origin Era Year 452,** while speaking in the Xia Kingdom, Eigong met a young Yi for the first time after he was brought before her; he was severely injured in an explosion. This meeting precipitated as the result of the young boy's desires to meet and impress Eigong.

Yi idolized Eigong, who was renown as a researcher specializing in Azure Sand. He considered her to be an impressive scientist who was underacknowledged because academics considered her Azure Sand research to be too radical. In anticipation of meeting Eigong in person, as she was slated to visit his village school to review the school's science fair, Yi hoped to impress her by proving one of her recently proposed theories. This theory involved a novel method for using rhizomatic energy from the Primordial Roots to charge technology. To this end, Yi constructed a machine to create a special device using Eigong's charging theory. While this device successfully created what would later be known as the Mystic Nymph, in the process it became overloaded, exploding and injuring Yi as he protected his sister Heng from the blast.

Initially, Eigong believed that the young boy wouldn't even survive another day. To her surprise, a miracle happened: the Primordial Roots appeared to be helping Yi regenerate his damaged organs and tissue. With the help of Eigong's rhizomatic stabilizer, an artificial ring situated over the heart that stabilized and resonated with the power of the Primordial Roots, Yi managed to make a full recovery. Furthermore, to Eigong's surprise, Yi's body also demonstrated an unprecedented symbiosis between solarian cells and the Primordial Roots' genetic material. The data obtained from Yi's body gave her significant hope that the data could be used to make breakthroughs in her research, causing her to question if she had been "blessed by the heavens".

Within the five years of meeting Yi, the young boy became one of Eigong's students. Goumang was also under Eigong's mentorship during this period of time, although it is unknown when she precisely joined her. As a mentor, Eigong apparently favoured Yi, and taught him all that she knew: she passed down her entire lifetime of martial knowledge to him.

Sadly, although Eigong hoped that Yi's data would lead to breakthroughs in her research, it appears that nothing fruitful came of it; by **Origin Era Year 457,** even after formulating many solutions, Eigong found that none of them went anywhere. One solution involved the isolation of the Tianhuo gene from an embryo; the result of this experiment was the creation of what was implied to be domestic cats. This solution was deemed a failure as the result bore little resemblance to solarians in appearance, intelligence, and also possessed a incredibly short lifespan.

Concerned about the amount of time that she had to develop a cure before all solarians died, Eigong decided to trust Yi and pivot toward a risky solution he proposed: the Eternal Cauldron Project. The Eternal Cauldron Project was devised by Yi in order to buy time for the development of a cure; this project centered around securing resources for the Soulscape system. The Soulscape system was a novel invention that could be used to stall the progression of the Tianhuo virus by placing solarians in hibernation pods and generating virtual realities for them to experience in order to prevent brain death. With no other options, Eigong agreed to temporarily postpone research and allocate all available resources to the development of the Eternal Cauldron Project. In accordance with this project, Eigong was assigned responsibility over the Research Institute, which had already been her area before the creation of the project.

In **Origin Era Year 468, on Winter 12/23,** Eigong discovered a ray of hope on the eve of New Kunlun's launch. This ray of hope was data obtained from Ji's cells, which she believed could be used to develop a serum for Tianhuo but also develop a way to accomplish true immortality.

This hopeful discovery would soon be marred by betrayal and tragedy. Around the time before New Kunlun's launch, Yi discovered that Eigong had been concealing information from him: namely, her role in the creation of Tianhuo, and her subsequent obfuscation of this role. This enraged Yi, as he believed that people deserved to know the truth behind the Tianhuo. Yi would confront Eigong over the matter, accusing her of murdering everyone, his parents, and soon his sister as well. During this confrontation, Yi also discovered that apparently he was the only Sol who was unaware of this truth, as Eigong claimed that the others had asked her to speak on their behalf.

The exact timeline of events following this confrontation is unclear, but it resulted in Yi being pursued by members of the Council. This pursuit would seemingly culminate in Yi's death at the hands of Eigong, and the fall of his body into a chasm close to where Peach Blossom Village would later be established. Although Eigong ultimately chose to kill her student, she didn't set out with the intention to do so; she would later contemplate how things went wrong between her and Yi.

### Foundation Era, pre-Game Events

Following the launch of the interstellar vessel, solarian society entered a new era known as the **Foundation Era.**

In **Foundation Era Year 2,** Eigong created her first prototype for a cure, the Tianhuo Serum, and started a clinical study to evaluate its efficacy. To obtain a group of test subjects to test this prototype on, Eigong took advantage of the events surrounding Yi. Under the pretense that the various autonomous systems of New Kunlun could not be trusted - as they had been developed by Yi, and thus could have been tampered with by him - she established the Dusk Guardians, an organization of solarians that reported directly to her. On the surface, the Dusk Guardians' purpose was to replace these autonomous systems and serve as the custodians and protectors of New Kunlun. In reality, Eigong created this group so that she could surreptitiously test the Tianhuo Serum, under the guise of administering "medicinal shots" to them.

Aside from the Dusk Guardians, another test subject was Fuxi. Shortly after Fuxi's early death to the Tianhuo Virus, Eigong appeared before a grieving Nuwa. In what appeared to be an act of kindness and mercy, she offered to make an exception for Fuxi and allow him to use the prototype cure. Desperate to save her brother through any means necessary, Nuwa consented and began to administer Tianhuo Serum to Fuxi.

At first, the Tianhuo Serum appeared to be promising. In **Foundation Era Year 37,** Eigong observed signs of Tianhuo growth suppression in test subjects. However, she also noticed the appearance of sinister side effects in a small quantity of test subjects; some test subjects developed secondary mutations of Tianhuo, which resulted in a loss of sanity and a disposition for violence.

Eigong would continue to observe her test subjects, especially taking note of the test subjects that developed secondary mutations. By **Foundation Era Year 125,** Eigong had began to refer to these rare test subjects as "mutants", owing to their origins. She noted that the mutants possessed exceptional abilities: namely, some of them were able to infect and devour portions of the Primordial Roots, becoming functionally immortal in the process. Later, in **Foundation Era Year 259,** she also discovered that the mutants exhibited what appeared to be a collective consciousness between themselves. The mutants were capable of sharing information with one another, and functioning as a cohesive group to such an degree that Eigong regarded their energy efficiency and coordination as being extreme.

It was implied that as mutants began to appear around New Kunlun, Eigong attempted to suppress the dissemination of information regarding the mutants. In at least two separate instances, Dusk Guardians attempted to record their accounts of encounters with mutants, but these recordings were altered to conceal information regarding mutants.

In **Foundation Era Year 351,** Eigong eventually concluded the Tianhuo Serum clinical study, after observing no further qualitative changes in test subjects. She concluded that 90% of test subjects failed to recover from the Tianhuo Virus, while the remaining 10% transformed into mutants.

At her wits' end, Eigong plunged into the depths of despair. Over the course of a century and a half following the conclusion of the Tianhuo Serum clinical study, Eigong gradually drove herself into madness. Her many successive failures led her to go back and forth about her ability to ever find a solution, to the point of singularly questioning the point of researching immortality. Desperate for any explanations or answers to the Tianhuo problem, as giving up would mean condemning the solarians that had placed their hopes in her to their deaths, Eigong reached a grave conclusion. Rather than the Tianhuo being a disaster that would bring the solarian race to its end, she came to earnestly believe that the Tianhuo was a "trial and a gift from the universe to all solarians", which would "allow solarians to unlock the next stage in their evolution".

Consequently, she believed that the only way to save the solarians would be to infect all of them with the secondary Tianhuo mutation. This, she determined, would require her to access the Primordial Roots in order to infect Fusang with the secondary Tianhuo mutation; this would subsequently propagate the infection to all solarians aboard New Kunlun. However, this would necessitate gaining all of the Sol Seals, as they were required to access Fusang.

### Game Events

Eigong is conspicuously absent for the majority of Yi's journey across New Kunlun.

After imprisoning Yi in the prison, Jiequan mentions to Yi that they can't seem to reach Eigong for a Council meeting.

After defeating the Fengs, Yi gains access to a sample of Tianhuo Serum. After submitting the serum to Ruyi for analysis, the Abacus determines that the serum is a catalyst for secondary Tianhuo mutation. Yi correctly deduces that Eigong is responsible for the creation of this serum, and resolves to question her about its existence and purpose when he next encounters her.

In the process of collecting all Sol Seals save for Eigong's, Yi gains access to the Research Institute, the area of New Kunlun administrated by her. While traversing the area, he uncovers the truth behind the destruction of the Dusk Guardians: that they were used as test subjects for Eigong's research.

Reaching the deepest depths of the Research Institute, Yi encounters Eigong's Vital Sanctum. Like the eight times he previously claimed Sol Seals, Yi connects himself to the machine and enters Eigong's Soulscape. Unlike previous times, Eigong's Soulscape is absent of any Soulscape memories. Here, Yi encounters Eigong for the first time in over five centuries.

The two have a harsh exchange about their last meeting, clashing their worldviews against one another. Yi declares that Eigong betrayed the values which she instilled within him - namely, that people deserve to know the truth. Meanwhile, Eigong wholeheartedly refutes this: she firmly believes that the ends justify the means, and that in the name of pragmatism, it was necessary to lie to everyone in order to efficiently develop a cure. She goes on to question and insult Yi's motives and intentions for the future, before cutting their exchange short and declaring that continuing their exchange is meaningless.

Yi agrees with her sentiment, and demands that she draw her sword so that they can fight. Amused by Yi's confidence, Eigong reminds him that he is within her Soulscape, and urges him to recall the first key point of the Fangshi Art of War: that all warfare is based in deception. When Yi recites this point, she tells him that he's correct, shortly before informing him that she seized control over his nerve centers the moment he entered her Soulscape.

Shocked at Eigong's underhanded tactics, a weakened Yi can only watch in horror as his former mentor seizes all of the Sol Seals he collected over the course of his journey. Eigong expresses gratitude toward Yi for obtaining all of them for her, as she had just been thinking about how she would secure hold of the permissions required to "usher in a new era". With the Sol Seals in hand, Eigong bids farewell to Yi, telling him to sleep peacefully as the "nightmare is about to end".

While Yi is unconscious, Eigong and her soldiers break into the Four Seasons Pavilion. They destroy most of the pavilion and permanently destroy Ruyi's core in the process of seizing control of Yi's Sol Seal. Ruyi manages to keep Kuafu and Shuanshuan safe from her attacks by sheltering them in a hidden area. In possession of all Sol Seals needed to open up the New Kunlun Control Hub, Eigong proceeds to her final destination in order to decrypt the barriers blocking access to the Primordial Roots.

After Yi regains consciousness, he immediately returns to the Four Seasons Pavilion to make sure that everyone is safe. Here, Yi  decides his fate and the fates of all solarians and apemen in a conversation with Kuafu. Regardless of Yi's choices, the determination of his fate always culminates in an encounter with Eigong.

Depending on what choice Yi makes, the decryption progress present on the the Root Core Monitoring Device in the New Kunlun Control Hub will change, due to his actions after his conversation with Kuafu.

If Yi chooses to prioritize the salvation of solarians, after speaking to Kuafu he immediately proceeds to the New Kunlun Control Hub; subsequently, the decryption progress only reaches 20%.

If Yi chooses to prioritize the salvation of the apemen, he will undertake the task of having the apemen returned to the Pale Blue Planet, as well as the task of waiting for the construction of the Rhizomatic Arrow. The additional tasks that Yi undertakes by prioritizing the apemen results in the decryption progress reaching 80% by time he enters the New Kunlun Control Hub.

When Yi reaches Eigong at the top of the New Kunlun Control Hub, she expresses surprise that he woke up so soon. Yi tells her that it wasn't time for a nap. Eigong questions why her former student continues to stand in her way, and attempts to reason with him, declaring her intent to shepherd solarians towards their fated destiny through "liberating" Fusang and unlocking immortality. Yi rebukes Eigong, telling her that she's gone insane and that he can't understand why she continues to repeat the same mistakes over and over again - pursuing her lofty goals at the cost of all else. Unable to stand by and allow Eigong's plans to come to fruition, Yi vows to not allow Eigong to have her way, preparing for battle. Realizing that Yi will never side with her, Eigong tells him that it's cruel of him to turn against her, as "no mentor can bear to end any of their students’ lives", and coldly warns him to not force her to slay him again.

### Home, Sweet Home ending

After landing a fatal blow on his former mentor, Eigong remarks that Yi has improved a lot; he showed zero hesitation between his movements and demonstrated the full strength of his determination. Wistfully, she confesses that he was supposed to be her greatest achievement. In response, Yi tells her that's why he chose to fight her: because he also believes in that sentiment. Realizing that her former student will inherit her will, Eigong expresses her hopes that he's right, and recognizes herself as an enemy, ostensibly to the progression of a cure. In her last words, she tells Yi that she's leaving the fate of all solarians to him, before succumbing to her wounds.

True to his words, Yi ends up becoming his mentor's greatest achievement by embodying her will; he takes up Eigong's mantle of researching for a Tianhuo cure. Along with the captive apemen, Yi warps New Kunlun back to Penglai, where he intends to stay and complete research for a cure. Yi continues to perpetuate the slaughter of apemen, using their brains to establish stable Soulscape systems for the remaining solarians as he tries to find a cure for the Tianhuo.

### Shooting Star ending

While fighting, Eigong is revealed to be infected with the mutated Tianhuo: multiple times throughout the fight, after dealing a particular heavy blow, Eigong's body sprouts the mutated Tianhuo's signature pink pustules before reabsorbing them back into her body.

After landing a fatal blow on his former mentor, Eigong remarks that Yi has improved a lot; during their fight he moved gracefully and fearlessly. She asks if Yi is scared, given the thought that everything around them might turn into dust because of his choice to stop her. Yi confesses that he is scared of what's to come, but that he has also learned to accept failure. Dryly, Eigong tells him that he didn't inherit that from her.

In an instant, the tables turn: although Yi managed to land a fatal blow on Eigong, he failed to kill her before the decryption of the Primordial Roots core was completed. The decryption of the core results in the deactivation of the binding device that had been regulating the tree's growth and expansion. Yi can only watch in horror as a fatally wounded Eigong warps on top of the Primordial Roots core, thanking him for joining her as they "bring an end to this era" and informing him that not even he can "stop the inexorable wheel of history". In one final triumphant cry of victory, Eigong proclaims ***"Solarians will last for ever and ever... a shining beacon of the Universe!"*** and slits her throat, releasing the mutated Tianhuo from her body and infecting the Primordial Roots. The Primordial Roots' core becomes infested by what resembles wailing solarian heads, and the roots begin to spread and engulf the entirety of New Kunlun, carrying the mutated Tianhuo along with it.

With her final actions, Eigong ensures that no solarian aboard New Kunlun can escape exposure to the mutated Tianhuo. Consequently, Yi is forced to destroy the Primordial Roots, as there is no other way to end the disaster. Knowing that his choice will also end his own life, Yi says his final farewells to his remaining loved ones - Kuafu and Shuanshuan. As Yi ends his farewells, the Primordial Roots' core completes its transformation into a colossal, sun-like entity; it opens its eye and peers down at Yi. Without hesitation, Yi aims and detonates the Rhizomatic Arrow at the mutated core, obliterating it, New Kunlun, and what remains of the solarian race.

## Trivia

### Gameplay

- Eigong's talisman grab is one of the few Crimson attacks in the game that can't be parried using Unbounded Counter, another being Sky Rending Claw's grab attack.
- Eigong's Crimson Judgement Cut Combo, including its follow-ups, is one of the strongest combos in the entire game. The string of attacks without interruptions may be the longest in the game and each one deals average to high damage, at most dealing 528.73 damage. For reference, Yi can get up to 240 HP by completing Shennong's questline.

### Lore

- Originally named *Yigong (易公)*, which was changed to *Eigong* remove players confusion about Yi and Eigong relation.
- Usually students are called "學生 (Student)", but in Nine Sols' worldview, they are called "徒弟/學徒 (Apprentice)". On the contrary, "老師 (Teacher)" is instead called "師傅 (Sifu)".
- Eigong is the only Sol whose soulscape fragments are not seen in the game, being masked when Yi enters her soulscape. When she traps him inside, his fragments are shown instead.

### Inspirations

- Eigong's hat design in the beginning act of the game is most likely inspired by Weimao Chinese veil hat.
- Her appearance resembles a Sphynx cat.
- Eigong's sword is inspired by Lightsabers from Star Wars.
