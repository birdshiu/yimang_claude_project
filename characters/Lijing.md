Lijing is the father of Lear, the Deputy Architect of the Haotian Sphere, and husband of Liwen.

## Appearance

Lijing is a Solarian with cream-colored fur, opaque white eyes, and a light orange snout. He has short, messy straight hair, most visible at the back of his head. His uniform consists of a greyish green blouse and pants beneath a white loincloth and a white tunic bearing the Jie Kingdom crest. His outfit is distinguished by a patterned, layered collar.

Lijing has a prosthetic jaw, consisting of a jade bar under his nose that supports a dark translucent curtain draping to his throat on each side of his head. Below the jade bar is a mechanical jaw marked with the characters “YMMR” in Solarian text, and beneath it, is a thinner dark curtain. A metal bar at the back of his head appears to be part of the jaw’s structure, possibly for attachment or removal of the prosthetic jaw.

## Story

Originally from the Xia Kingdom, Lijing and his wife, Liwen, were victims of the Jie Kingdom’s invasion. They were buried under the rubble of their capital during the attack, resulting in severe injuries, it is implied that this incident caused Lijing to lose his jaw, and Liwen her legs. While trapped, Liwen saw what she later called the most beautiful starry sky of her life, that moment inspired the couple to devote their lives to astronomy.

Following the fall of Xia, Lijing and Liwen were, among many skilled architects, forcibly relocated to the Jie Kingdom. There, they were assigned to lead the development of the Haotian Sphere, initially intended by Liwen as a deep-space research satellite, King Jietong soon revelead his plans to weaponize the structure by using its hollow interior to house Rhizomatic Cannons capable of planetary-range attacks.

When their son, Lear, became increasingly close to the King, Liwen became anxious, both by the militarization of their work and by the King's influence over their child, but Lijing remained quiet and supportive to his family. After Lear's failed plea to Jietong to rescind the weaponization order, the Li family was summoned to the palace.

Sensing that something was amiss, Liwen and Lijing initiated a plan, while Jietong was at the entrance of the Orbital Elevator, they sabotaged the Haotian Sphere, causing the Rhizomatic Cannons to fall and the structure to collapse. Lijing watched the event from a shore, while Liwen placed Lear in a boat while he was asleep, in an attempt to lead him to safety.

However, as Liwen talked to the sleeping Lear, Lijing warned about a squad coming down the hill, he urged the sailor to take Lear to the sea, leaving the couple behind. Lear awoke at sea just in time to witness his parents' execution by Jietong, charged with treason.
