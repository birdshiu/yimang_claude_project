**Shennong** (traditional Chinese: 神農; pinyin: *Shénnóng*) is one of the main apemen characters, and one who is closely inspired by his mythological counterpart. He acts as a vendor who can increase Yi's maximum health.

## Appearance

Shennong is a burly man with two bumps on his forehead. He is bald with long white hair on the sides and back of his head. He wears a primitive robe made from leaves and foliage, a leaf necklet, simple pants and rope straps around his ankles.

He can be often seen sitting while munching on a branch.

## Story

### Game Event

In the beginning of the game, Yi is watching the Peach Blossom Village's busyful preparing the harvest ceremony, can hear Shennong complaining about the ceremony.

During the night of the ceremony, Yi is Hidden behind the Shuanshuan's home, and talks to Ruyi to discuss the infiltration of the New Kunlun . but Shennong watched this, suspects Yi and interrogates him. Since Shennong suspected that the Divines served in the village were "The Demons that monitors and eat people," he noticed the heterogeneous appearance of Yi, who was different from the villagers, including himself, and called him the "white demon", and based on the suspicious elements of the village he saw, he revealed his distrust of the faith served in the village and questioned whether Yi had anything to do with them. Yi denies the existence of God and tries to avoid talking to him, but Shennong mutters that he will not break his insistence on his judgment and will surely find out the truth of the world. The villagers murmured towards him, "he is too imaginative and delusional."

After stopping harvest ceremony by rescuing Shuanshuan, When Yi has infiltrated the Apemen Facility and searches the escape route, he found in the instrument panel monitor, which warns of his invasion, simultaneously warns that Shennong has infiltrated the facility. Later, Shuanshuan who has followed Yi to the Four Seasons Pavillion, announces that Shennong has followed Yi and penetrated New Kunlun.

When Yi met Shennong again while exploring New Kunlun, Shennong was collapsed and having a seizure, asks Yi to feed the poison. when Yi responds to his request and brings him the poison, he eats it and recovers. Shennong has a special condition that he must treat by consuming poison, which his body is immune to. He learned to create spitwine (chewed Millet wine) brews from poison he is given that increases the vitality of people who drink it. Shennong served Yi some spitwine in return for his life by Yi, and notifying him that he would stay at the Four Season Pavilion to investigate New Kunlun independently, he offere that would serve spitwine whenever he received two poisons to Yi.

#### Sidequest: The Calvary's Here

When Yi had been defeated by J and imprisoned, escaped and returned, Shennong is announcing that the Geno-Army sent by Jiequan had invaded and destroyed the Peach Blossom Village and taken all the villagers, and he will go to rescue them despite Shuanshuan's dissuasion. Yi promises to save them for Shuanshuan, Shennong gives Yi the Abandoned Mines Access Token of Guru, his former teacher, and tells him there is a shortcut to the forbidden forest in the Abandoned Mine

Entering the Peach Blossom Village through the forbidden forest, Yi defeats all of the Geno-Army that was destroying the village and rescues the villagers were detained in Galactic Dock, but Shaman tries to rely again on the villagers by inciting them to serve Yi as the 'Divine One'. Shennong insists on the villagers to face reality and find a way to survive together, but citing divine's protection, resents the shaman's attitude to turn a blind eye to the reality and declares that they will no longer be involved in their crisis.

## Gameplay

Shennong moves into Pavilion after Yi gives him a Poison in Yinglong Canal to save him from his illness.
If Yi does not rescue him in Yinglong Canal and the following conditions are met:

- Have obtained Goumang's Sol Seal
- Have seen the jumpscare in the Cortex Center

Shuanshuan instead finds Shennong fallen ill at the entrance to the Pavilion, carries him to the room near the backyard and asks Yi to help him.
Despite the urgency of the situation, saving Shennong can be postponed until the late game and his questline can be continued from that point. Note that it is impossible to get the key to rescue the village or for Unknown Seed to grow from GM Fertilizer until Yi saves him, as he appears in those cutscenes.


## Trivia

- Shennong (神農) is a mythological Chinese ruler known as the first Yan Emperor who has become a deity in Chinese folk religion. Shennong has been thought to have taught the ancient Chinese not only their practices of agriculture, but also the use of herbal medicine. Shennong was credited with various inventions.
- The Shaman is implied to secretly be the lost Guru that Shennong talks about. In the original dialogue, she refers to Shennong like an old mother and is secretly concerned about all his actions. The Shaman and Shuanshuan call Shennong "阿農 (Ah-nong)", an affectionate nickname. Shennong was loved by the Shaman, even though this love was clumsy and awkward, reflecting the attitude of traditional Asian parents.
  - "Ah(阿) + one of the characters from someone's name" is a common way of addressing close friends or relatives in China.
