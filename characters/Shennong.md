A brawny man from Peach Blossom Village. He is a conspiracy theorist who harbors numerous doubts and grievances towards the world he inhabits. Shennong is one of the main apemen characters, and one who is closely inspired by his mythological counterpart. He acts as a vendor who can increase Yi's maximum health.

# Appearance
Shennong is a burly man with two bumps on his forehead. He is bald with long white hair on the sides and back of his head. He wears a primitive robe made from leaves and foliage, a leaf necklet, simple pants and rope straps around his ankles.

He can be often seen sitting while munching on a branch.

# Story
## Game Event
In the beginning of the game, Yi is watching the Peach Blossom Village's busyful preparing the harvest ceremony, can hear Shennong complaining about the ceremony.

During the night of the ceremony, Yi is Hidden behind the Shuanshuan's home, and talks to Ruyi to discuss the infiltration of the New Kunlun . but Shennong watched this, suspects Yi and interrogates him. Since Shennong suspected that the Divines served in the village were "The Demons that monitors and eat people," he noticed the heterogeneous appearance of Yi, who was different from the villagers, including himself, and called him the "white demon", and based on the suspicious elements of the village he saw, he revealed his distrust of the faith served in the village and questioned whether Yi had anything to do with them. Yi denies the existence of God and tries to avoid talking to him, but Shennong mutters that he will not break his insistence on his judgment and will surely find out the truth of the world. The villagers murmured towards him, "he is too imaginative and delusional."

After stopping harvest ceremony by rescuing Shuanshuan, When Yi has infiltrated the Apemen Facility and searches the escape route, he found in the instrument panel monitor, which warns of his invasion, simultaneously warns that Shennong has infiltrated the facility. Later, Shuanshuan who has followed Yi to the Four Seasons Pavillion, announces that Shennong has followed Yi and penetrated New Kunlun.

When Yi met Shennong again while exploring New Kunlun, Shennong was collapsed and having a seizure, asks Yi to feed the poison. when Yi responds to his request and brings him the poison, he eats it and recovers. Shennong has a special condition that he must treat by consuming poison, which his body is immune to. He learned to create spitwine (chewed Millet wine) brews from poison he is given that increases the vitality of people who drink it. Shennong served Yi some spitwine in return for his life by Yi, and notifying him that he would stay at the Four Season Pavilion to investigate New Kunlun independently, he offere that would serve spitwine whenever he received two poisons to Yi.

When Yi had been defeated by Jiequan and imprisoned, escaped and returned, Shennong is announcing that the Geno-Army sent by Jiequan had invaded and destroyed the Peach Blossom Village and taken all the villagers, and he will go to rescue them despite Shuanshuan's dissuasion.
