Yi's dedicated personal assistant and artificial intelligence system. **Abacus - Ruyi** (traditional Chinese: 算盤－如羿; pinyin: *Suànpán-Rúyì*), mainly referred to as **Abacus** (traditional Chinese: 算盤; pinyin: *Suànpán*) or **Ruyi** (traditional Chinese: 如羿; pinyin: *Rúyì*), is Yi's dedicated personal assistant and artificial intelligence system. The term "abacus" refers to a category of powerful computing systems in Solarian civilization.

## Appearance

Physically, Ruyi is housed in a large spherical computing device in the Four Seasons Pavilion, connected to a large number of cables. When active, a display on the front shows Ruyi's holographic avatar, which appears as a blue-tinted solarian figure resembling Yi in size and general body shape. Ruyi is also able to project his avatar anywhere inside the Four Seasons Pavilion.

During the events of the game, Ruyi has a sentimental, deferential personality and a sophisticated manner of speech. Most other characters, as well as the game database entry, refer to him as "Abacus".

## Story

Ruyi's origins are unknown. He was Yi's assistant just before the launch of New Kunlun for the Eternal Cauldron Project, but does not appear in any earlier flashbacks. Around the time of the launch, Yi was mortally injured by Eigong, and his belongings were destroyed by the Tiandao Council. Ruyi was nearly destroyed as well, but Kuafu intervened and convinced the others to merely throttle down his systems.

During the next 500 years, the Fusang slowly healed Yi. During this time, an unknown individual occasionally visited Ruyi to chat, increasing his sentiment setting to 90% and activating his built-in therapist module. Ruyi was ordered not to disclose the identity of this individual, and it is never revealed during the game, but there are few possible candidates other than Kuafu.

When Yi was finally awakened by Shuanshuan and finished his recovery, he re-established contact with Ruyi while waiting in the 95th Livestock Pen for the next harvest.

At the beginning of the game, Yi successfully escapes from the Livestock Area during the harvest ceremony. He makes his way to the Four Seasons Pavilion and restores Ruyi to full power. Ruyi reactivates the systems of the Four Seasons Pavilion and creates a firewall to prevent its detection from the outside, turning it into a safe area. As the game progresses, Ruyi continues to assist Yi in several ways, such as repairing Yi's Jade System, giving advice, analyzing data, and supervising the several characters that gradually accumulate inside the Four Seasons Pavilion. Eventually, Ruyi analyzes the Tianhuo Serum and Ji's Hair, using the resulting data to design the Super Mutant Buster.

Near the end of the game, Eigong finds an exploit in the firewall and breaks into the Four Seasons Pavilion with an armed force. Ruyi unsuccessfully attempts to prevent Eigong from taking Yi's Sol Seal, and successfully protects some residents of the Pavilion, but is severely damaged during the attack. After bidding farewell to Yi and storing his preferences in a backup database, he permanently shuts down due to the damage.

## Trivia

- Despite being referred to as "*Ruyi*" in dialogue box and database entry, he is only ever called "*Abacus*" in dialogue by the characters.
