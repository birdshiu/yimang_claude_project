Artifacts are items associated with Shuanshuan. Most can be given to Shuanshuan and others are gifted by him to Yi after giving him certain artifacts. Most are items of solarian culture that uncover more about their story. Gifting artifacts and joining Shuanshuan in further interactions creates a closer bond between him and Yi, eventually unlocks Gimme Gimme achievement and helps reach requirements for the True Ending of the game.

## Crimson Hibiscus
These flowers that appear around the Peach Blossom Village are used by villagers as a decoration during the ceremony.
Legend has it that harvests will be bountiful if the ceremony is successful. While that hasn't been proven, everyone still gets more food after the ceremony.


## Four Treasures of the Study
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
An ancient writing tool. Calligraphy has transformed into a traditional art, with emphasis being placed on the erudite, scholarly atmosphere created by each purposeful brush stroke

## Multi-tool
Multi-tool Kit	A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
A sophisticated tool set crafted by the great craftsmen of Ying. This is a must-have dream tool for even the most accomplished of craftsmen.

## Ancient sheet
Ancient Sheet Music	A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
This old sheet music was apparently used for sacrificial ceremonies. It was obtained from a mysterious solarian individual.

Given by the wanderer(Ji) in the Daybreak Tower at the Lake Yaochi Ruins.

## Unknown Seed
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
This seed was found in the Greenhouse. Due to genetic modification, it's hard to determine what it'll grow into.

Dropped from the pot in the water pit, accessible after draining the water, above the Root Node in the Greenhouse.

## Ready-to-Eat Rations
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
Just add hot water to this convenient, portable food product to taste many of Penglai's most popular drinks. This one is bubble tea flavored.

In a chest right before Goumang's soulscape at the Agrarian Hall, after defeating her.

## Qiankun Board
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
It comes with a complete set of Qiankun pieces. The game originated in the Chan Kingdom and is known as the oldest strategic board game on Penglai.

## Tiandao Academy Periodical
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
An academic text from the Tiandao Academy titled "Introduction to Telelurgical Applications". This scroll covers the basics of telelurgy and is easy to understand for beginners.

Drops from the breakable bookshelf in the center of the Tiandao Research Center.

## Kunlun Immortal Portrait
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
This ancient watercolor painting is said to depict an Immortal who appears in every age sporting the same appearance.


## Portrait of Yi
A gift from Shuanshuan. This was Shuanshuan's first attempt at watercolor after receiving the Four Treasures of the Study.
The character in the portrait looks elegant and stylish. Shuanshuan took some creative liberties and made Yi appear taller than he actually is.

Gifted by Shuanshuan after giving him Kunlun Immortal Portrait and Four Treasures of the Study.

## GM Fertiliser
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
While it promotes the growth of other genetically modified crops, it may also trigger random mutations. This fertilizer is highly corrosive, so any body part that comes into contact with it must be immediately rinsed with water.

Solving the platform puzzle and dropping it using panel above, in the top right room of the Factory (Great Hall).
In the chest left of the Root Node at the top of the Sky Tower.

## Fusang Amulet
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
A wooden amulet held by ancient miners to pray for safety. According to believers, Fusang cannot be violated, and only branches that have fallen naturally may be used, making them extremely precious.

In a chest at the top left part of Apeman Facility (Depths), accessible by transporting from Factory (Underground).

## Virtual Reality Device
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
The Draconis Anima is a head-mounted virtual reality device from the Shang Kingdom. It stimulates all five senses by sending signals directly to the brain.


## Antique Vinyl Record
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
This exquisitely crafted vintage record appears to be a collector's item. Real collectors would never play their collections.

In the rightmost chest of the Boundless Repository vault.

## Sword of Jie
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
The Jie Clan's ancestral sword. Custom-made defensive swords are an important part of Jie culture. The sword's form and complexity reflects its owner's identity and status.


## The Legend of the Porky Heroes
Chapters 111 to 120 of The Legend of the Porky Heroes.
After receiving the Four Treasures of the Study, Shuanshuan's handwriting has improved significantly. He handled the writing and cover illustration all on his own. The story is full of twists and turns, having changed protagonists twice.

Appears placed on the shelf behind Shuanshuan's desk after trading Chiyou the Portrait of Yi in the Pavilion and completing Jiequan's Soulscape.

## Penglai Recipe Collection
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
An advanced recipe collection for professional chefs. The warning on the cover asks amateurs to have fire-fighting equipment at hand when attempting any of the recipes within.

In a chest at the second floor of a residential tower at the top of the Empyrean District (Living Area).

## Red Guifang Clay
A solarian artifact that can be gifted to Shuanshuan.
This sticky clay was sourced from a tiny inland nation in the northwestern corner of Penglai. It's very suitable for making pottery.

In a chest at the rightmost edge of the Grotto of Scriptures (East).

## Damaged Fusang Amulet
The amulet Shennong returned to Yi.
This crudely-made Fusang amulet, given to Shennong by Shuanshuan, managed to miraculously block multiple attacks from the guards.

Returned by Shennong after helping him defend his people from attack on the Galactic Dock.
