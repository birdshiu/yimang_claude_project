Cutscenes including transmission messages from Heng and cutscenes of Yi's past with her on Penglai that occur while traversing through the New Kunlun. They happen only once per the specific transition or at a place in the location.

## Intro Cutscene
While awakened by Shuanshuan play on a flute, still half-conscious Yi envisions Heng playing her flute and cheerfully looking back at him, with fungal white flowers slowly surrounding the area and two Tianhuo flowers sprouting behind her.

>Besides, if something does go wrong, it'll be a good learning experience.

>
>(After a moment display screen shows a "Warning!")
>
>Young Yi:
>What's going on?
>
>(Yi tries to turn it off without machine responding)
>
>Young Heng:
>Yi?
>
>Young Yi:
>Please... stop!
>Damn it!
>
>(Whole area starts shaking from the energy)
>
>Young Yi:
>Heng!

>(Machine explodes)

After the explosion, cinematic starts, with scene showing bits of the machine, blood and Yi's hand on the ground. It changes to face battered Heng with vacant shocked gaze, staring at Yi's body, exploding into tears and calling out for her brother in anguish. The scene then turns to show Yi's corpse with half of him ripped off from explosion, his ribcage open and heart still beating slowly for a second before stopping completely. Scene transitions into further view, showing ruins of the lab, vessel of the experiment and Fusang Roots, undamaged, but scattered around. Amid Heng's grief a green essence starts to glow dimly from her chest, slowly forming into little concentrated ball of energy before bursting, filling the lab with green dim and encasing herself and her brother's body in a bubble of light green energy. During the formation process of the energy, the Roots start to move towards Heng, and after the dome appears they hug it slowly, white-green glow reflecting on Roots surface. The scene ends with Roots filling the screen, fading into dark, similar to when Yi gets enveloped by Anomalous Root Nodes and enters Limitless Realm throughout the game.
