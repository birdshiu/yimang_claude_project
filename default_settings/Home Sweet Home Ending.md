The **Home Sweet Home Ending**, also referred to as the **Normal Ending**, is one of the two endings of Nine Sols. In this ending, Yi defeats Eigong, gains all the Sol Seals, and reprograms New Kunlun to return to Penglai.

## Story

After the Point of No Return, Eigong possesses all ten Sol Seals, gaining ultimate control over New Kunlun. She begins to use this power to decrypt the Primordial Roots, intending to infect them with the mutated Tianhuo variant, which she believes will bring immortality to all Solarians. Yi escapes from Eigong's Vital Sanctum and finds her at the Root Pinnacle atop the New Kunlun Control Hub. At this point, the decryption progress has only reached 20%, giving Yi enough time to kill Eigong before she can complete her plan.

With Eigong defeated, Yi retrieves the ten Sol Seals and uses them to reprogram New Kunlun, initiating a warp to return to the Solarian home planet. The game cuts forward to after his arrival, as he visits a flower field with a monument matching where his parents were buried. A single new Tianhuo flower is particularly prominent. After contemplating the flower field, he crosses a nearby body of water to reveal a still-functioning computer system powered by the harvested brains of Apemen, and a series of Vital Sanctum towers housing some surviving Solarians as the search for a cure continues. Yi is greeted by three apemen, including two children and one adult. He settles down to play Qiankun with one of the children, while the adult reports that Soulscape operations are continuing to function. It is unclear whether these apemen include the original Shuanshuan or his clones or descendants.

## Obtaining

The Home Sweet Home is automatically chosen if the requirements for the Shooting Star Ending have not been satisfied before the Point of No Return is passed. Alternatively, if they have been satisfied, the Home Sweet Home ending can be obtained by choosing to save the solarians rather than the apemen during dialogue with Kuafu in the Four Seasons Pavilion after the Point of No Return.
