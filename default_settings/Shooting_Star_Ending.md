The Shooting Star Ending, also known as the True Ending, is one of the two Endings of Nine Sols.

## Obtaining

The Shooting Star ending is obtained by meeting a number of conditions before going past the Point of No Return to the final boss, and then choosing the first option during a dialogue with Kuafu.

The activation conditions include:

* Building close relationship with Shuanshuan by giving him Artifacts or interacting and watching cutscenes with him, which must add up to at least 24 events of the all 36 interactions. It will be enough to unlock the Gimme Gimme achievement.

* Completing Shennong's Questline. This requires a number of events to occur first:
    * Yi must free the villagers by entering through the Abandoned Mines, defeating Lieguan, and performing a hack in the Galactic Dock.
    * Fully growing a tree in the Four Seasons Pavilion after giving Shuanshuan the Unknown Seed and two GM Fertilizers, which will unlock Magical Bean achievement.
    * Shennong must be given all 15 Poisons.
        * After 9 poisons, Yi must aid the village by defeating a large yellow dragonsnake, giving Shennong the Yellow Dragonsnake Venom Sac, then returning to Shaman with healing brew.
        * After seeing all cutscenes featuring Shennong and giving him all 15 poisons, he will vanish from his spot at the Four Seasons Pavilion. Shuanshuan prompts Yi to find him at the Galactic Dock.
* Finding Lear's secret tomb in the Grotto of Scriptures (West) and obtaining the Rhizomatic Bomb, and giving it to Kuafu afterwards.
    * This requires finding and completing a total of three challenge rooms in the Grotto of Scriptures (East) and Grotto of Scriptures (West), then defeating Spirit Keeper: Cixing.

## Story
After the Point of No Return, Eigong possesses all ten Sol Seals, gaining ultimate control over New Kunlun. She begins to use this power to decrypt the Primordial Roots, intending to infect them with the mutated Tianhuo variant, which she believes will bring immortality to the solarian race. Yi escapes from Eigong's Vital Sanctum and returns to the Four Seasons Pavilion, finding that it has been raided. Ruyi has sustained heavy damage and unrecoverably shuts down after bidding goodbye.

In a discussion with Kuafu, Yi expresses his wish to free the apemen and return them to the Pale Blue Planet, abandoning the Eternal Cauldron Project as a failure. Shuanshuan wishes to remain with Yi rather than return to the Pale Blue Planet. Yi confesses his role in the Eternal Cauldron Project and reveals that he developed the plan to enslave the apemen in the first place, but Shuanshuan points out that Yi has become a better person and continues to cling to him. He is finally mollified by Yi's promise to return and pick him up from the Pale Blue Planet after his confrontation with Eigong. After Kuafu and Shuanshuan leave to the Galactic Dock, Yi retrieves the finished Rhizomatic Arrow from Kuafu's forge.

At the Galactic Dock, Yi bids farewell to the others and gifts Shuanshuan his Mystic Nymph, promising to return for it later. After the others depart for the Pale Blue Planet, Yi is left alone in the Galactic Dock and receives a final transmission from Heng, sent shortly before her death on Penglai.

Yi then ascends to the New Kunlun Control Hub to confront Eigong. At this point, Eigong's effort to decrypt the Primordial Roots Core is 80% completed. Yi battles Eigong, injuring her sufficiently to reveal that the Tianhuo mutation is already active in her body. He is unable to kill her before the decryption completes, and Eigong infects the decrypted roots with the mutation, destroying her body in the process.

Yi decides that the infected solarian species cannot be allowed to continue. He contacts Kuafu and Shuanshuan to say goodbye, and they watch from their ship as Yi fires the Rhizomatic Arrow, destroying New Kunlun and leaving Kuafu as the last surviving solarian. Afterward, Earth begins to warm as New Kunlun is no longer blocking any sunlight, and the apemen are free to build their own destiny. It is implied that these events led to the rise of modern human civilization.
