Fusang, also known as the Primordial Roots, or simply the Roots, is a large plant-like organism originating on Penglai. It is the oldest known plant on the planet.

# Characteristics
## Appearance
Fusang is a collection of large black tendrils overlaid with a mobile, white webbed pattern. They are often seen growing and moving like vines.

## Physical Properties
The Primordial Roots are described both as metallic while sprouting and as substance similar to wood after being shed, with items supposedly made from fallen branches of the Roots being described as "wooden".

These duality properties are similarly reflected in its generated rhizomatic energy that appears in both spiritual or fog-like state and, at the same time, as electricity.

## Abilities
The primary characteristic of Fusang is its ability to convert sunlight energy into rhizomatic energy through an unknown process. The generated energy then converges into the Core of the Primordial Roots that appears as a giant ball of light energy. Fangshi Guild devised branch Root Nodes that collect energy at those points for later use, before letting it move to the core. Tiandao Council created a Binding device connected to the Fusang's Core that allows to limit the tree's growth, maximizing energy extraction and regulating expansion.

It is also said to produce a form of "food", but details on this are extremely limited.[6]

Fusang is also known to have various supernatural and metaphysical properties, growing both in the real world and in the Limitless Realm, storing fragments of the world in that mysterious space. People who are able to resonate with the Roots, either through innate ability or extensive study, are able to experience and/or use these properties. Through anomalous Root Nodes, Fusang is able to transport those who are attuned with it into the Limitless Realm, as was the case with Lear and multiple times with Yi. By resonating with the Roots with the Fusang Horn, Yi is also able to travel between Root Nodes.

Fusang is also occasionally implied to have some form of consciousness, being able to communicate with Heng and express feelings such as suffocation to those that can hear it. The Primordial Roots may also devour and/or contain souls.

After being forcefully mutated by Tianhuo, Fusang's Core fuses with Solarians and reforms into a colossal organism with numerous tendrils growing on its spherical body.

# History
Throughout Solarian history, Fusang was an important resource and heavily influenced society, culture, and technology. In the early eras of their history, the Primordial Roots provided shelter and nourishment for Solarians, with civilizations growing from where it flourished. Fusang was also a common entity of worship, being exalted as "The Mother" and having ceremonies dedicated to it alongside Sol, "The Father," the name of Penglai's star. Later on, it was discovered that Fusang converts incoming sunlight into rhizomatic energy, which was harvested and stored to power Solarian technology.
