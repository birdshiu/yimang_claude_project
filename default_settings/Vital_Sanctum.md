The Vital Sanctum is a technology developed as part of the Eternal Cauldron Project, allowing solarians to be placed into cryosleep to drastically slow the progression of the Tianhuo sickness. During cryosleep, brain-death is prevented by using the Soulscape virtual dream system to sustain brain activity. Vital Sanctum technology was developed by a research team led by Lady Ethereal, along with the Soulscape system.

During Nine Sols gameplay, Vital Sanctums are mainly accessed by defeating major bosses. Interacting with a character's Vital Sanctum allows entry into their Soulscape without going into cryo-sleep, and is how Yi retrieves the Sol Seals of the other Sols.

Large towers of Vital Sanctums are located in the Empyrean District (Sanctum). They were capable of housing a total of 100,000 solarians aboard New Kunlun as work on a cure for Tianhuo continued.

# Soulscape System
The Soulscape is a virtual dream system created for the Eternal Cauldron Project. It is used to maintain brain activity during cryosleep, preventing brain-death. Soulscapes serve as personalized dreams or virtual realities for each Solarian in hibernation, and are primarily accessed by interacting with the person's Vital Sanctum.

## Story
The Soulscape system was developed alongside Vital Sanctum technology by a research team led by Lady Ethereal. During development, several serious problems arose. First, the dreams could be so vivid and lengthy that some subjects began to develop disillusion syndrome, confusing the real world with the virtual world. Second, the dreams could be much more enjoyable than the real world, resulting in severe psychological distress upon waking. When disconnected from the Soulscape, many test subjects became violent toward themselves or others.
Due to pressure from the Eternal Cauldron Project, Lady Ethereal did not disclose these problems and was unable to fully fix them before they caused the death of her entire research group. These events caused severe trauma and guilt, resulting in the state of Lady Ethereal as she is encountered during gameplay events.

## Gameplay
In Nine Sols gameplay, Yi accesses the Soulscapes of other Sols to acquire their Sol Seals, typically by interacting with their personal Vital Sanctum after defeating them. These Soulscapes appear as landscapes with white or off-white colors under similarly colored skies, accompanied by large floating panels recording significant memories from the owner's past. A final shattered panel, when pieced together, shows the owner's expectations or desires for the future.
At one point during the game, Yi is also unwittingly pulled into Lady Ethereal's anomalous Soulscape by unknown means.
