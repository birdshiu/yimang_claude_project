The Fangshi Guild was a powerful faction in Solarian history during the late Turbulent Era. It was founded and led by Lear with the goal of establishing world peace, and pioneered many advanced technologies.

# Story
## Involvement
Founded by Lear, the Fangshi guild began as a collection of societal outcasts due to their refusal to accept the Jie Kingdom as the singular leading force and seeking to create a new form of free thought and expression whilst also seeking to halt the conquest of the Jie Kingdom as Jeitong was actively trying to force the other eight kingdoms into a unified state under his rule. Led by Lear, the guild went into hiding within the region now known as the Grotto of Scriptures. The guild grew in strength as three major followers of Lear, known as the Three Great Sages Yin Jifu, Guiguzi, and Luyan, were former nobility and scholars who turned to the Guild as a way to try to break way from the tyrranical Jie Kingdom during the Turbulent Era.

The guild ended up entering into a war with the Jie Kingdom which led to the development of several weapon technologies, the most powerful of which being the Rhizomatic Bomb which brought the Jie Kingdom to its knees. However, following the collapse of the Jie Kingdom the three Great Sages began to push technology even further to more chaotic and destructive heights. While the fate of the Sages is not entirely known, Lear's story about the mythical dog having to eliminate his three friends who had become the new demons may indicate that Lear himself had actively killed his former friends as they had begun to abuse the technologies they made. Following the dissappearance of the Sages, Lear made the Inaction Declaration and disbanded the Guild, leaving behind the Grotto with its technologies sealed away.

The guild would remain a legend but fade from activity with descendants of the Guild's Members becoming devout Taoists in many cases though others sought to pursue the ways of advanced technology. The foundation of Taoism by Lear ended up serving as a major reason why many remained on Panglai and refused to go to New Kunlun.

## Technologies
* Rhizomatic Bomb: A destructive explosive akin to a nuclear weapon in its sheer power. Several were used to force the Jie kingdom to its knees.

## Accomplishments
* Developed Several advanced technologies that would lead to Solarian society becoming the modernized state it is by the time of the Origin Era.
* Ended the rule of the Jie Kingdom and the Turbulent Era.
Led to the development of Taoism as a counter philosophy to existing technological persuits.
* Created the circumstances for the Tiandao Council to take power.

## Inaction Declaration
A statement made by Lear following the disappearance of the Three Great Sages. Lear states in the declaration that the advanced technology of the Guild has led to more chaos and destruction similar to that of the Turbulent Era and that the Guild is to be disbanded so that solarians can focus on returning to a more peaceful spiritual state of balance and follow the Great Tao, while also ending the monopoly on advanced technology the guild had, to ensure more equal freedoms and a state of peace amongst the kingdoms. Only certain parts of it were recoverable due to several decades and centuries passing since the declaration was made and the point at which Yi discovers it. As such parts of the data are corrupted.
