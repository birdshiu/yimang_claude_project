# Yi
Yi is the protagonist and player-controlled character of Nine Sols. He was one of the ten Sols of the Tiandao Council, holding the Sol Seal of Innovation

## Appearance
Yi is a short solarian in his early adulthood. Characters often make comments regarding his diminutive height and youthful appearance:

* Shuanshuan asks him if he was transmutated because other solarian looks taller than him, to which he replies that it has nothing to do with gene modification
* Shanhai 9000 in the Empyrean District (Living Area) remarks that Yi does not look like a legal adult;[2]
* He is one of the shortest characters in the game, being approximately as tall as Shuanshuan, Yanlao, Ji in his young form and slightly taller than a few children of the Peach Blossom Village.
His body is fully covered by pale yellow or cream smooth fur, the length of which seems to be medium-hair.[3]

Yi has large, narrow eyes with dark brown iris, black pupils and a small black nose.

His mouth is omitted or covered by fur as a stylistic choice, but the narrative implies that Yi has it:

* No character ever points out Yi missing a mouth, unlike his age or height;
* He's able to drink;
* Able to eat;
* Inhales the Medicine Pipe and exhales the smoke afterwards;
* Coughs up blood when hurt.

Yi almost constantly wears a furrowed expression.

Yi wears a loose vibrant yellow Fangshi robe with grayish brown hems. His Innovation Sol Seal insignia is woven on the shoulders and a Fusang symbol is woven on the back, which are also grayish brown color. The robe's sleeves are long enough to hide his arms entirely and it extends down to his knees. Its loose cuts grant freedom of movement and basic protection. Yi's choice of color for his own robe accentuates his free-spirited nature.

Underneath, Yi is dressed in a skin-tight black body suit. It does cover his hands, they are human-like and have claws. He wears white socks and black taoist shoes with white outsole. His whole body suit, socks and shoes can decontruct and reconstruct, likely with Azure Sand nano-technology, similarly to Jiequan's armor, with the exception of his Fangshi robe.

At the center of his chest, directly over and notched into his heart is a large jade ring called Rhizomatic Stabilizer, that serves as a medical device for his unique heart, that has merged with Primordial Roots in an incident, granting him extraordinary abilities, such as being able to store Qi, increased regeneration and potentially slower aging and ability to be reborn from the Roots after death.

## Story
### Early Life
Yi was born to a Taoist family in the Xia Kingdom alongside his little sister Heng in a village near an ocean. As a child, Yi was very focused on and proficient in science and technology. He was not very social, often choosing to read or study instead of interacting with his peers, But he wasn't lonely because he remained close to his young sister Heng whom adored and followed him. Although Yi ignored Heng's ability to communicate to nature (including Fusang), thinking that she was delusional and into a hobby of making up stories because she had no friends, he hung out with her and gave her the science stories he knew, hoping Heng would realize the merits of science.

However, his parents revered Taoism, did not respect Yi's will because they had a strong negative prejudice against science. Yi was growing to resent his parents' traditional views on the dangers of science, and becoming estranged from his parents, but He wanted to be recognized by his parents by proving the usefulness of science with his ability.

Yi's talent in science led him to take on the project of constructing the Azure Flyer (renamed to Mystic Nymph by Heng) for a local science fair in order to impress the scientists visiting it. At the fair, Eigong, the scientist Yi admired, was going to visit for a lecture, so with the goal of getting Eigong's attention, Yi set out to develop Azure Flyer for a month with Heng as his assistant.

Finally, at dawn on the day of the fair, the Mystic Nymph was nearly complete, only requiring one last charge of rhizomatic energy. Yi was warned by Heng that the Primordial Roots were "suffocating," and that something was terribly wrong, but Yi, distrustful of Heng's more personified understanding of the Roots, did not heed her warning and initiated the charging. However, as she predicted, the procedure went very wrong, with Yi barely managing to tackle Heng out of the way before the charging machine exploded. In the aftermath of the blast, Heng suffered no major injuries, but the left half of Yi's body was entirely destroyed, leaving his heart exposed. For an unknown reason, perhaps due to Heng crying over her brother's corpse, the Roots began to envelop the two, forming some sort of green barrier around them.

After that, Yi was transported to Eigong, who was fascinated by his miraculous survival due to the Roots, and received professional treatment through her. A rhizomatic stabilizer, appearing from the outside as a green ring, was implanted into Yi's chest to keep his heart stable as he somehow recovered from his injuries. Despite the dramatic failure of the apparatus, the charging and creation of the Mystic Nymph was a success, and this impressed Eigong enough to convince her to take Yi in and mentor him.

So, Yi entered the Tiandao Academy as Eigong's student and spent time under her tutelage, honing his talents and learning Fangshi martial arts from her. He also met Kuafu, quickly becoming best friends with him. Five years later, Yi was chosen as a Sol in the Tiandao Council, bearing the Sol Seal of Innovation.

Shuanshuan is one of the main characters in Nine Sols. He is a resident of Peach Blossom Village

## Appearance
Shuanshuan is a small human boy, with olive skin and short, shaggy white hair that covers his eyes and has a cowlick on the top of his head.

## Story
In the beginning of the game, Yi is awoken from his 500-year-long dormant state by Shuanshuan playing the flute. The boy ran to the forbidden cave for the first time after his parents' departure at the ceremony, without ever expecting to see someone to "pop out from the stone". Over the next two years, he and Yi take care of one another as Yi regains his strength. They await the next harvest ceremony.

As the day of the ceremony arrives, Shuanshuan gets anxious. Yi tries to talk him out of participating, but Shuanshuan says that he cannot and that it is a blessing to be chosen by the Sols.

During the night of the ceremony, Yi joins the crowd and watches as the three chosen are harvested, Shuanshuan being the third. As he walks up, he thanks Yi for taking care of him and telling that it's time for him to go. As he is lifted into the air by the machine, Yi jumps up and blows it up using a Talisman, saving Shuanshuan's life. Shuanshuan is left confused by this action, as Yi departs without explaining himself, only saying that he's not who Shuanshuan thinks him to be.

Later, when Yi reaches the Four Seasons Pavilion, Shuanshuan manages to catch up to him, where Yi allows him to take up residence for the time being and appoints Abacus to watch over him while he's gone.

Over the course of the game, Yi can give Shuanshuan items, which serve to deepen his understanding of the Solarian world and teach Shuanshuan about their culture. Creating a closer bond between Yi and Shuanshuan by giving him artifacts and interacting with him in cutscenes is required to complete the Apemen Questline and is one of requirements towards the True Ending.

In the True Ending, Shuanshuan is left to be a pioneer of the scientific knowledge for humanity as they return to earth. In the Normal Ending, it is unknown what happens to him beyond becoming Yi's assistant.

# Goumang
Goumang is a solarian with dark blue fur, featuring long ears that angle down slightly and wings on her arms. She has large, wide eyes, small black nose and wears both eyeliner and eyeshadow, as well as some jewelry, notably having a golden headdress and pierced ears, with her earrings featuring a golden ball above and red tassels below. Her clothing features a red ornament with white edges on her chest on a rose dress that splits in the front below a red waistband. Below the knees, her legs become much thinner and are connected to or encased in silver metal prosthetics with bird claws instead of feet.

## Story
### Early Life
Goumang was born into the Yumin Kingdom, which had very little fertile soil. Due to this, the nation was stricken with poverty and famine. Goumang grew up homeless and was often cold and hungry due to the barren land around her. As she grew, she became heavily invested in agricultural sciences, particularly as genetic modification of crops became more widespread in order to sustain those who lived in the kingdom.

### Life as a Sol
Goumang's immense effort towards making sure that all were fed through the genetic modification of crops garnered her recognition from Eigong. She accepted Eigong's offer to join the Tiandao Academy and became Eigong's first student.

When Yi later entered the Tiandao Academy, he became Eigong's student as well, who she clearly favored, despite him being a newcomer. Feeling deeply jealous, she came to hate Yi, even as he treated her as a senior. To regain Eigong's interest, Goumang is implied to have tried to be the best in her own field by any means. Her efforts paid off, Goumang was later invited to become one of the Sols in the Tiandao Council, and bearing the Sol Seal signifying Equality.

During the Tianhuo pandemic, she seemed to propose her own plan around the same time the Eternal Cauldron Project was presented. The details of her plan are unknown, but she felt devastated by "Eigong choosing Yi over her", being treated as inferior, and she held a grudge againt him ever since.

Then, in one of the council meetings, Yi proposed the Eternal Cauldron project to the rest of the Sols in order to buy time to develop a cure. One component of this project involved Goumang's research on Jiangshi soldiers. In her research, Goumang posited the possibility of biological calculators - immense calculators that could be theoretically created by linking biological brains in series. These biological calculators were necessary for the success of the project because it would otherwise be impossible to achieve the computational power required to generate Soulscapes for all solarians aboard New Kunlun. Goumang confirmed that the creation of biological calculators was possible, but questioned where Yi intended to source the brains for this, as it required brains from beings of higher intelligence.

In the following meeting, Yi explained where he intended to source brains for the Eternal Cauldron project. The Haotian Sphere, a powerful deep-space telescope that had been running for centuries, discovered the existence of primitive, humanoid creatures possessing large and active brains upon the Pale Blue Planet. Goumang expressed her support of using these creatures to create biological calculators, believing that since primitive species had a tendency to wipe themselves out if left on their own, it would be better to cultivate them for the sake of solarians. As part of the project, Goumang was assigned responsibility over the Agricultural Zone of New Kunlun.

While being escorted to New Kunlun in a pagoda as a VIP, Goumang's palanquin almost crashed into a young blind girl and her assistive Shanhai 9000. Taking pity upon the girl, Goumang fed her an optoberry and allowed her to ride on the palanquin to New Kunlun. After the odd pair departed from the palanquin, Goumang ordered one of her servants to send someone to keep an eye on them, deducing that the pair intended to sneak onto the island.

Some time during the Tianhuo pandemic, Eigong apparently informed Goumang, along with the rest of the Sols besides Yi, that she was responsible for the creation of the Tianhuo. Goumang chose to continue cooperating with Eigong, concealing the origin of the Tianhuo from Yi.

Some time during or after the launch of New Kunlun, Goumang genetically modified herself, giving herself the avian trait of bird feet, and is implied to have gained bird wings or wore coat sleeves resembling bird wings and started wearing a coat with a tail mimicking a tail of a bird, granting herself ability to fly. This sort of genetic modification was common in her native home of Yumin Kingdom, as most of them can handle low altitude flight and traverse vertical cities in their kingdom.

Goumang continued her research and aggressive genetic modification of plants on New Kunlun, employing the aid of two young apemen, whom she eventually grew fond of. For reasons unknown, one or both of these apemen servants died at the age of 19. After their deaths, Goumang turned them into Jiangshi soldiers, ensuring that they would always be by her side; she bestowed the name Shi Nü upon the smaller apeman and the name Shi Qiong upon the larger apeman.

### Game Events
After being awoken by Yi's activity on New Kunlun, Goumang sets a trap for Yi in the form of an elevator at the entrance to the Greenhouse. Yi activates the trap when he steps inside the elevator, becoming imprisoned within it. After a brief exchange of insults, Goumang commands her soldiers to kill Yi. The soldiers fail to kill Yi, instead breaking the elevator and causing it to fall down the elevator shaft. Goumang does not interact with Yi further until he reaches her while she's resting within the Agrarian Hall, the room before her Vital Sanctum. A conversation about the past ensues, revealing that Goumang is deeply jealous of Yi for being favoured by their mentor Eigong, and that she blames him for ruining her plans to create a perfect society.

Goumang tells Yi that she's grateful for his disturbance, explaining that "it's not everyday that one gets to swat a particularly pernicious pest like Yi. Using her Control Bell, she commands her two Jiangshi soldiers to attack Yi. Yi manages to defeat the Jiangshi soldiers and Goumang, severing Goumang's legs and destroying her Control Bell. The destruction of the Control Bell releases the Golden Hoops controlling the Jiangshi soldiers, allowing them to move to Goumang's side and call her "mama". Goumang lashes out at Yi, questioning his motives and plans for the future. She tells Yi to kill her, but he refuses. Instead, he cruelly chooses to keep her alive, forcing her to wear a Golden Hoop. The Golden Hoop forces Goumang to maintain an upright position upon the mutilated stumps of her legs - a fate which she is indefinitely left to suffer.

### Chamber
Goumang harbors a pride disproportionate to her impoverished origins, positioning the solarians and her own class at the pinnacle of all species. The interior of Agrarian Hall is adorned with elements of genetic experimentation and lavishly decorated to resemble a royal palace. Infusion tubes on either side mimic dragon pillars, and a majestic dragon chair stands on an elevated platform.

The vibrant interior of Goumang's Vital Sanctum chamber resembles an organic greenhouse, with research results on plants (food) visible everywhere. Each culture dish acts as a miniature ecosystem, simulating different soil, climate, and nutrient conditions to identify the most efficient cultivation methods.

## Trivia
### Inspirations
Goumang's act of shaking the "control bell" to issue commands is inspired by the scene in Journey to the West where Sun Wukong is subjected to the Tightening Spell. By using remote devices to achieve absolute control over her subordinates, it showcases Goumang's obsession with domination. The spell causes Sun Wukong's golden headband to tighten and cause excruciating pain. This golden headband in shape of its most common depiction, being curled upwards at the front, is also inspiration for the golden hoops worn by Shinü, Shiqiong and the wildlife animals at the Agricultural zone.
